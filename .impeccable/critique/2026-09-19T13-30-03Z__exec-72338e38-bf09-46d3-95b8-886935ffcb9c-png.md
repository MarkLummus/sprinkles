#### Overall Impression

The library-led Home is a compelling expression of the product's full model. It makes the three working spaces feel connected without collapsing their different purposes. The essential next move is to make the visual syntax honest: a recipe's colour, a workspace label, a tally, and a standing must each have one job.

#### What's Working

- One editorial row grammar carries a Notebook recipe, a trusted Recipe Book item, and an Idea log entry without turning them into generic cards.
- The page rejects the category default: no food hero, ratings, or visual clutter compete with actual working records.
- The Recipe Sheet preview bridges library and kitchen without importing the paper-world into the whole shell.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Colour conflates workspace with recipe identity.** Olive Oil, Sweet Cream Base, and Olive Oil with rosemary receive rails in colours that also act like Notebook, Recipe Book, and Idea-log navigation colours.
  **Why it matters:** The same recipe must retain its identity as it appears across the Notebook, Recipe Book, History, and a batch.
  **Fix:** Reserve row rails for persistent recipe identity. Show workspace with a textual overline or neutral label. Restrict the app's fixed colour set to navigation, guidance, and tallies.
  **Suggested command:** `$impeccable colorize`.

- **[P1] Sweet Cream Base is not visibly a base.** It currently reads as an ordinary recipe that happens to be ready to make.
  **Why it matters:** A reusable foundation is a core product distinction and a bridge to adaptations.
  **Fix:** Add a concise `Base` designation and, when true, a provenance line such as `Foundation for 2 recipes`. Keep `Ready to make` as standing, not type.
  **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`.

- **[P1] Home will not scale as both priority queue and full library.** Repeating the same records under Make next and Your library is acceptable at three items but becomes a long duplicate list in a larger collection.
  **Why it matters:** It increases scanning work and allows previews to crowd out decisions.
  **Fix:** Make the first section explicitly bounded, for example `Make next · 3`; let `Library · 24` support grouping or filtering by workspace. Preserve the row system, but show less duplicate metadata in the queue.
  **Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout`.

- **[P2] Tallies need labels and a ceiling.** The short rods are memorable but their relationship to version and batch counts is not yet clear.
  **Why it matters:** A readable history cue turns to confetti as records increase.
  **Fix:** Pair tallies with a stable labelled number; cap visible rods and end with `+n` above that threshold.
  **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`.

- **[P2] Ask an expert needs contextual framing.** It is well located beside the active recipe but reads as generic support.
  **Why it matters:** The product must be explicit about evidence, provenance, and the limits of its advice.
  **Fix:** Say `Ask about this version` and explain in the entry view that the consultation uses the selected Version and does not alter it.
  **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`.

#### Persona Red Flags

- **Active experimenter:** The page says Olive Oil Ice Cream awaits tasting, but does not make the line from the prior batch to “Less oil” immediate.
- **Maker building a repertoire:** Sweet Cream Base does not teach its reusable role, so it can read as another finished flavour.
- **Library maintainer:** Repeated thumbnails and duplicate queue/library rows will make an expanded collection harder to scan.

#### Minor Observations

- The desktop sidebar is calm and functional; tablet navigation retains words without losing the structure.
- Phone correctly removes the tiny sheet previews.
- `Version 3 · From batch · Sep 14` is useful, but the batch needs a recognisable identifier or outcome fragment where space allows.
- `0 batches` could become `Not yet made` only where the product needs the encouragement; it should not manufacture a status everywhere.

#### Questions to Consider

- If development continues after a version is selected for the Recipe Book, which cue proves it is the same recipe rather than a duplicate?
- Should Make next show a specific action—taste, print, make, compare—or remain a calm queue?
- Could each Recipe Sheet preview earn its place by showing the selected version or a last as-made mark rather than a generic document silhouette?
