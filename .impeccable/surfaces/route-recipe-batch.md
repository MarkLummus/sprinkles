## 1. Job and audience

Mark, at the desktop, days after churning, with the annotated sheet in hand. The ice cream is in the freezer and the sheet carries ink in three places: written-over grams on the formula page, a struck step and changed values on the method pages, and the batch log on the last page. He is transcribing, not composing. The binder's finding sets the stakes: 14 of 29 churned batches left no result, and silence there means nothing in either direction. The job is to get the record in while it is still true, in about the time it took to write it.

Two later arrivals: reopening the batch weeks on, while developing version 2 (Phase 3 cites it as the change reason), and adding a tasting after the batch was saved, because draw notes are written at the machine and tasting happens a day later, sometimes more than once.

Transcription is by hand in milestone 1 (Mark, 2026-09-06). Nothing is parsed, inferred, or read from a photograph: TRUST-01 forbids model calls without a policy, and the audit's own conclusion was not to build a return path for the prose. What the product does instead is make the return cheap: the sheet only asked for ink where reality differed, and this surface only asks for the same.

Desktop only in Phase 2 (Mark, 2026-09-06). The layer stacks with the page below 1280 as Phase 1 does, but the phone receives no design and no testing here.

## 3. Selected direction

**Visual authority:** `DESIGN.md` as recorded from Phase 1, the Formulation Cookbook, with the three inherited rules binding: the Two-Ink Rule, the No-Verdict Rule, the Bookcloth Rule. Pen blue, defined and unused since Phase 1, is first painted here. No new world, no palette change, no new face.

**Thesis: recording a batch is writing on the page you printed.** The recipe page does not gain a form; its pen layer becomes writable, in the sheet's page order, and the ink lands in the three places it landed on paper. The surface refuses the recipe-app arrangement of a "log a batch" wizard with steps and progress, and the calculator's spreadsheet of every field at once. It refuses, just as firmly, to make the maker type the plan back in.

**Structural thesis, three places for ink.**

- **Ingredient table: an as-made column.** A blue column beside grams, blank by default. The maker types only where reality differed (383, 241, 45, and 0 on the skipped lecithin row). Blank means nothing was written, and stays blank in reading; 0 is a written value and reads as one; the plan beside it stays black. No per-row tick in Phase 2: the tick was the sheet's confirmation ritual and belongs to paper. The column's arrival is when the table's numeric columns right-align and size to content, a total row lands (plan total, and an as-made total that sums the as-made where written and the plan elsewhere, labelled as such), and a row under 0.05% reads "trace" or a further decimal (carried from the Phase 1 critique in `STATE.md`).
- **Method: strike or change, per step.** Each step gets a strike (skipped) and one blue line for what was done differently, in the maker's words ("blend 60 s"; "Fast, Soft, Prechill 15 min. Speed Δ @ 20 min, really thick @ 24, full churn 30"). A struck step reads struck, its prose intact. Nothing is pre-filled from the method: an unwritten step stays unknown, never "done as written" (BATCH-01). A step whose amounts changed only because the table did carries no line; the as-made column already says it.
- **Margin: the batch log leads the margin, as a churn section and its tastings.** Above the advisories and the authored notes, in the printed page's order. The margin's **churn section** leads with the churn date, then the battery's measured values, each with its unit on the label and each blank by default: time to draw temp. (min), out of the machine (°C), churn duration (min), exit consistency (Smooth ribbon / Wet, soupy / Chunky, separated), airiness (estimated; Low, dense / Medium, standard / High, airy), tempering time (min); then At the machine, the maker's words; then an optional ingredient-notes line in the maker's words for facts like the oil bottle's open date; then a next-time note, labelled as intention. Beneath it, **one tasting section per tasting**, each headed by its tasted date (or "date unknown" in ink when none was written): tasting temperature, °C; the six axis rows; "How did it turn out?" — the one eyebrow over the note field, open and typable immediately, no mode to pick; "Any problems? select all that apply" with the four defect chips (coarse & icy, sandy & gritty, gummy & elastic, greasy film) and the declared "Bitter · declared" toggle after a wider gap; the melt block (melt style: Watery, weeping / Creamy puddle / Stable foam; melt test: g lost at 20 min; both optional, both describing the same behaviour, so they sit together at the foot); and the shared next-time note. A measured field left blank reads as the word "unknown" in ink, never a value from the recipe.

**Dimensions (settled with sketch 007, 2026-09-11/12: 5-point goldilocks stops, core and declared split).** Every axis runs the same 5-point scale — too little · right · too much — with the digits 1–5 printed on the stops themselves and short anchor words under the track's ends (Hardness soft–hard, Scoopability crumbly–gummy, Smoothness grainy–smooth, Sweetness less–more, Density/body thin–heavy, Oil character faint–strong). Directionality is deliberate: it is what future improvement suggestions read (Mark's explicit call; the critique's descriptive-anchors argument is recorded and not adopted, and this question stays closed unless Mark reopens it). The four fixed core axes — sweetness, hardness, smoothness, scoopability — sit left of a vertical hairline; the recipe-declared pair — Density/body, Oil character — sits right of it, captioned "Declared for this recipe" in caption face inside the declared group's box. At desktop width the two groups render side by side as one row-major grid, so tab order follows the eye (Hardness, Scoopability, Body, Smoothness, Sweetness, Oil); when the arrangement stacks, the groups re-render as grouped DOM and the split becomes a horizontal rule. Marks survive re-renders, carried by axis id. Half steps are gone: a mark is one whole stop, or nothing. Bitter is not a goldilocks axis — "too little bitterness" carries no information — so it leaves the grid and returns as the declared toggle trailing the defects row. Any axis may stay unmarked. No overall rating (D12).

**Silence becomes a value.** A batch can be saved before it has been tasted, because the sheet is written in two sittings. In the pen, the tasting section is absent until the maker adds it — hidden-until-added is the primary mode (sketch 007, settled 2026-09-12): the record opens churn-only and reads as complete, not as an abandoned form, and "Add tasting" beside the footer save opens the section and focuses the Tasted date. In the read view, a batch with no tasting says so in words, so silence stays a value in both directions. The "as expected, nothing to note" shortcut is removed (Mark, 2026-09-12): axis marks and the defects checklist now carry real signal on their own, so a blank note reads clearly — flagged as the removal of a purpose-built disambiguator between "never tasted" and "tasted, nothing to add"; revisit once the record is in real use. A marked axis is a stored 1–5 value; an unmarked axis is absent, never zero, and reads "Not recorded".

**Saving is decoupled, two scopes (sketch 007, settled 2026-09-12).** The churn section ends with its own ceremony — Cancel | Save batch only — and that save renders only while a tasting section is open; the page foot is the second save: Cancel | Save batch | Add tasting when the tasting section is absent, Cancel | Save batch & tasting when it is present. Labels are state-based — is the section open? — never content-sniffing. With no tasting section there is one save ceremony: ingredient notes → next time → the footer; each pen's ceremony carries Cancel first. Clearing or removing a tasting shows a toast beside the action (the tasting head when the section is visible, the footer row when it is gone) plus Undo; the toast ("Tasting removed." / "Tasting cleared." / "Tasting restored.") self-clears after 5 s while Undo persists, restores the section with its values, and retires only on tasting edits — a churn edit never retires it, and a pending undo retires on a re-render that crosses the arrangement. Feedback lives where the action is; an empty clear in the visible mode says "Nothing recorded to clear." and the section stays; removing an empty tasting in hidden mode collapses it with no undo, nothing to restore.

**Blank stays visibly blank.** No stop, no segmented option, and no field carries a default; the demo values that read as authored data are gone. Clicking a picked stop or option again clears it, and each marked axis offers its own Clear next to its inline state ("(3)" in pen blue once marked, "(Not recorded)" in muted italic when not). Malformed numbers stay in place with inline feedback and focus on the first error; decimal point and comma are both accepted. Placeholder prose is neutral — "e.g. flavor, texture, anything that stood out" — because a fault-biased example prompts a fault.

**The ceremony.** "Record a batch" is a control in the margin, in the binder's button style. It opens the pen layer: the headnote's "churned ___" slot becomes the writable date field and focus lands there, the as-made column appears, each step gains its strike and line, and the churn section appears starting at its measured values. Everything the maker types is pen blue inside a hairline ink field. "Save batch" is explicit. Saving snapshots the version's rows and coefficients (BATCH2-01), returns the page to reading, and leaves the record in place: still blue, because it is the record, but now text with no field around it — the saved state prints the open batch's date in that same headnote slot. Form carries state; colour never changes on save. The version line under the recipe name then names the open batch's date, never the print date and never a count (Phase 1 D-13; not the version's latest churn — an older batch opened by URL reads its own date) (Mark, 2026-09-07). "Add a tasting" is the same ceremony on a smaller field: a dated tasting section opens in the margin, is written, and is saved.

**The ceremony's save pairs, revised 2026-09-12 (sketch 007).** The pen's save pairs live where the battery puts them, superseding the 2026-09-08 imprint arrangement for this pen: the churn date is the churn section's first field, the churn section ends with Cancel | Save batch only (rendered only while a tasting section is open), and the footer carries Cancel | Save batch | Add tasting or Cancel | Save batch & tasting. "Record a batch", "Record another batch", "Amend", and the read state's "Add a tasting" remain the front-matter controls that open the pen. In the real product Cancel discards the whole pen draft (D-24); naming the two Cancels' scopes is a persistence-contract decision carried for the phase plan, alongside the explicit persistence contract for the two save scopes.

**Reopening and amending.** A saved batch is addressable at `/recipe/:id/batch/:batchId`, under the version it was made against, so Phase 3's change reason can cite it and the reading state can be reached by URL; the batch's id is opaque and its churn date is its name. A version's page shows its latest batch by default; when a version has more than one, the margin lists them by churn date and choosing one swaps the layer. "Amend" reopens the pen layer with the record's values in the fields; saving again adds an amended-on date, the latest shown beside the churn date (Mark, 2026-09-06). Adding a tasting is not an amendment; it is a new dated section. The snapshot is never retaken.

**The record reads as the battery (read view, Phase 03.3.1's second half).** A saved batch's churn section reads the measured cells for the battery's fields — exit consistency and airiness as their picked words, the durations and temperatures as figures with units — and its tasting reads the six axis marks as 1–5 goldilocks stops with their anchor words ("Soft (2)"), the defects as their named chips with "· declared" on the declared flaw, and the melt block. The tasting head may carry the summary line ("Soft (2) · grainy · bitter") — it belongs to the batch read view, never to the pen. Every field the maker left blank reads as its absence in words per the Saying-absence rule. The record reads unchanged when the version is superseded.

**Deferred to a later phase (Mark, 2026-09-06):** the batch's as-made value as a second tick in pen blue on each graduated rule, with its own deviation in words. It follows from the snapshot and from the Two-Ink Rule, but no Phase 2 requirement names it. In Phase 2 the formulation note shows the plan's figures only. Also deferred: a blue "actual" beside each target chip in the method, until method amounts reference the ingredient rows (Phase 3); the per-step line carries it in words until then.

**Focal moment.** The spread after saving, looking like the photograph: 383 beside 370.4, 0 beside 1.2 on the struck lecithin row, step 1 struck, "−6 °C" beside out of the machine and "Soft, not greasy" at the machine in the churn section, and below it a tasting headed "date unknown" with three of the six axes marked on their 1–5 stops, all in one blue against the black plan.

**Raises carried from the recipe brief:** state changes weight and outline, never position or hue; every block wears its plain-language name; nothing moves on focus; one heavy element per view.

**Implementation consequence.** One component tree: the pen layer is a state of the recipe page's regions, not a second page or a modal. The batch record is a separate stored object that references the version and embeds the snapshot; it holds one churn section and a list of tastings; the version is never written to while recording. Blank is stored as absent, never as zero or as the plan's value; 0 is stored as 0. The battery extends the batch record: churn-phase fields (churn duration, exit consistency, airiness, tempering time), tasting-phase fields (melt style, melt test), axis marks as 1–5 values, and the defects checklist and the declared flaw as stored records. Whether the old-shape seeded records reset or lift is the planning call per the no-live-data rule — check whether any real stored record exists before planning; while Mark is the only user, reset over migration is the expected answer. The printed batch-log page (Phase 4) draws its field list from this brief, prints the churn section and one dated tasting section, and prints every blue field as blank space.

## 4. Scope and boundaries

**Fidelity:** production-ready. **Breadth:** the pen layer on the recipe page rebuilt to the full battery, the churn section and tasting sections in the margin, the decoupled saves, the batch row's read view of the battery, reopen by URL, add a tasting, amend. **Interactivity:** full flow (start, transcribe, add a tasting, save either scope, reopen, read, amend).

**In:** as-made per row; strike or change per step; the churn date; the churn battery — time to draw temp. (min), out of the machine (°C), churn duration (min), exit consistency, airiness (estimated), tempering time (min); At the machine; an optional ingredient-notes line; a next-time note on the churn; the tasting battery — tasted date, tasting temperature (°C), the six 1–5 goldilocks axis rows in their core/declared groups, the note under the one "How did it turn out?" eyebrow, "Any problems? select all that apply" with the four defect chips and the declared "Bitter · declared" toggle, the melt block (melt style, melt test), the shared next-time note; decoupled saves with state-based labels (Save batch only / Save batch / Save batch & tasting); hidden-until-added as the primary tasting mode; toast + scoped Undo on tasting removal; per-axis Clear and click-again-clears on stops and segmented options; no defaults anywhere; malformed numbers in place with inline feedback, decimal point and comma both accepted; save; reopen; add a tasting after saving; amend with a dated amendment; the version line's latest churn date; the margin's "no batch yet" state; the total row, right-aligned sized numeric columns, and "trace" that arrive with the as-made column; the batch row's read view of the battery — measured cells for the new fields, goldilocks marks, the defects read, the tasting-head summary line, absence labels in words; 44px touch targets below 760px (UX1-01–UX1-03 bind on every surface this phase touches).

**Out, and named:** phone design and testing (the battery's ≤760px step-down and 44px targets are the app's existing responsive rule, not a phone design); a photograph attached to the batch; any parsing or inference from a photo; comparison across batches (LEARN-01); a structured "what to change" delta block (Phase 3's change reason is its home); as-made figures on the graduated rules and a blue "actual" per target chip (deferred, § 3); diagnosis (D07); the printed page's geometry (Phase 4); typed ingredient fields on the batch (the sheet's "oil bottle open date" and cream butterfat are ingredient facts; the free-text ingredient-notes line is their only home here); a rating control (D12); draft persistence across a reload (UX1-02, Phase 4); structured machine settings (a later recipe-side concern); typed overrun as a percentage (superseded by airiness's three categories); half-step marks (superseded by whole 1–5 stops); the "as expected, nothing to note" shortcut (removed 2026-09-12; revisit once the record is in real use).

**Anti-goals:** no measured value pre-filled from the recipe; no as-made value copied from the plan; no colour on a deviation, a blank, or a mark; no required field anywhere in the record; no wizard, stepper, or modal; no editing of the plan while recording; no defect chip standing in for the maker's own account, and no defect set beyond the four settled chips plus the declared toggle; no content-sniffing save label; no tasting date invented by the app; no default on any stop, segmented option, or field.

## 6. Interaction and layout

- **Hierarchy while recording:** the as-made column; the steps' strikes and lines; the churn section from the churn date; the tasting section if one is open; the footer save. Reading order is the sheet's page order and the tab path follows the pen's DOM (the battery's controls bullet carries it; the 2026-09-08 imprint reading below is superseded). The six graduated rules leave the tab path while recording — they stay clickable and keep their focus treatment, but do not sit between the method and the churn section (Mark, 2026-09-07).
- **Topology:** one page; the pen layer is a state of it; a saved batch has a URL, `/recipe/:id/batch/:batchId`; no route for the recording state itself.
- **Fields:** hairline ink outline, no fill, no radius (no radius token exists); label in the grotesk with the unit in lowercase; typed text in pen blue in the grotesk for anything counted and in the text face for words. Numeric fields accept what the sheet says: time to draw temp., churn duration, and tempering time in minutes; out of the machine and tasting temperature with a leading sign; melt test in whole grams. Anything typed finer is kept as typed, never rounded, and the record shows what was entered; decimal point and comma are both accepted (sketch 007). Blank is stored as absent.
- **Marks:** each axis is a labelled group of five numbered stops (1–5 printed on them) on one track, with short anchor words under the ends and the picked state inline beside the axis name — "(3)" in pen blue once marked, "(Not recorded)" in muted italic when not — operable by keyboard as a named radio group with roving focus (arrows, Home, End); one click sets a stop, clicking it again clears, and per-axis Clear returns focus to the scale. Blank is a real state, never a default.
- **The battery's controls (sketch 007's contract; CSS and HTML live in the sketch findings file):** exit consistency, airiness, and melt style are 3-way segmented controls with shared borders, picked state in pen blue, no default, click-again clears; the defects are toggle chips with pressed state, "select all that apply" in lowercase on the caption's line, and the declared toggle trailing after a wider gap; the note grows without clipping, accepts multilingual and RTL text, and its placeholder is neutral; measurement fields accept blank, signed temperatures, and decimal point or comma, with malformed values staying in place and inline feedback focusing the first error. The tab path follows the pen's DOM — churn section from the churn date through its Cancel | Save batch only, then the tasting's fields, then the footer row; in the desktop 3-column axes arrangement the tab order follows the eye (Hardness, Scoopability, Body, Smoothness, Sweetness, Oil), and stacked arrangements re-render the groups as grouped DOM. Below 760px the stops grow to 40px on a rescaled 216px track and every control — stops, chips, segmented options, text controls, buttons, date and number fields — reaches 44px; desktop stops stay 34px on the 186px track.
- **Feedback:** saving states in words what was recorded and when ("recorded 4 Aug 2026 against 50 g oil · 800 g"); a tasting states its date, or "date unknown"; an amendment states its date the same way; nothing moves and nothing changes colour.
- **Leaving with unsaved ink:** the browser's own leave warning; no invented dialog.
- **Responsiveness:** designed and tested at 1280 and wider; below that the page stacks as Phase 1's grid does, the churn and tasting sections stack with the margin, and the axes follow sketch 007's verified ladder — the side-by-side split holds to 768px, steps to grouped columns below 760px, and collapses to one column at phone widths with no horizontal overflow at 393px.
- **Keyboard and contrast:** every field has a visible label and the shared focus outline; pen blue on ground meets AA (9.75:1); the strike control has a text label, not only a line.
- **Revised 2026-09-08 after the whole-page critique.** The controls leave the margin (Mark's direction; `route-recipe.md` § 3, "The imprint"): "Record a batch", "Record another batch", "Amend", "Add a tasting", the batch list and the churned date live in the imprint, the front-matter band beside the headnote, and so does each pen's ceremony — the churn date with "Save batch" and "Cancel", the tasting date with "Save tasting" and "Cancel". The margin holds the record's content only: the churn section's measured values, draw notes, ingredient notes and next-time note, then each tasting, in the sheet's order, as reading or as in-place fields while the pen is open, with no button among them. § 6's first bullet is read accordingly: the tab order ran imprint (churn date, save, cancel), as-made column, steps, churn section, tastings, then the save and cancel pair repeated at the foot (Mark, 2026-09-08) — superseded 2026-09-12 by the battery's save pairs, whose tab path the battery's controls bullet now carries. The churn-date and tasting-date fields stay the browser's own date input, with the calendar icon hidden; the browser's segment highlight is a named exception to the four-colour system (Mark, 2026-09-08). Escape closes the record or tasting pen only while it holds no ink, returning focus to the control that opened it; with ink, Cancel is the one exit (Mark, 2026-09-08). Saving or cancelling a batch returns focus to "Record a batch", "Record another batch" or "Amend", whichever opened the pen, as the tasting pen already returns to "Add a tasting". "Save batch" and "Cancel", and "Amend" and "Record another batch", are separated by the small gap, never by a space. A measured field is sized to what it holds — a number of three digits and a sign — not to the column; the words fields alone run the column's width. The record's own prose — draw notes, words, next-time notes, ingredient notes — reads in the text face when saved, as this section's "Fields" line already says, and the same face while being typed; only counted values sit in the grotesk. When the batch list first appears (a version's second batch) its links are ink. The "Skipped" checkbox of each step names its step ("Step 3, skipped"); the checkbox itself is drawn as the marks control's stops are, an ink square filled when checked. The "Batch" legend and the margin's region name are headings, so a screen reader's outline reaches the record.

## 7. Constraints and open decisions

**Binding**
- The Two-Ink, No-Verdict, and Bookcloth rules from `DESIGN.md`.
- The batch snapshots the version's rows and coefficients on first save and never retakes them (BATCH2-01, D04).
- No measured value, as-made amount, or step outcome is ever filled from the recipe (BATCH-01).
- A batch is one churn section plus zero or more tastings; a tasting stores whatever the maker wrote, with no required field; a batch may be saved with no tasting, and the tasting section stays absent until added (OBS1-01, read with the two-sitting sheet; Mark, 2026-09-06; hidden-until-added primary mode, sketch 007, 2026-09-12). The empty-visible-tasting-at-save case and the two save scopes' persistence contract are the phase plan's to settle.

**Open, to decide in this brief — the unreadable value (added 2026-09-09)**

One question with two faces, and they must be answered together: **what happens to an as-made amount the maker types that the app cannot read?**

- The plan pen already has an answer. `blockedSaveMessage` refuses the save, names the row, outlines it and focuses it, so a value the app cannot read is always a value the maker is told about. Its predicate (`parseGramsDraft`, non-negative, up to two decimals, nothing else) is strict precisely because that sentence exists.
- The batch pen has no such rule, by decision — this brief has never asked for one. So the same predicate cannot simply be reused here: the strict pattern rejects `.5` and `45.` as well as `-5` and `4o`, and with nothing to say so, a maker transcribing half a gram from the sheet would get no warning and an as-made total that quietly read the plan's number instead. A rule that silently substitutes the plan for the record inverts the Two-Ink Rule, which exists to keep the two apart.
- Today the batch pen is loose in both directions: `asMadeTotals` (`app/src/domain/batch.js`) keeps any finite number, so a typed `-5` moves the live as-made total, while the save path drops it (260909-oow, `be86eb5`) so nothing impossible is ever stored. Nothing persists; a total is briefly wrong while an unreadable value is on screen.
- A quick fix that pushed the plan pen's predicate down into `asMadeTotals` was planned and withdrawn on 2026-09-09 (`ed03f93`, reverted in `d74d01d`) for exactly this reason.

What this brief has to settle, in the maker's terms rather than the predicate's: does an unreadable as-made amount block the save the way an unreadable plan amount does; or is it kept as typed and marked unread, the way an absent value is already marked; or is negative mass the only thing refused, since it is the only one that cannot have happened? Whichever it is, the answer decides what the as-made total may do with a value it cannot read — silence is not available to it. Unchanged by Phase 03.3.1: the battery rebuild does not touch the as-made column, and this decision stays open on this brief's books.
