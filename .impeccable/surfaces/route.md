## 3. Selected direction

**Colorful app context.** The product's own name taken literally, then disciplined: sprinkles are a mark-making system, never decoration. Chosen over The Pint Lid, The Flavour Board and the category standard (the chosen sketch is kept beside this brief as `route.sketch.html`; the others were throwaway); The Freezer Shelf, The Dipping Cabinet and The Riso Bulletin were rolled and set aside in earlier rounds.

**Mark's steer, binding:** two palettes with two jobs.

- **The app palette** — a small set of sprinkle colours the app itself uses for visual interest and guidance: marks, tallies, the active nav, the primary action, moments of delight. Few, named, fixed.
- **The recipe palette** — a larger set from which each recipe takes its identity colour, chosen once and kept for every version, so the app is never limited to the app palette's first few hues. Identity only; two recipes may not share a colour while both are on the shelf.

Neither palette ever carries a verdict (D06). Colour identifies, guides and celebrates; words state standing.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A clean, bright app where every count is a sprinkle and every recipe owns a colour. It refuses the photo-card recipe grid and the antiseptic white list alike: the white stays, the marks make it alive.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, near-black ink, a fixed palette of about six sprinkle hues for the app's own marks, and a wider recipe palette for identity. A sprinkle is a short rounded rod, slightly tilted; a tally is a row of them. Controls are plain, rounded, and quiet; colour sits in the marks, the identity bar, the active state and the primary action, never in a whole region.

STORY: I see my recipes, each in its colour, how far each has come, and what I said last time. I open one and the paper is exactly what I have.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Brand with its sprinkle rule at top left; nav right, active item underlined in an app colour. Page title, one line of guidance. Recipes as rows: identity bar, name and version line, version tally in the recipe's colour, batch tally in app gold, the last verdict in pen blue. "Bring in a recipe" as the one filled action below the list. Signature moment: saving a version drops one more sprinkle onto its tally; a batch drops a gold one.

FORM: Colorful app context (formerly The Sprinkles Jar), grounded candidate 7 of 7 (literal-name slot), chosen from the safer hand of seed `b9d24c7e`, reroll 1, register safer.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
