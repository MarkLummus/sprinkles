## The rule used

- **Active:** the newest approved drawing of something built or about to be built. A board another sketch overrides in part stays Active and its row says what it is still the authority for (the six "Part leftover" boards among them).
- **Archive:** not chosen; a record of a choice whose answer is drawn on another board; an option board whose decision is answered; an as-built reference; a drawing replaced by a later approved board; a first-session study of something since redrawn and built; and what Mark sent there himself.
- **Concepts:** ideas still to be discussed: the Recipe book, Idea log and Kitchen places and Ask an expert, by Mark's words on the canvas.

Every question the inventory raised was answered by Mark, on the canvas (by rewriting a label or a page name), on Mark's List or in chat; each row's Why says which.
