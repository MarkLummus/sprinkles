Vanilla

Plain and cold.

Before you start

  • Chill the mix overnight before churning.
  • Keep the cream cold until it goes in.

Instructions

  1. 1

    Warm the milk. Whisk the milk powder and sugar into the milk. Heat, stirring, then take it off.

    temp85 °C

    why — The powder dissolves without lumps when the milk is warm.

  2. 2

    Finish cold. Stir in the cream and vanilla. Chill overnight.

  3. 3

    Churn. Churn, then harden in the freezer.
