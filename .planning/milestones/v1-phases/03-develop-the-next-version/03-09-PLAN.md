<objective>
Close G-03-3's symptoms 1 and 2 and the smaller defect found beside them: stop the changed-text device serving as the removed marker, strike only what actually changed, and make a removed step whose rows are all still covered say so instead of saying nothing.

Purpose: the maker removed a step and saw its own sentence printed twice — once live in the fields, once struck beneath — because `showStruckBeneath` is `textChanged || removed`, and by definition a removed step's text has not changed. The same block prints only lead-in and instruction whichever of the four fields moved, so editing a purpose strikes a sentence that did not move; and a first keystroke into an absent purpose or aside registers as a change from nothing to empty. Separately, the maker removed step 1 and saw no row flagged. That is not a fault — `uses.js` implements the brief verbatim and step 8 still uses both of step 1's rows — but correct silence looked exactly like broken machinery. Mark's ruling keeps the rule and asks for the cue.

Output: a comparison that says which field moved, a step that strikes it and nothing else, and a removed step that accounts for its own rows in words.
</objective>

## What this plan is closing

`03-UAT.md` gap **G-03-3**, symptoms S1 and S2 and the diagnosis's own finding (i), under Mark's ruling **D-UAT-3**. S3 — the flagged row's selector jumping to step 1 — is the shared seam with G-03-6 and is closed in **03-10**, not here; do not touch `IngredientTable.jsx`.

<flagged_assumptions>
**"Strike only the field that changed" is read per field, not per step.** The diagnosis names two routes to the same wrong visual: the removed-step reuse, and the fact that the struck-beneath block prints lead-in and instruction regardless of which of the four fields moved. This plan reads the UAT's missing item as requiring both — a lead-in/instruction strike when either of those changed, and purpose and aside each striking beneath their own field when they change. A field that was absent in the record and now carries text has nothing to strike, so it shows no struck line at all.

**Removal is still marked in show-changes, just not by duplication.** `route-recipe-version.md` § 3 says removed steps "reappear whole and struck" there, and this plan keeps that: a removed step's prose renders struck in place, reusing the stroke the reading state already draws on a skipped step, with the `removed` label as its sibling. What goes is the second copy of the same sentence beneath the first. In the pen the fields stay live and editable and the `removed` label carries the fact, because there is no room to strike a field's contents without taking the maker's editing away from them.
</flagged_assumptions>
