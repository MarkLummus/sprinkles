<objective>
Move everything that is about the recipe but is not the recipe into Versions: the version list, the labelled lineage lines, the batch list with the churned date, the record's and tasting's openers, and all four pens' ceremonies. Shrink the margin to the record's content with no control in it, extend the foot band to every pen, and build the keyboard contract — focus returns to the opener, and Escape closes only an untouched pen.

Purpose: this is the expansion out from plan 01's proven slice, and it is what actually closes the critique's first P1 — the printed spread carries in-place ink and never a button. It also closes the Phase 2 design debt the UI review recorded: Save no longer sits at the foot of the page's longest column.

Output: a complete `Versions.jsx`; `BatchMargin.jsx` reduced to the record's content; `VersionStrip.jsx` rendering inside Versions with the `strip` grid row retired; `PenFoot.jsx` covering all four pens; the focus and Escape contract in `RecipePage.jsx`.
</objective>

## Planner decisions recorded for this plan

1. **The `As expected, nothing to note` shortcut moves into the tasting ceremony in Versions.** `route-recipe-batch.md` § 6 (revised 2026-09-08) says the margin holds the record's content with no button among them, and this shortcut is the only true button left in the tasting block once the pair leaves. It is a shortcut for *writing* a tasting, so it belongs with that pen's ceremony. Its label is unchanged. Flag it in the re-critique.
2. **`penSaveDisabled` and `penHint` are derived once on the page.** The tasting pen's Save is gated by `isTastingSaveable` and carries a hint sentence; the plan's pen carries `blockedMessage`. Both the ceremony and the foot need both facts, so `RecipePage` computes one pair of values and passes them to both, exactly as `canSaveOver` was lifted in plan 01. Neither `Versions` nor `PenFoot` recomputes either.
3. **Escape is bound as one `document` keydown listener in `RecipePage`, guarded on `openPen`.** A handler on the article would miss an Escape pressed while focus rests on `body`, which is precisely the state the critique found batch Cancel leaving behind. The listener is registered and torn down inside a `useEffect` keyed on the pen state, and reads the existing `isPenDraftDirty` / `isDraftDirty(mode, draft, amendBaseline)` / `isTastingDraftDirty` checks — it introduces no fourth notion of dirtiness.
