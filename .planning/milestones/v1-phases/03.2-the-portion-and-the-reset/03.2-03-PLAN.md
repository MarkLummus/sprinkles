**The one decision CONTEXT.md left open, and how it is settled.** CONTEXT.md pins the stored shape but not what the pen does with it. Before this phase the pen offered one amount field per row, editing the row's single stored number. That number is now derived and cannot be written to. Three options were weighed:
- one field per portion — amounts edit, the split does not;
- one field showing the derived total, read-only for split rows — removes the maker's ability to change whole milk's amount at all, a regression;
- one field editing the total, re-allocated across portions by the app — invents arithmetic the maker did not author, and would silently move the split.

**One field per portion is taken.** It is the literal reading of "amounts edit, the split does not": every amount is editable, and the number of portions and their step assignment are not. For the ten unsplit rows it is exactly one field, exactly as today, with exactly today's accessible name — so the great majority of the existing pen tests keep their meaning unchanged. Reversibility: costly, not one-way — the draft row shape and the accessible-name convention ripple into Phase 03.3's step-order table, but both are internal and recoverable.
