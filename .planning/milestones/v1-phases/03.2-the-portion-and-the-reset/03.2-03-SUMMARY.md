# Phase 03.2 Plan 03: The Portion And The Reset Summary

**`IngredientTable.jsx` and `RecipePage.jsx` read `row.portions` in all three states — reading, the plan's pen, and show-changes — with one amount field per portion in the pen, a step selector that still chooses only the first portion's step, and every removed step carrying no numeral anywhere; the whole `app/src` suite returns to green at 657 tests.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~55 min
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 10

## Accomplishments

- `IngredientTable.jsx`'s `formatStepReferences`, `GramsCell`, `StepCell`, `DiffStepCell` and `rowDiffAccessibleLabel` all read `row.portions` instead of `row.grams`/`row.step`/`row.splitStep`; a new `formatDiffSteps` helper is the show-changes counterpart of `formatStepReferences`, resolving each side of a comparison through its own map with the first portion always printing a number or the word `unallocated`.
- The pen's grams cell renders one text field per portion, each bound to `draftRow.portions[i].grams`, with a distinct accessible name (`${name}, grams, portion ${n}`) on any row with more than one portion, and unchanged (`${name}, grams`) on a one-portion row. The step selector's `changed` check, value, and `onChangePenRowStep` handler all read/write `portions[0]` alone — the split stays fixed this milestone.
- `RecipePage.jsx`'s pen draft moved to `{ portions: [{ step, grams }], removed }` per row: `handleStartDeveloping` seeds it, `isPenDraftDirty` compares it per portion, `draftVersion` and `buildPenFields` both rebuild a row's portions the same way, and `handleChangePenGrams` gained a `portionIndex` parameter while `handleChangePenRowStep` kept its exact signature (it always writes the first portion alone).
- The show-changes body branch's separately-computed split-step suffix and its append were deleted — every portion after the first now renders inside `DiffStepCell` itself, closing the double-print regression the shape change would otherwise cause (pinned by a new test).
- `Method.test.jsx`'s own `makeRow` factory, `RecipeList.test.jsx`'s mass sum, and `FormulationNote.test.jsx`'s diff fixture all moved to the portions shape; the retired `splitStep` name was scrubbed from every remaining comment and from `db-reset.test.js`'s pre-reset fixture (renamed to `secondStep`, since the reset drops the whole store regardless of an old profile's field names).

## Decisions Made

- **No new CSS custom property.** The grams join marker reuses `.ingredient-table__split-step` verbatim; `app/src/styles/app.css` was not touched.
- **The show-changes step cell's fallback rule is asymmetric with the reading state's, deliberately.** `formatStepReferences` (reading) drops every unresolved portion and only says `unallocated` when none resolve at all. `formatDiffSteps` (show-changes) always gives the FIRST portion a number-or-`unallocated` placeholder and only drops later, unresolved portions — this is the direct generalisation of the pre-portions primary/split behavior (the primary always printed something; the split was appended only when it resolved), and the acceptance test locks it in.
- **`db-reset.test.js`'s pre-reset fixture keeps a renamed second key** (`secondStep`, not `splitStep`) rather than losing the two-key shape entirely — the reset drops the whole store regardless of what an old profile called its fields, so the rename costs nothing the test depends on.
