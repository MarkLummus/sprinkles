The pass therefore drove Chromium through `playwright-core` directly at exact viewports with DPR 1: the same library gsd-browser itself wraps, the same `getComputedStyle`/`getBoundingClientRect` contract D-18 requires, and no dependence on Mark's own Chrome. ## Issues Encountered

**Three measurement artefacts were caught before they became false findings**, each by checking raw values rather than trusting the diff:
- `.text-control` measures 0x0 in sketch 007 at state 1 — present but unrendered, so there was nothing to compare against the app's 90.78x24.
- `stop`/`option` `lines` read 3 in the app against 1 in the sketch, with identical `w`/`h`/`fs`/`tt`: the app's label wraps a hidden `<input>`, so `Range.getClientRects()` counts extra rects. `lines` is meaningful for captions only.
- Reading `--focus-outline-width` at `:root` in the sketch gives 1.5px; the pen lives inside `.app`, which overrides it to 2px. Measuring the real focused stop after a genuine Tab gives 2px solid / offset 2px on both sides.

A fourth was caught in the opposite direction: an initial state-1 capture compared DOM presence rather than visibility, which made the sketch look as though it kept `Add tasting` visible while the tasting section was open. Filtering to visible elements showed both sides identical.

**A reference limitation worth carrying forward.** Sketch 003's frame is pinned at 1440 and does not track the viewport at any width — the inverse of the `setWidth(0)` trap the plan warns about. 003's absolute widths are therefore not comparable at any viewport; only relationships are (and those pass). Sketch 007 needs the Pitfall 9 frame equalisation below 784px for the same reason.

**Forced colours unverified.** The headless driver cannot emulate `forced-colors: active`, so those rows are recorded `unverified` rather than `pass` (RESEARCH A7). This is the one part of the pass a later check in a real Chrome would still add.
