The sketch now leans on exactly this: `clearGroup` focuses the first cell **only when `event.detail === 0`** (line 379), i.e. a keyboard activation, so a mouse Clear leaves the tab position on Clear and the next Tab reaches cell 1 with its ring. The app's `firstStopRef.current.focus()` must take the same guard. Live confirmation belongs to the pass's state-2 focus spot check.

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Putting the unit word in the caption** (`BatchRow.jsx:69`): the sketch never does; it is the root cause of item 2.
- **Nesting a helper inside an uppercase caption** (`BatchRow.jsx:596`): the transform cascades into it.
- **Keeping the Bitter chip's aria-label, helper or wider gap** (`BatchRow.jsx:618-626`): the sketch's group cue "This recipe only" is the accessible name path now (line 276: `aria-labelledby="defects-caption defects-declared-cue"`); a leftover `aria-label="Declared for this recipe: Bitter"` would read a phrase the sketch no longer uses anywhere.
- **Routing the removal toast to the foot** (`RecipePage.jsx:953`): the sketch's `record-status` sits in the end-of-record ceremony (line 303) so feedback lives where the action was; `form-status` (309) is only the save/validation channel.
- **A fourth at-rule type or a nested at-rule**: `css-source.js` parses one level of `@media` only; `@container` and nesting break every style suite (`cross-cutting.test.js:119-147`).
- **Literal px in `app.css`**: `binder.test.js`'s no-bare-px gate; tokens first.
- **`outline: none` anywhere** (`binder.test.js:87-90`).
- **Changing `.batch-row__date`'s lowercase "churned 2 Aug 2026"** — that is sketch 003:238's own lowercase `.date` span, not a caption; D-12's "Churn date" is the *pen* caption at `BatchRow.jsx:427` only.
- **Reading widths from CSS and calling them measured**: the pass measures; this research only reasons (Pitfall 6).
- **Keeping click-again-clears on a stop or a segment option** (tenth round): a joined group is a radio, `selectRadio(group, button)` with no toggle-off (sketch line 362), and Clear is the only way back. The app's deliberate clear-on-re-click handler is now a divergence, not a feature.
- **Drawing a ring on an invalid field**: the `[aria-invalid="true"]` outline rule is gone (line 135 is now only the comment that records why). The ring means focus, and focus only.
- **Replacing the picked fill with an outline under `forced-colors`**: line 144 keeps a *fill* (`forced-color-adjust: none; background: Highlight`) and gives the picked box's ring `CanvasText`. D-05's wording ("the only outline on a picked control is a forced-colors fallback") predates this.
- **Measuring sketch 003's ladder with its "full" width preset**: `setWidth(0)` sets `width: 100%` (003:510) and `relayout()` does `parseInt(frame.style.width) || window.innerWidth` (003:511) — `parseInt('100%')` is `100`, so the frame is always `narrow`. Use a real viewport, or one of the numeric presets.

#### The rubric (sketch 008 @ `3a5beed`) — a planning rule, not a pixel

Sketch 008's header (line 230) and its Settled values (lines 268–282 at `0fa1015`; 279 is the recorded figure, added by `109733d`, the rubric at 279) name which shape a control takes. **The planner applies it whenever a task introduces or moves a control:**

- **A hairline box** (`.btn`) commits, discards, or starts a new record. It is the ceremony, and it stands apart. — Save batch, Cancel, Next version, Record batch, Record another, Save as new version.
- **An underlined word** (`.text-control`) acts on what is already on the page, and stands beside the thing it acts on. — Correct, Clear, Add tasting, Remove tasting, Restore tasting, edit this step.
- **An underlined word that opens a panel beneath it is a disclosure**: the panel is its open state, the word does not change, and `aria-expanded` carries the state. — "3 later versions", "1 later batch".
- **A square and a word** records an independent yes or no. — the checkbox, a defect, Show changes. Never a count: "a square on a count reads as a checkbox" (008 README § Box or word).
- **Joined boxes** choose one from a set, and the picked box fills. Picking is final; Clear in the caption line is the way back.

Mark's own test of the rubric: "All nineteen controls across 003 and 007 fit with no exception" (008:279). DESIGN.md § The binder carries it (`a0126a2`). Two consequences for this phase: the per-control `Clear` on the three segmented controls is a word, not a box (T4); and the two count disclosures must carry `aria-expanded` (S4).

## Common Pitfalls

### Pitfall 1: Changing a picked-state rule without its test pin
**What goes wrong:** `binder.test.js:191-207` enumerates the exact underline selectors (`.axis-mark__stop:has(...)`, `.segmented__option ... ~ span`, `.chip-toggle[aria-pressed='true']`); `:43-52` counts rules reading `--focus-outline-width` (exactly two); `:248-264` and `cross-cutting.test.js:119-121` count `@media` blocks (exactly two, at two named conditions); `cross-cutting.test.js:56-95` enumerates the 759.98 block's selectors. Any of Plans 1–3 goes red on the first `npm --prefix app test`.
**How to avoid:** every CSS task lists its pin updates in the same task; the underline list shrinks to `a`, `a:visited`, `.text-control` (+ hover) only; the outline-count pin grows to three with the focus-on-box rule named as the exception; the `@media` list becomes `(max-width: 1099.98px)`, `(max-width: 759.98px)`, `(max-width: 600px)`, `(forced-colors: active)`. The tenth round adds two more: `cross-cutting.test.js:196-205`'s "**the axis name is the finding's named exception: --type-label at weight 600**" test must be **deleted**, not edited (the axis name joins the weight-500 caption list, sketch 157); and `cross-cutting.test.js:56-95`'s 759.98 selector enumeration grows by `.text-control`, the caption lines and `.axis-mark__clear`.
**Warning signs:** a test named "exactly two" or "the only …".

### Pitfall 2: `:has(input:checked)` fill without `z-index` on the segmented option
**What goes wrong:** the picked option's pen-blue border is hidden under its right neighbour's ink hairline (the `-1px` overlap, `app.css:1696`).
**How to avoid:** keep `position: relative; z-index: 1` on the picked option (sketch 49; the app already has it at `:1726`).

### Pitfall 3: The global `button` box on a defect that no longer has one
**What goes wrong:** the tenth round retired the boxed chip — a `.defect` is a borderless, padding-less button whose only mark is a 13px `::before` square (sketch 87–88). The app's global `button` rule gives every button a `border: var(--rule-ink-field) solid var(--ink)` (`app.css:29`) and `6.5px` padding (`:41`), and `button:hover` (`:44-48`) thickens that border with compensating padding. Left in place, a defect keeps its box, and hovering it thickens the box instead of the square.
**How to avoid:** `.chip-toggle { border: 0; padding: 0; background: none; min-height: var(--stop-h) }` plus `.chip-toggle:hover { border: 0; padding: 0 }` to cancel the global hover, and `.chip-toggle:hover::before { border-width: var(--rule-hover) }` to move the thickening onto the square. Check `binder.test.js:111-130` still passes (it reads the bare `button, select` rules only).
**Warning signs:** a defect that changes width on hover, or a visible rectangle around the word.

### Pitfall 4: `field-sizing: content` + `width: 100%` textareas inside a flex row
**What goes wrong:** a prose field in a `.field-row` shrinks to content. The sketch keeps textareas *outside* `.field-row` (234–235, 258, 298).
**How to avoid:** only the date/numeric fields and the melt category move into field rows; textareas keep `.batch-margin__field`.

### Pitfall 5: Container query vs viewport query for the version row
**What goes wrong:** copying 003:71 `@container` verbatim adds an at-rule type `css-source.js` cannot read and the pin suites reject; and the app's band has no `container-type`.
**How to avoid:** D-15's 760px viewport rule inside the existing 759.98 block.

### Pitfall 6: Reasoning about wraps from CSS
**What goes wrong:** two spacing bugs in 03.3.1 were only visible in the DOM (CONTEXT § Established Patterns; sketch README "Caption-to-content spacing unified"). Item 2's wrap analysis above is *reasoned*, not measured.
**How to avoid:** the pass records `getClientRects().length` for every `.lbl` at every width; a value > 1 is a gap row.

### Pitfall 7: The 393 table
**What goes wrong:** with a batch in view the ingredient table shows three numeric columns + Data = 368px of sized columns (`tokens.css:152-167`) in a 353px content width (393 − 2×20); `table-layout: fixed; width: 100%` cannot shrink a sized column, so the table overflows the region and `document.scrollWidth > innerWidth` — D-15's acceptance fails for a reason sketch 003 never drew (it was tested at 834/1024, `page-shell-front-matter.md` line 24).
**How to avoid:** the planner picks one before execution (Open Question 4): narrower column tokens inside the 600 block, or `overflow-x: auto` on `.ingredient-table-region` (scrolls the table alone, keeps the page at 393), or accept the table as the one named exception in the criterion.

### Pitfall 8: Two "Add tasting" buttons, one "Restore tasting", and the focus refs
**What goes wrong:** `PenFoot.jsx:63-66` focuses the *foot's* `addTastingRef` after Remove tasting — the sketch now focuses the *end-of-record* ceremony's Add tasting after an empty removal (line 542) and **Restore tasting** after a data removal (line 560). If `SaveCeremony` renders the opener for both mounts with one ref callback, the last-mounted one wins; if the data-removal path keeps targeting Add tasting, it targets a button the sketch hides while a restore is pending (line 484).
**How to avoid:** the refs are props of BatchRow's mount only (Pattern 5): `addTastingRef` for the empty path, a `restoreRef` for the data path; `RecipePage.jsx`'s `removeTastingAttempt` (`:630`) splits into the two landings. Render-only tests assert which mount carries which ref; the pass's removal spot check sees the focus.

### Pitfall 9: Sketch frame width vs app pen width below 784px
**What goes wrong:** 007's `.frame { width: min(760px, calc(100% - 24px)) }` (109) plus `--gap-xl`/`--gap-m` padding makes the sketch pen 24px narrower than the app's `.batch-margin--pen` at the same viewport once the pen stops being 640px (below ~784 in the sketch). Absolute widths of wrapped rows then differ by design, not by a gap.
**How to avoid:** in the pass, set the sketch frame's width by script so `.pen`'s measured width equals the app's `.batch-margin--pen` width at each D-16 width (`document.querySelector('.frame').style.width = (penWidth + 2*framePadding) + 'px'`), then compare; the sketch's `@media` blocks key on the *viewport*, which both tabs share.

### Pitfall 10: Repointing `--focus-outline-width` is an app-wide change
**What goes wrong:** T8 moves `--focus-outline-width` from `--rule-baseline` (1.5px) to a new `--rule-hover` (2px) so the ring matches sketch line 153. That token has two consumers (`app.css:21-23` the global ring, `:483-484` the landing-focus rule) and `--rule-baseline` itself has many more (table rules, the graduated rule's baseline, the `.front` border, `.text-control:hover`'s underline). Editing `--rule-baseline` instead of adding `--rule-hover` would thicken every table rule in the app.
**How to avoid:** add `--rule-hover: 2px` as its own token, repoint `--focus-outline-width` at it, and repoint only the three hover rules that the sketch draws at 2px (`button:hover` `app.css:44-48`, the stop, the segment option). `--rule-baseline` keeps its 1.5px and its other consumers, including `.text-control:hover`'s underline thickness (sketch line 30 is still 1.5px — an underline does render at 1.5px).
**Warning signs:** `binder.test.js`'s token pins reporting a changed resolved value for a rule you did not touch; the ingredient table's row rules growing.

### Pitfall 11: Removing click-again-clears without giving Clear somewhere to live
**What goes wrong:** T3 removes the app's clear-on-re-click handler, but the three segmented controls have **no Clear control at all** today (T4). Ship T3 alone and a maker who picks "Wet, soupy" by mistake can never get back to blank — a direct regression against "blank stays visibly blank" (D-04).
**How to avoid:** T3 and T4 land in the same task, for all four control families (six axes plus three segmented controls). The acceptance criterion pairs them: picking twice leaves the pick, and a Clear is present and reachable.

### Pitfall 12: The caption line's 44px reserve read as a spacing bug
**What goes wrong:** below 760px every axis head and every segmented caption line reserves 44px (sketch 180) whether or not Clear is showing. Against the app's current 24-ish head that reads as a large new gap above every axis, and the pass will see the pen grow by roughly 9 × 20px at 393.
**How to avoid:** record it as the expected value with its sketch line, and measure the head *before and after* a pick — the point of the reserve is that the number does not change. A head that measures 24 before and 44 after is the bug; 44 before and 44 after is conformance.
