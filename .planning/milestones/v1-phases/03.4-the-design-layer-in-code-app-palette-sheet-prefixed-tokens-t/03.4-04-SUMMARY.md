# Phase 03.4 Plan 4: Home rebuilt as "Active work first" — recency, the hand, and the App marks row grammar Summary

**A framework-free `lastEvent.js` derives recency and standing from a recipe's own versions and batches, and Home now leads with the most recently touched recipe's own words in the hand, followed by rows that state where each recipe lives, how many batches it has, and what to do next.**

## Accomplishments

- `app/src/domain/lastEvent.js` (new): `lastEventAt` (the greatest ISO string among a recipe's own version `createdAt` and batch `recordedAt`/`changed` fields, never `churn.churnDate`/`tasting.tastedDate`), `standingFor` (the three Notebook standings, `not-yet-churned`/`awaiting-tasting`/`tasted`), and `activeWork` (one entry per recipe — id, name, latest version, its own versions and batches newest-first, its `lastEventAt` and its standing — ordered by recency, a null last event ordering last, ties broken on the latest version's id), all node-tested with 14 tests covering every `<behavior>` bullet.
- `RecipeList.jsx` rebuilt as three presentational layers: `HomeLead` (the most recently touched recipe as a larger block — place, name, version/mass line, and the maker's Next time in `.app-hand`, the one renderer of the hand role class plan 02 shipped), `HomeBody` (the empty shelf when the store holds no recipes — one sentence, then Recipe book and Idea log each in their own text companion — or the lead block plus the rows otherwise, with the one-recipe case never special-cased away), and `RecipeRows` rewritten over `activeWork`'s entries with the App marks grammar: a destination rail, the place name, the recipe name as the link, a hollow-sprinkle batch tally with its count in words, and one filled action plus an optional secondary chosen by the recipe's own standing.
- `home.css`: the lead block's rules, the row's CSS Grid (rail spanning three lines, place above name, actions at the right edge), the filled action per DESIGN.md ("The filled action" — the app blue's text companion as the fill, the app background as the label colour), the empty shelf's two-link sentence, and the file's one media block extended so the row stacks and its actions wrap full width below 759.98px. Every Jar-era rule left without markup (`.home__bar`, `.home__tally-mark--version`/`--batch`, `.home__words`, `.home__guidance`, `.home__tally-label`) is gone.
- `tokens.css`: two new tokens — `--app-rule-tally` (the hollow sprinkle's 2px edge) and `--app-size-lead-name` (the lead block's own larger recipe-name size).
- Full suite: 1101 passed (1078 baseline + 23 new), 0 failed. Build succeeds.
