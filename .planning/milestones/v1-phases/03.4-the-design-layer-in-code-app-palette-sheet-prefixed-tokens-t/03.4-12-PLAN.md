<decisions_recorded>
1. **Every hide is scoped under `.home__row`, never bare.** Board 171 draws its LEAD block with both actions (line 32) and its ROW with one (line 45); the rod and the tally likewise exist only in the row's markup. A bare `.home__action--secondary { display: none }` inside the phone block would strip the lead's second action too, against the board. The three new rules are `.home__row .home__rail`, `.home__row .home__meta` and `.home__row .home__action--secondary`.
2. **The hidden elements leave the grid template with them.** A grid item with `display: none` generates no box, so the `rail` and `tally` areas must go from the phone template or they would reserve a column and a row for nothing — a named column plus its 20px gap is a visible indent the board does not draw. The phone row's template becomes two columns: place over name beside the standing word, then the actions spanning both.
3. **The count in words goes with the marks, and UX1-03 still holds.** UX1-03 forbids a count carried by marks alone; hiding both leaves no count at phone width rather than a count in colour, and the recipe's own history still states it. Mark ruled the tally out to match the artboard; recorded here because `display: none` also takes the words out of the accessibility tree at that width.
4. **The single filled action keeps the phone block's stretch.** With the secondary hidden, the existing `.home__actions { width: 100% }` and `.home__action { flex: 1 1 0 }` make the one remaining button full width, where board 171 draws it at its natural width. Those two rules are the ladder's own touch decision from 03.4-03 and Mark did not rule on them, so they stay — and the difference is written into the conformance record as a new open row rather than silently corrected.
5. **The lead's meta line loses the mass and gains nothing.** The gap's own list left a second question open — whether to add the board's standing word and last-batch date to that line. Mark ruled only that the grams are not needed. So the line becomes the version identity alone, and the fuller board meta is recorded as open for Mark and Impeccable.
6. **The seeded version's own label still contains grams, and that is data.** Board 170's meta reads "Version 3 · Less oil · …"; ours will read "Version 1 · 50 g oil · 800 g" because the seeded version's label says so. The test's negative assertion therefore targets the computed one-decimal figure the code appends, not the letter g.
</decisions_recorded>
