<decisions_recorded>
1. **One new stylesheet for the App side of the route.** notebook.css holds every App-context rule of the recipe route (band, frame, later the rail and the log's App restyle). It is imported in main.jsx after home.css, is added to tokens.test.js's CSS_FILE_NAMES, and gets its own discipline test in home.test.js's shape: no hex, no px literal, every selector starting `.notebook`, and exactly three named @media steps. The Sheet's rules stay in app.css.
2. **The ladder's breakpoints.** The boards give rungs, not thresholds. 1024 and 393 map onto the app's existing steps: one column at (max-width: 1099.98px), phone at (max-width: 759.98px), where the shell's tab row already switches. Between 1366 and 1600 this plan sets the desktop rung at 1500px, so iPad landscape rules apply at (max-width: 1499.98px). Media-query conditions cannot read custom properties, so the literals live in notebook.css's own comment prelude, as 600 does in app.css. The choice is recorded for Mark at plan 08's conformance check.
3. **Rename's fields reuse the ceremony's App controls.** The boards draw Rename closed only. The open state takes 1600-pen.html's App form controls (field, caption label, outlined Cancel, filled Save), so nothing new is invented. D-12 fixes the order: Cancel, then Save. Flagged for plan 08's record.
4. **'Save over this version'** keeps its existing behaviour (only on a version with no batch). The pen board draws a churned version, so it shows only 'Save as a new version'. The extra button takes the outlined style and sits after the filled one. Flagged for plan 08's record.
5. **From batch.** 1600-pen.html draws one citable batch as a checkbox labelled '<date> · <the batch's words>'. The ceremony renders exactly that when one batch is citable. With more than one, it keeps the existing select, which is already approved pen grammar, so a group of checkboxes never pretends to be single-choice. With none it says 'no batch'. The batch's words are the maker's own (atTheMachine) and are never re-cased. Flagged for plan 08's record.
6. **The root version's 'From' line** (1600-batch.html: 'the 1 kg formula, off the app') has no field in the store, and D-08 forbids growing the schema for the seed. A root version therefore shows no From line. Listed for Mark in plan 08's record.
7. **The recipe route's canvas.** app.css's `body:has(...)` keeps only `.not-found`. Layout C puts App context above and below the Sheet, and every board sets body to white; the Sheet keeps its own paper through `.recipe-page`'s own background.
</decisions_recorded>
