<decisions_recorded>
1. **A Sheet-side hand class.** The boards draw the Sheet's hand at 20px with a line-height of 1, where the App's `.app-hand` is 22px at 1.25. So `.sheet-hand` is its own rule: --size-hand-min (20px, 'the hand … 20px minimum', settled decision 2) and a new --sheet-leading-hand of 1. It keeps the same forced-colors and print fallbacks as .app-hand.
2. **The open step's fields.** 1600-pen.html draws the open step's text as one pen-blue block in a hairline outline, not the app's separate lead-in, instruction and target fields. The pen's field grammar stands unchanged (route-recipe-version.md § 3; the 03.5 briefs: 'the pen's grammar on the plan … stands unchanged'). The existing fields stay in pen blue inside a hairline ink outline around the open step's text block, and the difference goes on plan 08's record for Mark.
3. **Before you start notes in the pen** keep their existing text fields. 1600-pen.html draws them as pen-blue text with the inherited marker and a remove control, but no decision changes how notes are edited. Flagged for plan 08's record.
4. **Remove moves to the closed step.** The open form no longer carries remove or restore (settled decision 5: 'remove sits on a closed step'). A removed step shows 'edit this step · restore'.
5. **The narrow list lives in app.css's (max-width: 759.98px) block**, the phone rung that also brings the bottom tab row. The 600px block's `overflow-x: auto` on the table region stays until measurement shows the list makes it moot; plan 08 measures zero page overflow at 393.
</decisions_recorded>
