1. **The hand-back is removed, not re-pointed.** notebook.css re-declares the desktop track, stop and label sizes for `.notebook-log` wherever the log is full width. It does this at two classes of specificity, which outranks app.css's own single-class rules: the touch block's stop height, and the width-only block's 216 track and 44 × 44 stop. So under a coarse pointer the record pen's stops stay 32px tall below that cut. The comment above those rules says its intent is to "hand back control" to app.css. Scoping the log-column rules to (min-width: 1366px), where the log is a 350 column, and deleting the re-declarations does exactly that. The ceremony wrap rule stays unconditional, as plan 07 left it. Task 1 measures the stop height before and after.
2. **Why the record pen's cut is a checkpoint.** The two sources disagree, and the sketch draws no record state to settle them (decision 14: phone logging deferred; plan 07: reading state only). The 723 board's stylesheet does carry the axis rules, but only because gen.py unwrapped the whole old block, and no axis renders on that board. The orchestrator should put the question to Mark before execution starts. If his answer is recorded here as the line "Record pen cut: 760" or "Record pen cut: 724", the executor takes it and continues without stopping.
3. **The wide-touch block follows the battery's cut.** Its caption reserve (sketch 009's M4) exists for the battery's wide, row-major arrangement under a coarse pointer, and the 44px caption line is for the stacked one. So its min-width is the complement of the width-only block's max-width, whichever cut Mark picks: "settled against the new ladder".
4. **The width-only block keeps its stop height.** Its 44 × 44 stop is part of the 216 track geometry (5 × 44 − 4), not a touch size, so it stays width-keyed (DESIGN.md's Capability Rule: touch layouts read the width).
5. Record pen cut: 760 (Mark, 2026-09-27; add a todo to measure the record pen)
