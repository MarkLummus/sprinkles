### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Fixed two stale probe selectors from earlier decisions**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (the conformance runner end to end at 984)
- **Issue:** `readVersionColumn`'s board branch located the version column via `caption.parentElement`, correct for the original boards but broken once decision 18 (plan 15) wrapped the caption inside the fold-version button's own span — every board-side `identity`/`dl`/`nextVersion` field read `null`. Separately, `readRail`'s board branch fell back to any `<a>` in the recipe `<header>` when `#fold-history`'s body was absent, which was correct for "no fold at all" but picked up the version column's own draft link as a false-positive "rail node" once decision 19's boards began omitting a closed fold's body from the markup entirely.
- **Fix:** Locate the version column through the fold-version button's own parent when present, falling back to the old shape for a board predating the fold. Treat a `button[aria-controls="fold-history"]` with no matching body as "closed, genuinely no rail to read" rather than a cue to widen the search.
- **Files modified:** `.planning/phases/03.5-separate-the-recipe-from-the-sheet/03.5-conformance-probe.js`
- **Verification:** Every affected width's reading now matches expected board/app values; documented in the record's own "Two probe fixes made during this run" section.
- **Committed in:** `57325e9` (Task 1 commit)
