## Accomplishments

- **Task 1 (tracer):** `BELOW_DESKTOP_QUERY` moved from the `(max-width: 1499.98px)` interim placeholder to `(max-width: 1365.98px)` — sketch 011 decision 18's cut, the complement of `LOG_BESIDE_SHEET_QUERY`. Added `useFold(openByDefault)`, returning `[open, toggle]` and resetting `open` to `openByDefault` whenever that argument changes (decisions_recorded 2) — an iPad rotation across 1366 resets the fold, and nothing about it is ever stored. Built `FoldRow.jsx`, the one full-row fold head every below-desktop fold in the route will use (this plan's own fold-version; plan 16 extends it to Balance, Watch for, Tasting, History and Batches). `VersionRow.jsx`'s `foldable` prop became `foldsOpen` (default `true`, the 1366-up answer); its own `FoldRow` — whose label doubles as the "Version" caption — is now the section's first child at every width, and the details `dl` always carries `id="fold-version"` and `hidden={!detailsOpen}`. `RecipePage.jsx` passes `foldsOpen={!belowDesktop}`. `notebook.css` gained the fold-row rules (reading only existing tokens) and lost the stale `.notebook-version .text-control` rule the retired "Details" link used to read.
- **Task 2:** The band's rhythm (frame row-gap 28/24, band row-gap and padding-top 24+20/20+16, grid column-gap 40/32) moved from the retired `(max-width: 1499.98px)` block into `(max-width: 1365.98px)`, as that block's first rules — roomy from 1366, tight below, at the same cut as the folds and the log. `tokens.css`'s two affected token comments were reworded to name the 1366 cut instead of the retired interim rung (values unchanged). `VersionRow.jsx`'s Show changes control now reads "Hide changes" while `showingChanges` is true and drops `aria-pressed` (decisions_recorded 5 — a control that renames itself by state must not also announce a pressed state, or it doubles the state in its accessible name).
- **03.5-band-probe.mjs** grew two groups: `folds` (control existence, `aria-expanded`/panel-display correctness, 44px height, full-row width, control-word text, click-to-flip, reload-restores-default, and crossing 1366 via `setViewportSize`, plus an app-vs-board style comparison at 1366/1024) and `rhythm` (the four gap/padding values at 1024/1365/1366/1920, compared against `1920-batch.html`/`1024-batch.html`'s own frame/band/grid structure).
