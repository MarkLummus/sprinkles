<decisions_recorded>
1. **One upright row for batches and for History.** counts.py's vnode (batches) and gen.py's history_upright (versions) draw the same row: a 12px mark column, a 14px gap, a row at least 44px tall, the title over a 12px meta line, and a 1px connector down to the next row. UprightRail draws it once, and plan 18 reuses it.
2. **The row in view is not a link.** Since 260917-odu the app never links an entry to itself, and RecipeHistory already renders it as text with aria-current="page". The boards draw every row as an anchor, but the row in view looks the same either way: bold ink, no underline. Plan 14 lists this for Mark.
3. **A row's meta is the tasting provenance, then 'out of machine −6 °C' when measured.** An unmeasured reading is left out, as the retired register's batchHistoryMetaFor left it out, rather than written as 'not measured'. The retired register's other parts (the at-the-machine words, the changed date) are not on the board's rows and are not carried.
4. **Pens.** The list is withheld while the record pen is open (G-03.3-4: the batch in view is not the one being recorded). Its rows are text while any other pen is open (D-UAT-1/2's link suppression), and its fold control stays usable, as History's does on 1600-pen.html.
5. **Order and count.** Rows follow sortedBatches (churn date descending, undated last). The count is `${n} batches`. The list exists only from two batches, so the singular never appears.
</decisions_recorded>
