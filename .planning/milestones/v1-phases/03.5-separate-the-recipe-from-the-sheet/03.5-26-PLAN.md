<decisions_recorded>
1. **The ordering rule (Mark, option 1).** Every batch has a standing day: its churn date when it has one, otherwise the first ten characters of its recordedAt (the UTC calendar day it was recorded), otherwise none.
   - Later standing day first. A batch with no standing day comes last.
   - Two batches on the same standing day order by recordedAt descending, a missing recordedAt last. This is CR-01's tie-break, kept.
   - A non-string recordedAt counts as missing.
2. **Why not recordedAt first.** It would reorder dated batches by when they were typed in. A batch entered late for an older churn date would then outrank a newer churn, and the batch list's own titles ("churned 16 Aug", "9 Aug", "2 Aug") would stop running newest to oldest.
3. **The seed's three standings stay reachable (D-05).** Mexican Chocolate flips from Tasted to Awaiting tasting. Strawberry (V2.1) stays Tasted, Coconut and Mocha stay Awaiting tasting, and Standard Base and Underbelly Light Base stay Not yet churned.
4. **The seeded recipes are all undated except Mexican Chocolate's v1 and the olive oil batch.** Coconut, Strawberry and Mocha therefore order by recordedAt day and then recordedAt, which is the order CR-01 secured: Coconut v2 over v1 (2024-12-28 over 2024-12-27), Strawberry V2.1 over V2 over V1 (V2 and V2.1 share 2024-09-14 and fall to the one-second recordedAt gap), Mocha v3 over v2 over v0.
</decisions_recorded>
