# Phase 03.6 Plan 05: Removing a step takes its lines with it Summary

Removing the step 'Gum slurry' in Olive Oil v1's pen now strikes its five lines (Whole milk 120 g, Sucrose 12 g and the three gums) under a 'Removed' head in the step's place, with no control on them, the total reads 799.7 then 666.0 g, the later steps renumber, and restoring the step brings back exactly the lines it took.
