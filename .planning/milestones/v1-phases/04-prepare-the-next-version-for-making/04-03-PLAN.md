## Phase Goal

Maker can print the new version as a bench sheet, carry it to the kitchen, and match the printed sheet back to its version — with the whole develop loop operable by keyboard and recoverable.

(ROADMAP Goal, verbatim; not in "As a / I want to / so that" form and not invented here.)

<objective>
The record carries the shape the printed log asks it to hold, so "a builder must not invent any field on paper that the app has no home for on return" (print brief § 7) is true before the log prints: declared scales and problems become lists of 0 to 2 that the pen, the reading and the tests read per recipe (sketch 010 D25, D26), the draw-temperature field leaves the data (D17), and the At draw / From freezer values get their stored home (D6, D6b) for 04-05's pen controls.

Purpose: PRINT-03's blank log prints only what a version declares and only fields the app records; the pen today draws an empty "This recipe only" cue and the Bitter chip for every recipe (BatchRow.jsx, research and CONTRACT § 2 "where the built app departs").
Output: the reshaped domain, validator and seeds; the pen and reading drawing only declared items.

Decision: promote — the single `declaredFlaw` and the boolean tick are replaced, not kept alongside; the store resets in 04-04 (no live data), so nothing needs lifting.
Invariant test (accepted into Task 2, the pen's half; Task 1 proves the data half from createBatch to the store file): for 0, 1 and 2 declared problems, a tick made in the pen round-trips through save, the reading, exportStore and importStore unchanged.
</assumption_delta_decision>
</objective>
