- db.js's upgrade callback today resets on any bump (`oldVersion > 0 && oldVersion < DB_VERSION` drops versions, batches, recipes) and must stay free of async/await (its own comment). seedIfEmpty (app/src/store/seed.js) reseeds an empty store at boot.

The upgrade runs at the next app open and is proven here against fake-indexeddb (db.test.js, db-reset.test.js); the device profile resets on Mark's first open after the build.
- Tests that open a real store set a fresh `new IDBFactory()` from fake-indexeddb per test (db.test.js's beforeEach); app/tests/db-reset.test.js may import idb directly (it lives outside app/src on purpose).
