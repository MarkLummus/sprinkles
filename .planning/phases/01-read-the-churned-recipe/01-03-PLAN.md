Create `app/src/domain/figures.js`: framework-free, no framework import, no DOM access, no store import. It consumes `computeBalance` and `weakestBasis` and computes nothing they already compute.

**`FIGURE_SPECS`** — an exported, ordered array of six specs. This order is the render order and never varies:

| key | label | unit | decimals | rule domain | targetKey | contributing fields |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `pac` | PAC | (none) | 1 | 0–40 | `pac` | `pac`, `msnf` |
| `pod` | POD | (none) | 1 | 0–30 | `pod` | `pod`, `msnf` |
| `fat` | Total fat | % | 1 | 0–30 | `fat` | `fat` |
| `msnf` | MSNF | % | 1 | 0–15 | `msnf` | `msnf` |
| `sugar` | Sugar solids | % | 1 | 0–25 | `sugar` | `sugar` |
| `solids` | Total solids | % | 1 | 25–55 | `solids` | `fat`, `msnf`, `sugar`, `other`, `emulsifier`, `stabilizer` |

The rule domain is the graduated rule's printed scale — the numbers at its two ends. It is not a target and must never be rendered or described as one.

The contributing-fields column is the load-bearing decision. PAC and POD list `msnf` alongside their own field because the lactose term routes MSNF into both: PAC is the mass-weighted `pac` contributions *plus* lactose × 100, and lactose is MSNF × 0.545. A figure that reported only its own field's basis would say PAC rests on allulose and salt while silently omitting that it also rests on two estimated dairy MSNF values. That under-reports the uncertainty, which is the exact failure FORM1-02 exists to prevent.

**`describeDeviation(value, band, decimals, unit)`** returns `{ kind, amount, words }`:
- `band` absent or `null` → `{ kind: 'none', amount: null, words: 'no target set' }`. Branch on the target key being absent from `version.targets`, never on its value being falsy — a band of `[0, 0]` and an absent band are different facts, and defaulting an absent one to a zero-width band draws a band the recipe never authored (D-10).
- `value < lo` → `{ kind: 'below', amount: lo - value, words: '<amount><unit> below <lo><unit>' }`.
- `value > hi` → `{ kind: 'above', amount: value - hi, words: '<amount><unit> above <hi><unit>' }`.
- otherwise → `{ kind: 'inside', amount: 0, words: 'inside <lo>–<hi><unit>' }`.

A value exactly equal to `lo` or to `hi` is **inside** the band. This is the adjacency decision for this phase: the bands the recipe authored are inclusive ranges, and reporting `0.0 above 26` for a value of exactly 26 is a distinction the maker cannot act on and reads as a defect. Round the amount to the figure's decimals before wording it, so a value 0.04 outside a band does not read as `0 above 26`; if the rounded amount is 0, the figure is inside.

The unit is appended to both the amount and the bound for percent figures — `4.5% above 20%`, `inside 16–20%` — and omitted for PAC and POD, which are unitless indices. The en dash separates the two bounds.

**`buildFigures(version)`** returns `[]` when `computeBalance(version.rows)` is `null`. Otherwise it returns one object per spec, in spec order:

`{ key, label, unit, decimals, domain, value, band, deviation, contributorRowIds, basis, estimatedRowNames }`

plus, on the fat figure only, `milkfat`, `addedFat`, and `addedFatShareOfFat` from the balance.

- `value` comes from the balance: `pac` and `pod` from the balance's own fields, the rest from its percent block.
- `band` is `version.targets[targetKey]` when that key is present, `null` otherwise.
- `basis` is the worst basis across all the spec's contributing fields, using the same stated → derived → estimated → inherited ranking `weakestBasis` applies.
- `contributorRowIds` are the ids of rows contributing a non-zero amount of any contributing field, in the version's own row order — never sorted, never grouped, never deduplicated by name.
- `estimatedRowNames` are the ingredient names of those contributors whose basis for at least one contributing field is `estimated` or `inherited`, again in row order. A `derived` basis is not estimated and does not appear here.

Never mutate the version, its rows, or its embedded ingredient records. Never sort the rows.

Nothing in this module may return a verdict, a score, a pass or fail, a rating, or any statement about how the ice cream will taste. It reports a number, a band, and the distance between them.

Write `app/src/domain/figures.test.js` covering the behaviour table above.
