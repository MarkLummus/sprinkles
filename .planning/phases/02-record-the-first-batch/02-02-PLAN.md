## Phase Goal

**As a** home ice cream maker, **I want to** write on the churned recipe what I actually did — the amounts I really used, the steps I changed, what I measured, and how it tasted — **so that** the next version has evidence to cite instead of my memory.

*Provenance: as recorded in 02-01 — a mechanical restatement of the ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line for Phase 2, not a new story. Run `/gsd mvp-phase 2` to rewrite the Goal line itself.*

<objective>
Two more of the sheet's three places for ink, and the file format that carries them off the device.

The method gains its strike and its one changed line per step, in the maker's words. The margin's churn section gains the measured values the sheet asks for — come-up, draw temperature, overrun — each with its unit on its label, plus draw notes, the free-text ingredient-notes line that gives the oil bottle's open date a home, and a next-time note labelled as intention. A field left blank reads the word `unknown`, and nothing anywhere is filled in from the recipe.

Then the store file grows to carry batches: `schemaVersion` 2, a `validateBatch` built in the shape `validateVersion` already established, and the same refuse-the-whole-file gate. A batch that cannot survive an export and an import is not really stored.

Purpose: after 02-01 the maker can write amounts. After this plan they can write what actually happened — which is the difference between a formula with numbers scribbled on it and a record of a churn.

Output: the full churn half of the batch record, writable, readable, and portable.
</objective>
