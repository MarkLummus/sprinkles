## Phase Goal

**As a** home ice cream maker, **I want to** open the pen on a version I have already churned, change it with the old values struck beside the new ones, and save the result as a next version that names its parent and my reason, **so that** I can develop the recipe without touching the record of what I actually made.

*Provenance: as recorded in `03-01-PLAN.md` — a mechanical restatement of the ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line, not a new story. Run `/gsd mvp-phase 3` to make the ROADMAP line itself a user story.*

<objective>
Give the pen everything the plan can be changed by: every row's amount, allocation and presence; every step's words, targets, uses and presence; every authored note; and the headnote prose — with the two removal cross-flags and the stale-amount flag that keep a removal from silently orphaning something.

Purpose: REC1-03 in full. 03-01 proved one grams field end to end; this plan is the expansion out from that proven slice. The removal cross-flags are the phase's subtlest requirement — the 2 Aug batch struck step 1 and used no lecithin, and on the plan those are two separate removals linked through `uses`, never one cascading into the other, because a maker may keep step 1 and put something else in the oil.

Output: `diff.js` and `uses.js`; the pen's row controls in `IngredientTable`; the pen's step controls, cross-flag and stale-amount flag in `Method`; per-note editing and the inherited-from marker in `Authored`; the headnote prose field in `Headnote`.
</objective>

`diff.js` — `buildDiff(current, baseline)`:
- Identity: `buildDiff(v, v)` reports `changed` false on every row, every step, every target, every figure, and the total.
- Grams: with the oil row's grams at 48 against a baseline of 40, that row reports `gramsFrom` 40, `gramsTo` 48, `gramsChanged` true, and every other row reports `gramsChanged` false.
- Share follows grams even where grams did not move: with the oil row at 48, whole milk's `gramsChanged` is false while its `shareChanged` is true — `46.3%` against `45.9%`.
- Share is compared at display precision: a grams change too small to move `formatShareOfBatch`'s one-decimal output reports `shareChanged` false.
- Figures are compared at display precision: `buildDiff` reports total fat `from` 18.0, `to` 18.8, `changed` true, and reports `changed` false for a figure whose underlying value moved by less than half of the last printed decimal.
- Figures are zipped by `key` from `buildFigures`, in `FIGURE_SPECS` order, and the six keys are `pac`, `pod`, `fat`, `msnf`, `sugar`, `solids`.
- Removed rows and steps are excluded from the figure and total math on both sides — with the lecithin row removed in `current`, the figures are those of the remaining eleven rows.
- The total: `from` `799.7 g`, `to` `807.7 g`, `changed` true with the oil row at 48.
- Steps: changing step 3's `instruction` reports `textChanged` true for step 3 and false for every other step; changing only `purpose` or only `aside` also reports `textChanged` true.
- Targets: changing step 8's `blend` chip from `45 s` to `60 s` reports that chip `from` `45 s`, `to` `60 s`, `changed` true, and the step's other chip unchanged. A target chip present in one side and absent in the other reports `changed` true with the absent side `null`.
- Uses: adding a row id to step 2's `uses` reports `usesChanged` true for step 2 and false elsewhere; reordering a step's `uses` with the same members reports `usesChanged` false.
- Removal: a row removed in `current` and not in `baseline` reports `removed` true; a row removed in both reports `removed` true and `gramsChanged` false.
- `buildDiff` never mutates: both arguments deep-equal a `structuredClone` taken before the call. It never reorders: the row descriptors are in `current.rows` order and the step descriptors in ascending `n`.
- Empty: `buildDiff` over a version with no rows returns an empty `rows` array, an empty `figures` array, and a total whose `from` and `to` are both `0.0 g`.

Two pure modules, both framework-free, both under `app/src/domain/`, both opening with the header line every domain file uses and carrying a doc block per export in `batch.js`'s style, citing the decision each settles.

**`app/src/domain/diff.js` (new).** Export one function, `buildDiff(current, baseline)`. It returns `{ versionLabelChanged, headnoteChanged, rows, steps, targetsChanged, figures, total }`.

`rows` is one descriptor per row of `current`, in `current.rows` order, never sorted: `{ id, ingredientName, gramsFrom, gramsTo, gramsChanged, shareFrom, shareTo, shareChanged, stepFrom, stepTo, stepChanged, removed, removedChanged }`. `gramsChanged` compares the two numbers directly, because grams are what the maker typed and this app never rounds them. `shareFrom` and `shareTo` are `formatShareOfBatch`'s own output strings against each side's active mass, and `shareChanged` compares those strings — a difference no reader can see on the page is not reported as one. `stepFrom`/`stepTo` carry the row's step allocation. A row absent from `baseline` reports every `From` as `null` and every `Changed` as `true`.

`steps` is one descriptor per step of `current`, in ascending `n`: `{ n, textChanged, targets, usesChanged, removed, removedChanged, textFrom }`. `textChanged` is true when any of `leadIn`, `instruction`, `purpose` or `aside` differs, and `textFrom` carries the baseline's four fields so the component can render the parent's text struck beneath without re-reading the baseline itself. `targets` is one descriptor per chip, matched by `label`, `{ label, from, to, changed }`, with `null` on whichever side lacks the chip. `usesChanged` compares the two `uses` lists as sets, so reordering the same members is not a change.

`figures` zips `buildFigures` over each side — each side filtered through `activeRows`/`activeSteps` first, so a removed row never reaches `computeBalance` — matched by `key`, in `FIGURE_SPECS` order: `{ key, label, unit, decimals, from, to, changed }`, where `from` and `to` are the numeric values and `changed` compares them at the figure's own display precision, `value.toFixed(decimals)`. Never re-derive the figure math: the whole point of zipping `buildFigures`'s output is that the diff and the rules can never disagree.

`total` is `{ from, to, changed }` over each side's active mass through `formatGrams`, compared as those strings.

State the same guarantee `buildFigures` states, in the same words: never mutates either version, its rows or their embedded ingredient records, and never sorts.

**`app/src/domain/uses.js` (new).** Export four functions. `stepsUsingRow(version, rowId)` returns the non-removed steps whose `uses` includes `rowId`, reading `step.uses` as `[]` when the key is absent — absence is a fact, not an error, the doctrine `batch.js` already applies. `removedRowsUsedBy(version, step)` returns the rows named in that step's `uses` that are removed, in the version's row order; it is what raises the flag beneath a step. `orphanedRows(version)` returns the non-removed rows that appear in at least one removed step's `uses` and in no non-removed step's `uses`; it is what raises the mirror flag beside a row. `stepsWithStaleAmounts(version, baseline)` calls `buildDiff(version, baseline)` and reads its row descriptors — it does not recompute a delta of its own — and returns one entry per non-removed step whose `textChanged` is false and whose `uses` name at least one row with `gramsChanged` true, as `{ n, changes: [{ rowId, ingredientName, from, to }] }` in the step's own `uses` order.

Removal never cascades. Say so in the module header in those words: every function here reports; none of them writes a `removed` flag, and no caller may set one on the maker's behalf. A maker may keep step 1 and put something else in the oil, so the second removal is always the maker's own tap.

Every look-up by a key that came from stored data uses an own-property check or an array scan, never a bare bracket read against a maker-influenced key — the discipline T-02-32 named.

**`app/src/ui/IngredientTable.jsx`.** In `developing` mode each row gains, after its grams field: a step-allocation control, a `remove` text control, and — when the row is flagged — the orphaned-row line.

The step-allocation control is a `<select>` over the version's non-removed steps, showing each step's `n` and `leadIn`, bound to the draft's value for that row, with an explicit `aria-label` naming the row and the column. A row with a `splitStep` (whole milk and sucrose each go into two steps) keeps both: render the primary allocation as the choice and leave `splitStep` untouched — editing a split allocation is not in this milestone's scope and the row must not lose its second step by being edited. When the allocation differs from the value the pen opened on, the old allocation renders struck in ink immediately before the control, the same sibling-not-ancestor arrangement the grams strike uses.

The `remove` control is a text button, never an icon and never a colour: `remove` on an active row, `restore` on a removed one, exactly one control per row. Removing sets the draft row's `removed` to true and nothing else — it does not clear the grams, and it does not touch any step. A removed row renders in place, never filtered out and never moved: its name, its grams and its share all struck, its Data cell unchanged, and its accessible name reading `removed` as a word. It contributes nothing to the share denominator or to the total, which already follow from `activeRows` — confirm the balance the component computes still passes through it in `developing` mode.

The `% of batch` cell gains its own strike: when the row's share differs from the value the pen opened on, the old share renders struck in ink immediately before the current one, independently of whether the grams moved. A row whose grams held but whose share moved because another row changed shows the share strike alone — that is the whole reason share is compared separately from grams.

The orphaned-row flag renders beside the row's name when `orphanedRows(draftVersion)` names it: the words `used by step 1, which is removed` — naming the removed step by its number and lead-in — followed by a `remove this row` text control. Tapping it removes the row, one tap, nothing else. The flag clears when the step that caused it is restored, because it is derived, not stored.

Extend `rowAccessibleLabel` so a row's accessible name carries every changed fact in words: `was 40 g, now 48 g`, `was 5.0%, now 5.9%`, `was step 8, now step 6`, and `removed`. The strike is never the only carrier.
