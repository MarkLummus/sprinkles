<objective>
Make the pen keep the page. Prose edits in place as the printed paragraph rather than as a form field; a step's purpose and aside appear only when they hold text or the maker asks; the twelve uses checkboxes hide behind one control per step; the batch pen's per-step line does the same; and every field in a step body carries its step in its accessible name.

Purpose: this is the other half of the critique's first P1. Plans 01 and 02 took every control out of the printed spread; this plan makes what is left in the spread read as the sheet — the finding that scored the developing state 1 out of 5 for aesthetic and minimalist design, failed seven of eight cognitive-load items, and put roughly 261 interactive elements on one page.

Output: the printed-paragraph treatment in `app.css`; on-demand purpose and aside and the uses line in `Method.jsx`; the on-demand per-step line in the batch pen; the record's prose in the text face; and the accessible names D-28 fixes.
</objective>

## Planner decisions recorded for this plan

1. **A prose field carries no border at rest, and the global `:focus-visible` outline is the outline the brief describes.** `route-recipe-version.md` § 3 asks for "the hairline ink outline appearing only when the paragraph is focused". Plan 03 already gives every focusable element a 1.5px ink outline on focus, so giving prose fields a second, thinner border that also appears on focus would draw two rings. One mechanism, no movement (an outline occupies no layout box), and the field reads as the paragraph until it is focused.
2. **The visible field labels come off the pen's prose fields; the accessible names stay.** The critique named "full-width native inputs labelled Lead-in, Instruction, Purpose, Aside" as the shape of the failure. D-28 requires each field's accessible name to carry its step, which `fieldLabel` already produces, so removing the visible label word loses nothing to a screen reader and is what lets the step read as a step. Counted fields keep their visible labels, because a number needs its box and its label.
3. **The on-demand and checklist open/closed states are component-local `useState` in `Method.jsx`, not page state.** They are display state, distinct from the draft data `onChangePenStepField` and `onTogglePenStepUses` already write. Lifting them to `RecipePage` would put ten booleans per step into the page's state for no gain.
4. **Escape inside the open checklist calls `stopPropagation` on its own handler.** The page's Escape listener from plan 02 sits on `document`, above React's root container, so stopping propagation in the checklist's React handler prevents the page listener firing for that same key press. This is what makes D-24's "a second Escape applies the pen rule" true rather than one Escape doing both.
