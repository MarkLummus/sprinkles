# Phase 03.1: The imprint and the binder - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-09-08
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Phase 03.1 builds the 2026-09-08 Impeccable revisions of the three surface briefs on the recipe page, with no new requirements. The page's top band becomes two blocks: the recipe (name, version line, intro paragraph) at the prose measure on the left, and beside it a block whose running head reads **Versions**, which holds every control and every fact about the recipe that is not the recipe: the openers, the version list, the current version's parent, batch and reason, the batch list with the churned date, and, while a pen is open, that pen's ceremony. The spread beneath (Ingredients and Method in column one, Balance then Notes in column two) is the printed page and nothing else: in-place ink while a pen is open, never a button. While a pen is open its Cancel/Save pair is repeated once at the foot of the page under a hairline rule.

Every control on the page is redrawn in the brief's "binder" treatment (called the control treatment here): ink at hairline weight, no fill, no radius, no icon; disabled by a dashed stroke with its reason in words; pressed, current, open or marked by a 1px outline plus weight; keyboard focus by a 1.5px outline on every focusable element; checkboxes drawn as ink squares; selects drawn like buttons with an ink chevron; native date inputs with the calendar icon hidden; number spinners hidden; prose fields growing with their text and no resize grip; every link underlined in ink.

The plan's pen keeps the page: prose (intro paragraph, each step's lead-in and instruction, purpose and aside, each authored note) edits in place as the printed paragraph with the outline on focus only; a step's purpose and aside open on demand behind "add purpose" / "add aside"; the uses list reads as one line of names with a "change" control that opens the twelve checkboxes for that step alone. The batch pen's per-step line opens on demand the same way. The three build gaps named by the critique close: the rule heads strike the parent figure and draw the hollow tick while the pen is open, the six rules leave the tab path in the pen, and a grams field holding a non-number blocks the save in words with the row marked and focused. The column arithmetic holds in every state (total row strikes the bare number with the unit once and never wraps; the Step column widens in the pen with the Data column going blank-headed; "unallocated + 3"). "was 50 g oil · 800 g" and the split-step suffix read in ink. Escape closes only an untouched pen and returns focus to its opener; closing any pen by save or cancel returns focus to its opener.

Running heads lose their book vocabulary: Ingredients, Balance, Method, Notes, Versions; the recipe block wears no head because the recipe name is its head. Control labels are shortened and Cancel comes first in every pair.

Not in this phase: the sub-1280 stack (Phase 4, UX1-01); the accessibility items the rebuild does not touch (row double-announcement, step-number list semantics, live announcements; Phase 4 carries); the third-voice colour for the system's own words ("unknown", "unmarked"; open, built colour stands); the list page's own design beyond what global rules give it; anything the briefs name as out (adding rows or steps, target bands, scaling, library, printing, draft persistence, phone).

The re-critique baseline is `.impeccable/critique/2026-09-08T12-33-31Z__app-src-ui-recipepage-jsx.md` (25/40, three P1s); the phase is done when a re-critique against the revised briefs finds the three P1s and the two P2s closed.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

The briefs settle everything they name. The decisions below fill what they left to this discussion and, where marked, revise them; each revision is recorded for Impeccable to absorb into the briefs and `DESIGN.md`.

### Region names (revises the briefs' vocabulary)
- **D-01:** The book vocabulary is dropped from every running head. Mark: "these are 'inside baseball' terms; only someone who knows the background would ever understand 'headnote' or 'imprint'; this product is not a book." The heads read, in page order: *(no head on the recipe block)*, **Versions**, **Ingredients**, **Balance**, **Method**, **Notes**. "Versions" was chosen over Imprint, Versions and batches, History, Log, Development and others "for now". — **Reversibility:** reversible — six strings and their tests; the brief's names live only in planning files.
- **D-02:** The recipe block (name, version line, intro paragraph) wears **no running head**: the recipe name in 2rem is its head. The heading outline is h1 recipe name, then h2 Versions, Ingredients, Balance, Method, Notes; no hidden "Recipe" heading.
- **D-03:** The version line under the name reads just the version line ("50 g oil · 800 g"). The churned date moves wholly into Versions; nothing recorded prints in the recipe block. This supersedes Phase 2's D-13 placement of the churn date in the version line (the fact itself, latest churn date and no count, stands).

### Versions: what it holds and in what order
- **D-04:** Reading state, top to bottom: **the openers**, then **the version list**, then the current version's **lineage** (Parent / Batch / Reason as labelled lines, then Show changes), then **the batch list**. The block's width is everything right of the recipe block's 65ch measure; the two blocks' seam does not line up with the spread's.
- **D-05:** Openers sit as two groups on two lines: **Develop** alone on the first line (the plan's pen); **Record batch** (or **Record another**), **Amend**, **Add tasting** together on the second (the record's), separated by the small gap. Which openers appear follows Phases 2 and 3 (Record batch when the version has none; Record another, Amend and Add tasting when a batch is in view).
- **D-06:** While a pen is open its ceremony **replaces the openers** at the top of Versions; the other openers are not shown disabled, since the ceremony's presence says why. The version list, lineage links and batch list stay beneath as plain text with one hint sentence for the block: **"Links return after you save or cancel."** This narrows Phase 3's D-UAT-1 (every opener visible and disabled with its reason) to the links only; the one-pen rule itself is unchanged.
- **D-07:** The version list is a **vertical list, one version per line, flat** (no indent by lineage), in creation order, the current entry by weight and outline as plain text, "churned" in small print beside a churned version's line, every other entry an ink link. No batch count (Phase 2 D-21).
- **D-08:** The lineage reads as **labelled lines**: `Parent` (the parent's line, a link), `Batch` (the cited batch's churn date, a link; absent when none was cited), `Reason` (the maker's text, or "none recorded"), then **Show changes** as a control on its own line at the end. A root version shows no lineage. The reason paragraph therefore leaves the recipe block. The toggle's pressed state and `?changes` addressing are unchanged (Phase 3 D-02).
- **D-09:** The batch list is **always a list, one batch per line, even with one**: "churned 2 Aug 2026" as an ink link, the open batch by weight and outline as plain text, an amended batch adding "amended 5 Aug 2026" in small print on its line. With no batch the same line reads **"no batch yet"** in ink, and Notes also keeps a "no batch yet" placeholder line where the record would sit (both places).

### Control labels and their order (revises Phase 3 D-01)
- **D-10:** **Cancel comes first in every pair.** Plan's pen on a churned version: **Cancel · Save**, where Save creates the child. Plan's pen on an unchurned version: **Cancel · Save as · Save**, the desktop pattern: Save as forks, Save writes over. Batch pen and tasting pen: **Cancel · Save**. The foot band repeats whichever set the ceremony shows. Mark: "when there are 2 save versions, the Save as and Save are fine; this is a common pattern in software." Supersedes Phase 3 D-01's outcome-at-the-end labels; D-01's opener wording is replaced by D-11.
- **D-11:** Articles are dropped and labels shortened: **Develop**, **Record batch**, **Record another**, **Amend**, **Add tasting**, **Show changes**, **remove**, **restore**, **change** / **done**, **add purpose**, **add aside**, **done differently**. The batch pen's per-step line is labelled **Done differently** (was "What did you do differently?"); the **Skipped** checkbox keeps its word.
- **D-12:** Capitalisation: **sentence case** for buttons and selects ("Save as", "Record batch", "Show changes"); **lowercase** for in-line text controls that sit inside a row or a step and read as part of its text ("remove", "restore", "change", "done", "add purpose", "add aside", "done differently"); units lowercase everywhere (existing rule). Recorded for `DESIGN.md`.

### The control treatment (the brief's "binder", `route-recipe.md` § 6 and § 8)
- **D-13:** The treatment is written as **global element rules** in `app/src/styles/app.css` (button, select, `:focus-visible`, checkbox, date input, number input, textarea, a), every value through a token. The list page's Export and Import buttons and its links inherit it without being designed; no page can paint browser chrome again. — **Reversibility:** reversible — rules can be scoped later by wrapping them in a selector.
- **D-14:** Outline weights: **focus 1.5px** (`--focus-outline-width` reads `--rule-baseline`) on every focusable element, including the graduated rules, radios, checkboxes and links; **state 1px** (`--rule-graduation`) for pressed, current, open batch and marked rows. A focused element that is also stateful reads both. The graduated rule's own `:focus` rule is retired in favour of the global `:focus-visible` rule.
- **D-15:** **Hover** thickens a control's border from 1px to baseline weight (1.5px), nothing moves and nothing recolours, so hover and focus read alike; the pointer is a hand over anything that opens or links. Revises the brief's "nothing on hover".
- **D-16:** **Target size:** vertical padding rises from `--gap-hair` (2px) to `--gap-xs` (6px) on buttons, selects and in-line text controls so every control is at least 24px tall (WCAG 2.2 2.5.8, which UX1-01 tests). Revises § 8's padding values; recorded for Impeccable.
- **D-17:** **Selects** (the Step column, the citation) are drawn like a button: `appearance: none`, ink hairline, no fill, a small ink chevron drawn in CSS; the dropdown list itself stays the browser's, a second named exception beside the date input's segment highlight.
- **D-18:** **Links** carry a hairline ink underline everywhere, visited the same as unvisited, hover unchanged; text that is not a link is never underlined. The running head "Sprinkles" is included.
- **D-19:** **Number inputs** keep `type="number"` with the spinner arrows hidden in CSS; sized to three digits and a sign as the brief says.
- **D-20:** **Placeholders:** the version-line field and the reason field carry an example in ink small print ("e.g. 55 g oil · 800 g"; "e.g. less oil after the batch of 2 Aug"); no other field carries one; the date input's native "mm/dd/yyyy" stays as part of its exception. A placeholder is never a default: a blank field saves as blank.
- **D-21:** **Blocked save:** Save stays enabled; pressing it shows the sentence beside both pairs, outlines the offending row, and **moves focus to the offending field** (the grams field, or the version-line field for a blank or duplicate line) so the row scrolls into view. No live dashing while the maker is still typing.
- **D-22:** In the pen the Step column widens by a new token so each option reads "3. Build the base"; the **Data column goes blank-headed** in the pen to make room, as the brief allows; the name column absorbs the rest.

### The pen keeps the page: on-demand fields, the uses line, the foot, Escape
- **D-23:** An emptied purpose or aside field **collapses back to its "add purpose" / "add aside" control when it loses focus while empty**; the draft stores no purpose; focus is not moved. Opening one via the control focuses the new field.
- **D-24:** The uses line's control reads **"change"** closed and **"done"** open; the checklist **stays open until "done"** is pressed, and the line of names above it updates live as boxes change. Escape inside the open checklist closes the checklist only and returns focus to the control; a second Escape applies the pen rule.
- **D-25:** The batch pen's per-step line is **on demand**: the Skipped checkbox is always present; the line sits behind a "done differently" text control, an emptied line collapses on blur, and Amend opens the lines that hold text. The churn section's three measured fields and three prose lines, and each tasting's words and next-time lines, are **always present**, drawn as printed lines with the outline on focus only.
- **D-26:** The foot band, shown only while a pen is open: a **hairline ink rule across the whole page**, then the pair **right-aligned under the Notes column**, the blocked-save sentence beside it; no head, no opener, no field. Mark: "I like the hairline rule across the page, but I want it right-aligned under the notes column."
- **D-27:** "Untouched", for Escape, means **unchanged from what the pen opened with**: a fresh pen's baseline is blank, Amend's baseline is the record's values (the existing `isDraftDirty` baseline argument). Escape closes an untouched pen and returns focus to its opener; with ink it does nothing and Cancel is the one exit. After a fork saves and the page lands on the child's URL, focus goes to the **child's Develop control**; every other close returns focus to the control that opened the pen.

### Accessibility scope against Phase 4
- **D-28:** This phase takes **only the accessibility items the rebuild touches**: h2 headings for the rebuilt regions (D-02), every field's accessible name carrying its step in the rebuilt step body ("Step 3, instruction"; "Step 3, done differently"), the intro-paragraph field before Save in the tab order, focus return for the moved ceremonies (D-27), the six rules out of the tab path in the pen (a named build gap), one hint sentence per block (D-06). The row `aria-label` double-announcement, the step-number list semantics and live announcements stay Phase 4 UX1-01 carries as STATE.md lists them.

### Carried forward, not re-decided here
- **D-29:** The briefs as revised 2026-09-08 are binding where the decisions above do not touch them: `route-recipe.md` § 3 "The imprint" (now Versions) and § 6/§ 8 the control treatment; `route-recipe-version.md` § 3 "The pen keeps the page", § 6 fields, column arithmetic and the parent's words in ink, § 7 the three build gaps and the open third-voice colour; `route-recipe-batch.md` § 6 revision (native date fields, Escape, focus return, field widths, the record's prose in the text face). Phase 3 D-02 (`?changes`), D-03 (struck baseline), D-04 (unique version line), D-UAT-1/2 (one pen, no dialog, route-keyed page) except as D-06 narrows; Phase 2 D-21 (no batch count), D-24 (browser's own leave warning).
- **D-30:** The **third-voice colour** (the system's own words about a record: "unknown", "unmarked", "date unknown") stays open; the built colour stands and no builder changes it (brief § 7, Mark 2026-09-08). The Two-Ink leaks the critique named ("was 50 g oil · 800 g", the split-step suffix) do change to ink, per the brief.
