<objective>
Land the portion as the authored amount, end to end, with every figure provably unmoved.

A row stops carrying one amount and one or two step references and starts carrying `portions` — an array of `{ step, grams }` in step order — whose sum is the row's total, derived by one function and never stored. The seed is rewritten to the settled split amounts, the split-step prose loses its now-duplicated numbers, and the store resets: `DB_VERSION` bumps to 4 and the upgrade drops and recreates both object stores so a returning profile comes back empty and reseeds clean.

Purpose: everything Phase 03.3 renders reads this shape. Building the step-order page against `step`/`splitStep` and then rebuilding it against `portions` is the cost this plan avoids.

Output: `rowGrams` in `app/src/domain/rows.js`; twelve seed rows on `portions`; the three seed constants living with the seed; `db.js` resetting at version 4; and `app/tests/db-reset.test.js` proving the whole path against a real IndexedDB.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
The `assumption-delta` detector returned `detected: true` on a `pluralization` signal. Its literal match ("second pass" in the ROADMAP Phase notes) is a false positive, but a genuine assumption delta is present and is the substance of this phase: **a row goes from carrying one amount to carrying many.**

- **Primary noun:** `portions`. A portion — an amount at a step — is now the thing a row is made of.
- **Decision: `promote`.** `portions` becomes the primary representation and the unsplit case becomes the one-portion variant of it. `grams`, `step` and `splitStep` are removed, not kept alongside. This is D-01's "one rule covers split and unsplit with no second case and no branch at the read sites", and it is why the shape change is atomic rather than incremental: keeping the singular field alongside the plural one would let a later read site silently take the old path.
- **Companion invariant test (accepted):** every row's total equals the sum of its portions, asserted for a one-portion and a multi-portion row alike. It goes red the instant a future phase reintroduces a stored total. Planned in `03.2-04` Task 3.
</assumption_delta_decision>
