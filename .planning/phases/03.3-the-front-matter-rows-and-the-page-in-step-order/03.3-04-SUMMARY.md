# Phase 03.3 Plan 04: The thirteen labels and the structured figure label Summary

## Accomplishments

- Renamed the eleven front-matter labels in `VersionRow.jsx` (Version, Why, From batch, From version, Next version) and `BatchRow.jsx` (Correct, Time to temperature, Air with "overrun %" as the detail, At the machine, How did it turn out?, Melt test) — the version-line field's visible span and aria-label, the reason field's aria-label only, the lineage's three labelled lines, the opener buttons, and the churn/tasting fields' visible spans and aria-labels in both recording and reading states. `Draw temperature, °C` confirmed untouched everywhere.

- Threaded `figureLabelText` into the two other places a figure's label already reached plain text: `domain/advisories.js`'s estimated-exposure sentence and `RecipePage.jsx`'s `markedFigureLabel` (feeding `IngredientTable.jsx`'s "contributing to" accessible-name phrase).
