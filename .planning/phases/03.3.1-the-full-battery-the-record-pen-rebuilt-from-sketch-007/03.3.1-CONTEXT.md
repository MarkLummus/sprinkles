# Phase 03.3.1: The full battery: the record pen rebuilt from sketch 007 - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-09-13
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Phase 03.3.1 rebuilds the batch record pen to the settled sketch 007 battery — the 5-point goldilocks axes with the core/declared split, the new churn-phase and tasting-phase fields, the standalone defects checklist, the note before Texture & flavor — extends the store shape to carry it, and updates the batch row's read view to the same battery. No new requirements: the design input is the settled sketch state (variant A, browser-verified 33/40, zero P1s), packaged in the structural contract and the sketch findings; the revised surface brief `route-recipe-batch.md` (2026-09-12) is the phase's prerequisite and is satisfied.

**One revision to the confirmed design happened in this discussion, and it is the phase's biggest fact:** Mark replaced sketch 007's *decoupled saves* (churn-row save vs "Save batch & tasting" as two different actions with state-based labels) with **one save action, repeated** — Cancel | Save batch twice, identical in label, meaning, and visual treatment. Saving a churn and saving a tasting are the same action, one save being more complete than the other. The structural contract's save matrix, its `#churn-saves`-only-while-tasting-open rule, and the brief's save-pair sections are superseded where D-01–D-04 below conflict; everything else in the contract stands byte-for-byte. The revision is recorded for Impeccable to absorb into the brief and the contract.

Not in this phase: the printed page's geometry (Phase 4, which reads the final field list from this phase's plan); the unreadable-as-made decision (stays open on the brief's books; the rebuild does not touch the as-made column); more than one tasting per batch (a future capability); draft persistence across reload (UX1-02, Phase 4).

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### The one-save model (revises sketch 007's decoupled saves)

- **D-01:** The record pen has **one save action with two identical ceremonies**: Cancel | **Save batch** at the **end of the record** — after the tasting section when it is open, after the churn section when it is not, just above the shared Next time — and Cancel | **Save batch** at the **foot**, with **Add tasting** beside the foot set while the tasting section is absent. The two ceremonies are **visually identical regardless of position on the page** — same markup, same treatment, differing only in placement (Mark's explicit call). Both Cancels discard the whole pen draft (D-24); identical controls, so the carried P3 "name the two Cancels' scopes" resolves itself. The imprint ceremony stays retired for this pen per the brief's 2026-09-12 revision — with the tasting open the page holds two Cancel | Save sets, never three (Mark: "3 save/cancel button sets on the page with potentially different meaning. that's wrong.").
- **D-02:** **What a save persists is everything the record currently holds.** Mark's framing: "saving a churn and saving a tasting are the same action, one being more complete than the other." Pressing Save with the tasting section open but **empty persists the churn only** — no empty tasting record is manufactured (his earlier disable-the-button idea dissolved with the model). The labels "Save batch & tasting" (state-based) and the sketch's save matrix, including `#churn-saves` rendered only while the tasting section is open, are **retired**. The content-sniffing ban survives as: the label never changes with what is typed; the one label is true at every completeness.
- **D-03:** **A single tasting per churn** — a batch carries zero or one tasting. "Add tasting" is offered on the saved record only while no tasting exists; once one exists, **Correct reopens the same pen with everything editable**, tasting fields in place — one door, same Save. This overturns the brief's binding "a batch is one churn section plus zero or more tastings" (now zero or one) and "adding a tasting is not an amendment" (completing the record is the same save).
- **D-04:** **The record's dates read: churned, tasted, changed — and only the latest change shows** (Mark: "the important dates for a version are: created, churned, tasted, corrected (only the last one)", renaming corrected → changed). Every save after the first dates the record's `changed` value, replacing the amendment list; the batch list's small print reads "changed 5 Aug 2026" (was "amended"). The churn date stays the record's name; an absent tasted date reads "date unknown".
- **D-05:** **The churn date is required — the one deliberate exception to no-required-fields.** The batch's churn date is its name and the version line carries the latest churn date, so Save batch on a blank pen (or any pen without a churn date) refuses in words: **press-to-block per 03.1 D-21** — Save stays enabled, pressing shows a sentence beside both save sets and moves focus to the churn date (working sentence: "Enter the date you churned."; exact wording at planner discretion within the plain-words rule). Mark chose this over "date unknown" for a date-less batch.
- **D-06:** **The save-model revision is recorded for Impeccable to absorb**: `route-recipe-batch.md` § 3 "Saving is decoupled, two scopes" and "The ceremony's save pairs", § 7's save-scope bindings, and the structural contract's "Save and save-pair matrix" are superseded by D-01–D-04. A re-run of `/impeccable document` after the build records what shipped.

### The seeded 2 Aug record on the new battery

- **D-07:** The seed batch's transcription onto the battery: **oil character 4** (the sheet's 4.5 had no whole stop; Mark chose 4, "leaning strong"), sweetness 4, bitterness 5 → the **"Bitter · declared" toggle on**; hardness, smoothness, scoopability, body blank (never marked on the sheet); melt test 3, melt style blank; tasted date blank → "date unknown"; tasting temperature −12; tempering blank; time to draw temp. 20, out of machine −6, churn duration 30; exit consistency blank; airiness blank (the sheet's overrun "?" was never a number); At the machine "Soft, not greasy"; ingredient notes "oil bottle opened 24 Jul"; note blank; defects none; Next time blank.
- **D-08:** **Mark reviews the rewritten seed data before execution commences.** The phase plan sequences the seed rewrite (`data/olive-oil.js`, `data/batch-2026-08-02.js`) as its first task and pauses for Mark's review of the rewritten records before the remaining tasks execute.

### The store

- **D-09:** **The store resets rather than lifts** (confirmed: the profile holds only the seed and throwaway tests since the 03.2 reset). `DB_VERSION` bumps, the upgrade **drops and recreates both object stores**, a returning profile reseeds clean through `seedIfEmpty`, the seed files are rewritten to the new shape, and `transfer.js` writes the new `schemaVersion` and **refuses older files** with a clear error — no lift branch, no second ladder (the 03.2 D-05/D-08 pattern repeats; `upgrade` still awaits nothing but IDB-native requests). The migration discipline is deferred again, not abandoned: the next shape change after Mark holds real records is expected to lift. — **Reversibility:** one-way for records held outside source — a pre-change export no longer imports, the same trade 03.2 recorded and Mark accepted while no real records exist.
- **D-10:** The new stored facts per the roadmap and brief: batches gain the churn-phase fields (time to draw temp., out of machine, churn duration, exit consistency, airiness, tempering) and tasting-phase fields (melt style, melt test) with units on the labels; a marked axis stores its **1–5 value** (an unmarked axis is absent, never zero); the four defect chips and the declared "Bitter · declared" toggle are stored records; blank stays absent. Whether the zero-or-one tasting is stored as a single object or a list of at most one is planner discretion.

### Carried into the plan (roadmap; not re-decided)

- The two P3s: drop the underline on filled controls; ~~name the two Cancels' scopes~~ (resolved by D-01's identical ceremonies). The persistence contract for the save scopes is settled by D-02. The undo-retired-on-resize edge and the matchMedia-under-emulation caveat carry as build constraints and verification notes. The read-view summary line ("Soft (2) · grainy · bitter") belongs to the batch read view, in scope per the brief. The plan must cite the structural contract by section name in every task and verify conformance in the browser.

- Test strategy: domain modules node-env (validation, blank-is-absent, snapshot immutability), component tests per file, browser verification against the structural contract.

## Specific Ideas

- Mark's workflow, verbatim: "print version -> churn version -> enter churn record with optional as-made changes -> <wait 24hrs> -> enter tasting record -> [next version | record another batch]". The pen already carries the as-made and step lines; the record is one page that becomes more complete over time.
- "saving a churn and saving a tasting are the same action, one being more complete than the other."
- "with the tasting added, we have 3 save/cancel button sets on the page with potentially different meaning. that's wrong." — the page holds at most two, and they are identical.
- "i'd like both save ceremony sections to look identical regardless of location on page."
- The record's dates: "created, churned, tasted, corrected (only the last one)" — rendered as churned / tasted / changed (latest only).
