### Box or word — which shape a new control takes
**Source:** sketch 008 @ `0fa1015` header line 230 and Settled values line 280 (the box-or-word line; the recorded-figure line inserted at 279 by `109733d` moved it); DESIGN.md § The binder @ `a0126a2`.
**Apply to:** any task that introduces or moves a control. A **hairline box** commits, discards, or starts a new record and stands apart (Save batch, Cancel, Record batch). An **underlined word** acts on what is already on the page and stands beside it (Correct, Clear, Add tasting, Remove tasting, Restore tasting). An underlined word that opens a panel beneath it is a **disclosure**: the panel is its open state, the word does not change, `aria-expanded` carries it ("3 later versions", "1 later batch"). A **square and a word** records an independent yes/no (the checkbox, a defect, Show changes) — never a count. **Joined boxes** choose one from a set; picking is final and Clear is the way back. Two consequences this phase: the per-control Clear is a word, not a box; both count disclosures need `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`.
