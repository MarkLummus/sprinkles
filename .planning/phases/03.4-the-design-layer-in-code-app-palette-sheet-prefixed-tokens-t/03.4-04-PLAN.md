<decisions_recorded>
1. **"Last event" means the save timestamps, not the kitchen dates** (RESEARCH.md assumption A2, left open by D-05). The helper reads `version.createdAt`, `batch.recordedAt` and `batch.changed` — when the maker put something into the app. It does not read `churn.churnDate` or `tasting.tastedDate`, which say when something happened at the machine and can be back-dated. Recency is about where the maker left off, so the save clock is the right one. Recorded here because the two readings order the list differently the first time a churn is back-dated.
2. **Only the three Notebook standings are implemented.** D-07 names five, but "ready to make" and "saved idea" need the Recipe book and the Idea log, which do not exist and carry no data; inventing a destination field to fake them would be speculative. The helper returns the three reachable standings and its comment names the two that arrive with their destinations.
3. **Where the actions lead:** Record a batch, Continue developing, Next version and Adapt all open the recipe at its latest version, because the recipe page is the one place development and recording happen today; Record a tasting opens the newest batch's own path. Two actions can therefore share a destination while reading as different intents — that is honest about what exists rather than inventing a route.
4. **Ties in recency** break on the latest version's id, so two recipes saved in the same millisecond always order the same way in every run and every test.
</decisions_recorded>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| stored record -> the DOM | A recipe name, a place name and the maker's Next time render on Home; any of them may have arrived through an import |
| stored record -> the domain helper | Timestamps and batch shapes reach pure functions that must not throw on a malformed record |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03.4-12 | Elevation of Privilege (rendering) | the lead block's Next time and every row's text | high | mitigate | all maker-authored text renders as React children, never as markup; RecipeList.test.jsx keeps an escaping test asserting angle brackets arrive escaped and no raw tag appears in the markup |
| T-03.4-13 | Denial of Service | a malformed imported batch reaching the recency fold | medium | mitigate | `lastEventAt` ignores a null timestamp instead of coercing it, `activeWork` tolerates a batch with no churn, and both are covered by their own node tests — an incomplete row renders rather than throwing the page away, the discipline the current row renderer already follows |
| T-03.4-14 | Repudiation | a standing that misstates what happened | medium | mitigate | the standing is derived from the stored record's own fields on every render — no cached or stored flag exists to drift (D-05) |
