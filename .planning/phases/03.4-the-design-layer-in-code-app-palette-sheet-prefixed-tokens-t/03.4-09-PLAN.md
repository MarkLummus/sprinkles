<decisions_recorded>
1. **The canvas is per context, not flipped globally.** `body` takes `var(--app-background)` as its default and a single `body:has(.recipe-page, .page-head, .not-found)` rule hands the cream back to the three paper frames. The reviewer's original IN-03 suggestion (paint the App white on html/body full stop) would have turned the paper frames' overscroll white — a new cosmetic defect against "cream is reserved for the three paper frames". The `:has()` switch was measured to re-propagate when the matched descendant appears after first paint, so a client-side navigation into and out of a Sheet frame moves the canvas with no JS (debug variants C and D).
2. **`html` stays unstyled, deliberately.** Giving the root element a background is the one change that would *stop* body's background reaching the canvas (measured, variant B). The prohibition is written into the test pin, not only into a comment.
3. **The rail's padding reads the gap scale, not the board's hand-copied numbers.** Board 170 line 30 draws `8px 20px 32px 30px`; the rail reads `var(--gap-xs) var(--gap-m) var(--gap-l)` (6px 20px 32px 20px). This follows 03.4-07's own recorded decision #5 — the board governs structure and order, tokens govern values — and the difference is recorded in the conformance row rather than smuggled in.
4. **The rail gets `box-sizing: border-box` with its padding.** There is no global box-sizing reset in this project (the 260922-d2y quick task added border-box per rule, twice). Without it, 40px of padding plus a 1px border would push the rail's declared 224px basis to 265px and move the whole page.
5. **The focus fallback is one scoped `:focus` rule, not a device-conditioned one.** `.shell__place:focus` reaches every stop test 4 names, because More's summary also carries `.shell__place`. A `(pointer: coarse)` condition was considered and rejected twice over: shell.css is pinned to exactly one media condition (the touch step-down), and what an iPad with a keyboard and trackpad attached reports for `pointer` could not be verified from here — a rule that might not match the device is worth less than a rule that always paints. The accepted cost is that a mouse click on a shell stop also leaves the ring until focus moves, which is exactly the trade app.css:594-607 already made for the fork's landing focus. Mark rules on that cost at UAT.
6. **Test 4's truth needs splitting by device at re-verification, not here.** The tab row and More are `display: none` above 760px, and Mark's iPad reports 1366 landscape / 1024 portrait, so that arm of test 4 cannot be exercised on the iPad at all. The re-verifier should exercise the tab-row arm at phone width and the rail/tools arm on the iPad. Recorded here so it is not re-read as a code gap.
</decisions_recorded>
