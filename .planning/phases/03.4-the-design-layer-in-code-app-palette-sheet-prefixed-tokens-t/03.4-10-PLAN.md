<decisions_recorded>
1. **The step is 1099.98px, the ladder's own number.** DESIGN.md line 352 already names `max-width: 1099.98px` ("the spread collapses"), and the debug session measured a candidate at exactly that number: full-width columns and a one-line name and note at every width from 760 to 1090, with the row form handed back at 1100 where it first reads. Adding the step costs the project no new number, which is what DESIGN.md's Drawn-For Rule asks.
2. **The new block goes above the existing 759.98px block in the file.** Both are top-level siblings and both match below 760px; the later one wins on the declarations they share. Writing the wider step first keeps the phone block's own readings (its padding step-down and its stretched actions) intact.
3. **The stacked lead's actions get no declaration at all.** Mark decided the two actions sit at their natural width, left-aligned, rather than stretched. That is what the measured candidate already produced (125.5px and 81.67px at 1090px), because `.home__actions { width: 100% }` and `.home__action { flex: 1 1 0 }` live inside the 759.98px block and are not reached by the wider step. The decision is recorded here precisely because it is delivered by *not* writing a rule — a later reader must not "fix" the omission.
4. **The rows adopt the stacked template at the same step.** Mark's words were "adopt iPhone layout earlier in responsive ladder", and the rows' own defect is in the same band: the desktop grid's fixed standing and tally tracks plus the actions measured 778px of content inside a 760px page (802px when a tasting is awaited), gone by 900px. Giving the rows their own third number (899.98px) would add a breakpoint to the ladder for a 140px band; giving them the same 1100px step the lead now takes keeps Home to one story and one number.
5. **The rod is hidden in the stacked lead, the row keeps its own.** Measured: kept, the lead's rod renders as an orphan 8x56px bar on its own row and adds 69px of height. Hiding it follows the 759.98px block's own precedent. The row's rod stays at every width in this plan — dropping it is phone-width only, ruled under G-03.4-6b and shipped by plan 12.
6. **`.home__lead-name` gains `overflow-wrap: anywhere`.** Its sibling `.home__name` has carried it since 03.4-04; the lead's name did not, which is why it spills rather than breaking. A hardening independent of the step, so a long name cannot reproduce the symptom inside the row form above 1100px.
</decisions_recorded>
