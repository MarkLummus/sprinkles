# Phase 03.4 Plan 12: The phone row loses its rod, tally and secondary action; the lead's meta line loses its grams

**Closed G-03.4-6b and G-03.4-6c: at 393px a recipe row now matches board 171's own three-element row (place/name/standing plus one filled action, no colour rod, no batch tally, no secondary action), the lead's meta line carries only the version identity, and DESIGN.md plus the conformance record both state the exception Mark ruled.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~20 min
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 6 (home.css, home.test.js, RecipeList.jsx, RecipeList.test.jsx, DESIGN.md, 03.4-GAPS-CONFORMANCE.md)

## Accomplishments

- **The phone row as board 171 draws it (Task 1):** inside the existing `(max-width: 759.98px)` block, `.home__row`'s template dropped to two columns (`1fr auto`) and three area rows (place/standing, name/standing, actions/actions) — the `rail` and `tally` area names are gone, since the elements that filled them no longer generate boxes. Three new row-scoped rules hide the row's rail, its tally (`.home__meta`), and its secondary action, each comment-cited to Mark's UAT rulings 6.2 and 7.3 of 2026-09-22, board 171 lines 36-46, and the reversal of 03.4-07 decision #2. Each selector begins with `.home__row`, never bare, so the lead's own two actions (which share the `.home__action--secondary` class) cannot be lost.
- **The lead's meta line drops the mass figure (Task 2):** `HomeLead` no longer computes a balance or a mass string; the meta paragraph renders `versionIdentity(entry.versions, entry.latestVersion)` alone. The two domain imports the mass computation used (`computeBalance`, `activeRows`) were removed as orphans — confirmed by search that nothing else in the file reads either. The RED test (run before the fix) failed on the pre-existing computed figure ("799.7 g"); the GREEN test then passed with the figure gone.
- **DESIGN.md and the conformance record brought up to the app (Task 3):** the App marks "The rail" bullet gained one dated sentence recording the phone-width exception, leaving its original sentences intact. Conformance rows 28, 31 and 32 were rewritten to name the rules that now hide the row's rod, tally and secondary action, all three set to `matches`. Departure #2 was rewritten as superseded and closed, and a new departure #5 records the one difference this plan deliberately left open: the single remaining filled action still stretches to full width under the phone block's own pre-existing touch rules (03.4-03), which board 171 does not draw.
