## Verdict

**Pass with conditions.** Measured in the running app (main checkout at `dd9118d`) at 1920, 768
(iPad Mini) and 393 (iPhone 15) on `/`, `/notebook` and `/recipe/olive-oil-ice-cream-v1`. The
desktop shell and Home are token-true and match DESIGN.md's App marks line for line: icon in
accent, word in companion, active place on the subtle surface via the router's `aria-current`,
224 × 44 rail places, the 8px Notebook rail and hollow batch tallies with the count in words, five
sprinkles in navigation order, Caveat self-hosted and loading. The detector reports nothing; no
console errors; no duplicate ids; zero horizontal overflow at 768.

Two things fail at the world's edges. The phone step's tab row was never measured at 393: five
tabs total 532px, so More (and Ingredients and Kitchen inside it) is off-screen. And the App
ground was applied to Home's root rather than to the shell, so the header and rail sit on the
Sheet's paper on every other route, and the placeholder pages render in user-agent type.

## Findings (most severe first)

1. **[P0] The bottom tab row overflows the phone.** At 393 the five `.shell__tabs` items measure
   103.6 + 127.7 + 109.6 + 93.0 + 98.2 = 532px; Idea log is clipped at 393 and More sits at
   x = 433.9–532, entirely off-screen, with its list opening at the same x and growing the fixed
   bar to 365px. Ingredients and Kitchen cannot be reached by touch. Violates D-16 and the
   cross-cutting "zero overflow at 393" bar. **Brief for `/gsd-quick`:** give the tab row its own
   item rule in `shell.css` (`flex: 1 1 0; min-width: 0; flex-direction: column; padding:
   var(--gap-xs) 0; font-size: var(--app-size-label)`), pin the bar to
   `height: var(--app-size-tab-h)` (it measures 69px against the 56px clearance today), open More
   as a panel above the bar rather than in its flow, and hide `.shell__tools` below 760 (the header
   is 107px tall at 393 with Search/Import/Export repeated inside More). Pin the item padding in
   `shell.test.js`; verify the five rects and the open panel at 393.
2. **[P1] The App ground stops at Home.** `body` is `rgb(255,255,255)` on `/` (via
   `body:has(.home)`) and `rgb(247,247,244)` — `--sheet-ground` — on `/notebook` and the recipe
   route at every width; `.shell`, `.shell__head` and `.shell__rail` paint nothing. Violates
   route.md § 1 (paper inside three frames only; the shell is App) and the Context Boundary Rule.
   **Brief for `/gsd-quick-batch`:** `.shell { background: var(--app-background); color:
   var(--app-text) }`; `.recipe-page` (and the not-found page) paint `--sheet-ground` themselves;
   delete `body:has(.home)`. Flag to Mark: one background declaration lands on the Sheet's root,
   which CONTEXT put out of scope; the alternative is the "white card on cream" look home.css
   already refuses.
3. **[P1] Placeholder pages have no rules.** `shell.css` declares no `.place__*` selector;
   `/notebook` renders its `h1` at the UA's 30px/700 with a 20.1px margin and the note at 15px
   Sheet table size in ink on paper. Violates the every-value-reads-a-token convention and D-10's
   "shell intact, the place's name as title". **Brief (same batch as 2):** `.place__title` reads
   Home's title role (`--app-size-title`, 800, -0.02em, `var(--gap-l) 0 var(--gap-hair)`),
   `.place__note` reads `--app-size-meta` in `--app-text-secondary`; `shell.test.js` pins both to
   `.home__title`/`.home__lead-meta` so the two App pages cannot drift.
4. **[P2] Two brand marks on the recipe route.** `RecipePageForRoute` still renders the Sheet's
   `.running-head` "Sprinkles" (12px uppercase, link to `/`, 34px band) directly under the shell's
   22px "Sprinkles"; two `header` landmarks; 141px of chrome above the H1 at 393. The running
   head's job (the way home) moved to the rail. **Brief for `/gsd-quick` after Mark decides:**
   empty the band or make it recipe context ("Notebook", App label role); keep `.page-head` as the
   `.page-status` anchor (`shell.test.js` guards this).
5. **[P2] The filled action is 70px tall.** `.home__action` is `content-box` with
   `min-height: 44px` + 12px padding × 2 + 1px border: every action measures 70 × 125.5 /
   70 × 81.7 (70 × 170.5 each at 393) and drives the row to 111px. `--touch-min` is a floor, not
   a target; DESIGN.md's App controls are "plain, rounded, and quiet". **Brief for `/gsd-quick`:**
   `box-sizing: border-box` on `.home__action`, pinned in `home.test.js` — the same fix the 03.3.1
   audit applied to `.axis-mark__stop`.
6. **[P2] "Adapt" goes where "Next version" goes.** Both `TASTED` actions link to
   `/recipe/olive-oil-ice-cream-v1`; nothing on that page starts an adaptation. A control is a
   promise (PRODUCT principle 2). The same check applies to "Continue developing".
   **Brief for `/gsd-quick` after Mark decides:** drop the secondary until Adapt exists, or make
   it open the pen in the mode its word names.
7. **[P3] Heading levels on Home.** Each row's recipe name is an `h2` sibling of the `h2`
   "Recipes" above it; make row names `h3`. `/gsd-quick`, one line in `RecipeList.jsx` + test.
8. **[P3] Recipe-name links wear the Sheet's hairline underline.** `.home__name a` and
   `.home__lead-name a` measure `underline` 1px/offset 2px under 24–28px bold headings, and
   `home.css`'s header comment claims Home overrides the global underline — no rule does.
   DESIGN.md leaves the App link treatment open. `/impeccable` decides; `/gsd-quick` writes the
   rule or fixes the comment.
9. **[P3] The App's focus ring reads `--sheet-ink`.** The global `:focus-visible` is the only
   focus rule, so every rail place, tab and action reads a `--sheet-` token — the one thing D-02
   says the App never does except for the hand. DESIGN.md lists the shared focus colour as
   unresolved: a decision for Mark, no code yet.
10. **[P3] The hand has nothing to say in the seeded app.** `batch-2026-08-02.js:46` sets
    `nextTimeNote: null`, so D-18's one hand appearance renders on no route. An injected
    `.app-hand` confirmed the role: Caveat `loaded`, 22px / 27.5px, `rgb(31,61,122)`, Georgia
    fallback stack. Forced-colours and print fallbacks not exercised here. **Human UAT:** record a
    Next time, return to Home, read it at desktop and on the iPad.

**Watch (D-19), not a finding:** the Notebook companion `#EE0803` is exactly 4.50:1 on white and
reads as a pure red on the uppercase NOTEBOOK label beside a red rail on every row — DESIGN.md
already flags it against the No-Verdict Rule; Mark confirms or falls back to `--app-text`.

## What's working

- Token discipline is complete and provable: `tokens.test.js` gates unresolved `var()` reads,
  dead tokens and `sheet-`/`app-` prefix discipline; every colour, size and weight read from the
  DOM resolves to a token.
- The destination grammar is exactly DESIGN.md's — icon in accent (`rgb(253,91,87)`), word in
  companion (`rgb(238,8,3)`), Kitchen's one token for both, the active place on
  `--app-surface-subtle` from the router's own match, a 1px divider between the two groups.
- Every text companion clears AA (4.50–4.60:1; Kitchen 5.88:1); the filled action reads white on
  `#1576DE` at 4.51:1; the hidden navigation leaves the accessibility tree.
- Home's spacing lands on the shared scale as drawn (lead → section 32, section → row 12); 768
  is clean on both Home and the recipe route; Caveat ships with its OFL and loads from the app's
  own origin.
