Three of this phase's own stated must-haves are not met in the code as it stands, despite the full automated suite (1093/1093) and the build both passing:

1. **D-16's bottom tab row overflows at 393px** and makes Ingredients and Kitchen (both living inside More) unreachable by touch — a measured, confirmed regression against the phase's own success criterion, not a matter of interpretation.
