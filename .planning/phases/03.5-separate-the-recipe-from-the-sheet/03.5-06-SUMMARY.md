# Phase 03.5 Plan 06: The Sheet's table and step pen in style 6, D-19's hand Summary

**Every ingredient-table state (reading, recording, the pen, show-changes) now reads sketch 011's style 6 — plan grams before the name, an inline estimated/unreviewed chip, and the batch's as-made grams and a changed Instructions line written in a new Sheet-side `.sheet-hand` rule — while the step pen opens exactly one step at a time with Cancel restoring and Done keeping, remove moved to the closed step's own line, and the uses control renamed to "change the ingredients".**

## Accomplishments

- `app/src/ui/IngredientTable.jsx` — a shared `.ingredient-table__plan-grams` "slot" carries the row's plan amount in every state: plain text in reading/recording (via `GramsCell`'s non-developing branch), the pen's own field with a struck parent value as a sibling before the slot, or (in show-changes, via `DiffGramsCell`) a struck-then-current pair. The total row nests its own struck-then-current pair *inside* the slot instead. `dataFlagFor`'s estimated/unreviewed flag renders as `<span class="target-chip ingredient-table__flag">` after the name whenever it names one. `AsMadeCell`'s read-only span moved from `.ink-text` to `.sheet-hand` everywhere. The header/total row/`columnCount` collapsed to one unconditional style-6 shape (`Ingredient`, `[As made]`, `% of batch`); the Data and Remove columns and their markup, CSS rules and tokens (`--sheet-col-data`/`--sheet-col-remove`) are gone.
- `app/src/ui/Method.jsx` — `StepPenBody` is exported and no longer owns its own reveal state: `Method` holds `openStepN` (one step open at a time, reset whenever the pen closes), threading `isOpen`/`onOpen`/`onDone`/`onCancel` down. Remove/restore moved to the closed step's own `method-step__acts` line (`edit this step · remove`/`restore`); the open form's uses toggle now reads "change the ingredients"/"done", its purpose/aside triggers reworded to "add a purpose"/"add an aside" and joined onto that same controls line; Cancel (underlined, first) and Done (a bare `<button>`, reading the binder's own global hairline-box rule) close a new `method-step__done-cancel` line. The open fields sit inside a new `method-step__open-fields` wrapper carrying a hairline outline. The reading branch's changed-step line moved from `.ink-text` to `.method-step__changed.sheet-hand`.
- `app/src/ui/RecipePage.jsx` — `restoreStepFromVersion(penDraft, version, stepN)`, a pure exported helper returning a copy of `penDraft` with one step replaced by `structuredClone` of the version's own step; `handleRestorePenStep(stepN)` wired to `Method` as `onRestorePenStep`.
- `app/src/styles/app.css`/`tokens.css` — `.sheet-hand` (the Sheet's own 20px/1 hand, mirroring `.app-hand`'s forced-colors/print fallbacks but never its `max(22,20)px`/1.25 leading); the style-6 rules (`.ingredient-table__plan-grams`, the extended `.ingredient-table__flag`, the portion note's indent) and their literal board tokens; the 393 narrow two-line list joined into the *existing* width-only `(max-width: 759.98px)` block; the step pen's `.method-step__acts`, `.method-step__open-fields`, `.method-step__done-cancel` rules and their tokens; the now-orphaned `.ingredient-table__grams-cell` and `.method-step__edit` rules deleted.
