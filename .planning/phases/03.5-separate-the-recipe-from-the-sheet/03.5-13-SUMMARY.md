## Accomplishments

- **Task 1 (tracer, TDD):** `app.css`'s touch union condition changed from `(max-width: 759.98px), (pointer: coarse)` to `(pointer: coarse)` alone — the six rules and their source-order position are unchanged. - **Task 3:** For option-keep-760 ("no condition changes"), rewrote the width-only block's prelude, the wide-touch block's comment and `useBelow760`'s own comment to say they now govern only the record pen's battery and are the route's one width cut not yet measured — kept at 760 per DESIGN.md and Mark's answer, naming the complement pair (759.98/760).
