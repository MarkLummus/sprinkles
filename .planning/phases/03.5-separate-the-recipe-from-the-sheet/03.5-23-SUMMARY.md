# Phase 3.5 Plan 23: Full-row fold heads and Tasting as its own section Summary

**Tasting is now a section named Tasting with an h2 heading on the boards and in the app, every fold head fills its row (Version 281 to 353 at 393, Tasting 232 to 353 with its date at the row's end), and the band probe measures each head against its row and the board instead of its parent, which is how the gap got through.**

## Accomplishments

- **G-03.5-8d (Tasting as a section).** Drawn on the boards first: gen.py's `batch_log` draws `section[aria-label=Tasting]` with an `h2` head whose inline style resets the margin and inherits the face; `phone_folds` puts the fold button inside that h2 and closes the fold before the section ends. The app follows: `TastingReading` renders `section.tasting-reading > h2.region-name > FoldRow`, with the head wrapper gone and its two orphaned app.css rules removed. Nested in place, after the churn cells and notes, before Next time and the Recorded line.
- **G-03.5-4 Tasting (fold fills its row).** With the vestigial flex row gone the h2 is a block, so the button's `width: 100%` reaches `.notebook-log`'s content box: 232 to 353 at 393, to 683 at 723, to 350 at 1366, with the date at the row's end.
- **G-03.5-4 Version (fold fills its row).** `align-items: stretch` in the 723.98px `.notebook-band__grid` block: the base `start` acts on the inline axis once that override makes the grid a flex column. Version 281 (234 on Mexican Chocolate) to 353 at 393 and 683 at 723.
- **Probe.** The immediate-parent width check is gone. Each fold has a named row oracle (`ROW_OF`): Version, the band grid's second track from 724 or its content box below; History, `.notebook-band`; Balance and Watch for, `.recipe-page`'s second track from 984 or its content box below; Tasting and Batches, `.notebook-log`. A fold with a count has the count's right edge at the row's right edge. At 393 and 723 the width equals the matched-width board's. A throwaway pass saves a second batch and a second version so Batches and History meet the same oracles. Widths 723 and 983 were added.
- **Boards.** Byte-identical no-op regeneration first (18 files across both folders), then the edit; the 14 changed boards render pixel-identical to HEAD in full-page screenshots; 393-all-folded, 1600-no-batch, details-fold and versions-1-vs-many are unchanged. README decision 20 records it.
