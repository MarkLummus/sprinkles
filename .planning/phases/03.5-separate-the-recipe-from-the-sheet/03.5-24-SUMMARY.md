**The upright rail's line now runs only between circles, exactly as upright-393.html draws it (mark 4px below its row's top, line 2px below the mark and 4px above the next), and the horizontal History rail's line runs from the first node's centre to the last node's centre on the boards and in the app.**

## Accomplishments

- **G-03.5-5, upright (Task 1, tracer).** The diagnosis held: the connector's offsets assumed a top-aligned mark, but the row centred its mark in 44px, so the line crossed its own mark, poked 10px above the first and stopped short of the last. The mark now sits in a title-line box (`.notebook-upright__mark-box`, `--app-notebook-history-name-line-h` tall, mark centred), the link's grid aligns items to the start, and the connector is the row's `::before` (was `::after`), so the later positioned link paints over it, with no z-index. Its top composes from the name line height, the mark size and `--gap-hair` (18px), its bottom is `-1 * --gap-s`, its left centres it on the mark. The CSS comment that called the offsets "this file's own reasonable placement, not board-measured pixels" is rewritten as upright-393.html's measured geometry.
- **G-03.5-5, horizontal, boards first (Task 2).** Mark's 2026-09-29 decision (the horizontal rail connects nodes only) is drawn on the boards before the app: `history_rail` in gen.py and longhist.py's inline strip put the track and the node row in one positioned strip as wide as its content, the track from left 12px to right 168px. The regeneration was proved a no-op first (18 files byte-identical in both folders). DESIGN.md lines 472-473 and README decision 21 say the line connects nodes only.
- **G-03.5-5, horizontal, the app (Task 3).** `RecipeHistory.jsx` wraps `.notebook-history__track` and the node list in `.notebook-history__strip` (`position: relative; width: max-content`); the track's left is `--gap-xs + mark/2` and its right is `--gap-xs + node-w - mark/2`. The node list stays the later positioned sibling, so marks still paint above the line, and the fade stays the rail's last child.
- **Probes.** The harness gained `readAppUprightConnectors`, `readBoardUprightConnectors`, `uprightConnectorChecks` and `uprightBoardMatchChecks`. The band probe's `history` group and the batches probe's `many` group check every non-last row (connector at least 1px below its own mark and 1px above the next, centred on the marks, no connector on the last row, each mark hit-tests to itself) and compare three offsets with upright-393.html. `many` also runs at 723 now. The band probe's `rail` group checks the track's edges against the first and last marks, against 1366-batch.html and 1920-batch.html, and with the rail forced to overflow.
