<objective>
Make the pen's remove and restore links act on their own line, prove it end to end with one path, then make every domain reader that read a row's removed flag read a row as removed when all its lines are out.

Purpose: Mark's answer 1 on decision 51 (2026-10-05): "if an ingredient is shared across multiple steps, operations on each step must not affect the other". Today one flag per row means pressing remove on Step 2's Whole milk strikes Step 3's too. Per line, Step 3's milk is Step 3's. The same plan carries the pen's half of answer 3: the pen's portion line reads the lines still in.
Output: `isLineRemoved`, `isRowRemoved` and a line-aware `activeRows`; a per-line control, strike and portion line in the pen; a jsdom end-to-end test that drives Olive Oil v1; the uses and diff readers moved onto `isRowRemoved`.

Task order: Task 1 is the tracer, one press on one line wired through the domain, the page's draft state and the table, verified through the whole page. It is additive on purpose: the draft row's own `removed` flag keeps working untouched, so the suite stays green and plan 02 retires it. Task 2 expands the domain readers so a one-line row removed through its line reads exactly as it did through the row flag.
</objective>

<decisions_recorded>
1. **Mark's three answers (decision 51, 2026-10-05), the phase's whole scope.** (1) C: each remove link removes its own line, restore likewise; the ingredient leaves the recipe when its last line goes; decision 44 C replaces 44 B. (2) B: a removed step's lines go with it, struck, restored with the step (plan 05). (3) The wrong figures are fixed with the per-step change: the pen's portion line follows the lines still in (this plan) and Show changes' percentages follow the lines (plan 04).
2. **Stored truth.** `portions[i].removed` is an optional boolean on a portion, absent meaning in. This is a planning choice inside the brief's own model (brief (a)). A step's removal is derived and never stored per line (plan 05).
3. **The old whole-row flag stays in the record shape and means "every line out".** `row.removed` is still a required boolean in transfer.js's row check and `removed: false` on every seeded row in eight data files. Leaving both alone means zero seed churn and no migration. A stored `true` reads as every line out (older records and the fixtures that set it). The pen stops writing it `true`: from plan 02 the pen carries removal on the lines and writes the row flag `false`. Rated costly: seven modules read the record shape, so undoing the per-line flag later is a coordinated change, not a local one.
4. **Strictness.** Every reader compares `=== true`. A truthy string on a record never removes a line (T-03.6-01).
5. **`activeRows(version)`** returns the very same row object when every line is in and a copy with a shorter `portions` array otherwise, in the row's own order, dropping a row with no line in, never mutating its input. Its callers need no other change.
6. **A struck line's portion line reads the opening figures** (the row's lines in when the pen opened, over the opening batch) and a line that is in reads the lines still in over the live batch. This is decision 44's 86.3% fix applied per line, and it reproduces every number the board's generator derived (perstep-capture.mjs, `renote`).
7. **The measured numbers to hit (Olive Oil v1, Whole milk 120 g in Step 2 and 250.4 g in Step 3, Sucrose 12 g and 64 g, batch 799.7 g).** Resting: 799.7; milk lines 46.3% in all, sucrose 9.5%.
