# Phase 03.6 Plan 01: Per-line removal in the pen Summary

One press on a split ingredient's remove link now strikes that line alone, the Step 3 line keeps its own link and amount, the total and shares come from the lines still in, and every domain reader treats a row as removed only when all its lines are out.

## Accomplishments

- Task 1 (tracer): `activeRows` returns each row reduced to the lines still in (the same object when every line is in, dropped when none is). The pen draft carries `removed` per portion, `handleTogglePenLineRemoved` flips one portion, saved portions carry `removed`, and the table strikes, labels and recomputes per line. `RecipePage.perStep.test.jsx` drives Olive Oil v1 from "Next version" through the press and the restore and holds the board's numbers: 799.7 to 679.7 g, `250.4 g of 250.4 g · 36.8% in all`, `120 g of 370.4 g · 46.3% in all`, and back.
- The pen's portion line follows the lines still in: a line that is in reads the lines still in over the live batch, a line that is out reads the opening figures. The stale 370.4 g and 47.5% of decision 51 finding 4 (a) no longer print.
- Task 2: `uses.js` (removedRowsUsedBy, coveredRowsFor, orphanedRows) and `diff.js` (buildRowDiff, both branches) read `isRowRemoved`. A one-line row removed through its line reads exactly as the old flag did; a split row with one line out is not removed and its total drops by that line.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical functionality] The pen's dirty check ignored a pressed line**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** `isPenDraftDirty` compared only grams, step and the row flag, so a pen whose only change was one line pressed out counted as untouched and Escape would have closed it, discarding the press.
- **Fix:** it also compares each portion's removed flag against the stored one. New case in RecipePage.test.jsx.
- **Files modified:** app/src/ui/RecipePage.jsx, app/src/ui/RecipePage.test.jsx
- **Commit:** 12900cf

**2. [Rule 2 - Missing critical functionality] A blank amount on a line that is out blocked the save**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** `findBlockedRow` (lineage.js) checked every portion of a row not removed as a whole, so a struck line with a blank field would block Save with "needs an amount, or remove the row" though the line is already out.
- **Fix:** it skips a portion whose draft flag is set. New case in lineage.test.js; its sibling line in still blocks.
- **Files modified:** app/src/domain/lineage.js, app/src/domain/lineage.test.js
- **Commit:** 12900cf

**3. [Rule 3 - Blocking] isRowRemoved tolerates a row with no portions array**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** a pre-existing uses.test.js case builds a bare row with no `portions`; a strict `row.portions.every` threw, and the plan requires every existing case to pass unchanged.
- **Fix:** `row.portions ?? []`; `activeRows` itself stays strict and the three bare `{ id }` fixtures in rows.test.js were given one portion each, as the plan says.
- **Files modified:** app/src/domain/rows.js
- **Commit:** 172e13a

**Total deviations:** 3 auto-fixed (2 Rule 2, 1 Rule 3). **Impact:** the first two touch lineage.js and RecipePage.jsx's dirty check, both outside the plan's file list but required for the pen to behave with a line out; no scope beyond that.
