<objective>
Make a version saved with a line out come back with it out: accepted on import, absent from the reading Sheet and from recording, and aligned with a batch's as-made values.

Purpose: plans 01 and 02 made the pen act per line and save what was pressed. A saved version is the thing a maker reads, records a batch against and exports. This plan makes every one of those read the lines still in, and closes the one place a per-line cut could silently misplace a number: a batch's as-made array is index-aligned with the stored row's portions, and filtering a line out would shift the indexes under it (T-03.6-06).
Output: a validator that accepts and refuses the field correctly, a reading and recording table that receives stored rows and skips lines that are out, an index tag on kept portions, and as-made totals that honour it.

Task order: Task 1 is the tracer for this plan: one line removed in the pen, saved, and read back in the reading state, with the validator and a round trip. Task 2 expands to recording and as-made alignment.
</objective>

<decisions_recorded>
1. **Optional means absent or boolean.** `null` is refused too: the pen always writes a boolean, an absent key is what every seeded row has, and a third spelling would be a third meaning (T-03.6-05).
2. **The table receives the stored rows and the full method in every state.** Reading and recording used to receive `readingVersion.rows`, which already dropped whole removed rows and could not tell the table a row once had two lines. With stored rows the table keeps `isSplit` (stored portion count), the stored portion index for as-made, and decides itself which lines to show: it keeps lines that are out, struck, in the pen and in Show changes, and skips them in reading and recording. A group that ends up with no line in is never created, so no empty step head prints.
3. **`activeRows` tags a kept portion with its stored `index` only on a row it had to copy.** A row with every line in comes back as the same object and its positions already equal their indexes. Readers use `portion.index ?? i`.
4. **A reading with a line left in a split row reads the lines still in.** The portion line is `formatPortionLine(portion.grams, rowGrams(liveRow), baselineMass)` with `liveRow` taken from the table's `baselineActiveById` Map (plan 01); in reading and recording the baseline is the live batch.
5. **The one-line-left portion line is left as it builds and recorded as an open item** (task statement). Do not decide it, do not hide it.
6. **Measured numbers for the saved version (Olive Oil v1 with the Step 2 milk line out):** total 679.7 g; Whole milk '250.4 g of 250.4 g · 36.8% in all'; Sucrose '12 g of 76.0 g · 11.2% in all' and '64 g of 76.0 g · 11.2% in all' with shares 1.8% and 9.4%.
</decisions_recorded>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| imported file to store | A store file the maker (or anyone who handed them a file) imports; the import validator is the only gate before a write |
| stored version to domain readers | A stored version row now may carry `removed` on a portion |
| batch as-made array to stored portions | The array is index-aligned with the stored row's portions |
