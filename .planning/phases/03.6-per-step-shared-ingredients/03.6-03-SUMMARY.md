# Phase 03.6 Plan 03: A saved version with a line out reads without it Summary

A version saved with a line out now imports and exports with the flag intact, reads in the Sheet without that line (Olive Oil v1 with the Step 2 milk line out: 679.7 g, Whole milk `250.4 g of 250.4 g · 36.8% in all`, Sucrose `12 g of 76.0 g · 11.2% in all` and `64 g of 76.0 g · 11.2% in all`), and a batch's as-made value stays on the line it was typed against by stored position.

## Accomplishments

- Task 1 (tracer): `validatePortion` accepts an absent or boolean `removed` and refuses `'true'`, `1` and `null` with the path named (`$.versions[0].rows[0].portions[0].removed`, `$.batches[0].snapshot.rows[0].portions[0].removed`). A bad flag writes nothing; a line out survives `exportStore` then `importStore`. `RecipePage` hands the table `version.rows` and `version.method` in every state. `groupPortionsByStep` takes `keepOutLines` and skips a line that is out before any group is made for it, so no empty step head prints; the pen and Show changes pass true. The reading portion line reads `formatPortionLine(portion.grams, rowGrams(liveRow), baselineMass)` with `liveRow` from `baselineActiveById`. The perStep test presses remove, saves as "less milk", lands on the saved child and holds the measured figures, one Whole milk row, no remove or restore button, and a Step 2 head.
- Task 2: `activeRows` tags each kept portion of a row it had to copy with its stored `index` (a row with every line in is still the same object, untagged). `asMadeTotals` reads at `portion.index ?? i`. The as-made total with `['', '250']` against the version with the Step 2 line out is 679.3 g (plan 679.7 g); a value stored at the out line's position (`['120', '']`) is never read against the line still in. The recording field for Whole milk's Step 3 line holds '250', is named "Whole milk, as made, grams, portion 2" and calls `onChangeAsMade('row-01', 1, '260')`; a saved batch's value at index 1 reads on that line.
