<decisions_recorded>
1. **The step's removal is read, never stored.** `isLineRemoved(row, portion, method)` gains the optional third argument: when it is given, a line is also out when `method` holds a step whose `n` equals `portion.step` and whose `removed` is `=== true`. `isRowRemoved(row, method)` gains the same argument. Called without it, both read only the line's own state, which is how the pen's seed and dirty check read it (a step's removal is derived and must not be seeded into a line). `activeRows(version)` passes `version.method ?? []`.
2. **A row whose every line is out through its step is a removed row.** That is why the three gum rows lose their orphan flags and the removed step's cue stops naming them, with no change to the flag texts.
3. **Groups.** `groupPortionsByStep(rows, steps, stepNumberMap, keepOutLines)` resolves each portion's step in `steps` by `n`. A removed step's lines form one group of kind 'removed', headed 'Removed' with the step's lead-in, sorted at the step's own place: after every active step that precedes it in the method and before the next active one, two removed steps in method order. A step with a display number forms a numbered group as before. A portion whose step is in neither (not in `steps`, or no number) forms the 'Unallocated' group, last. A line that is out is skipped when `keepOutLines` is false. The lone-group rule (no head for a lone group) applies to 'Unallocated' only; a lone 'Removed' group keeps its head, because it says what the struck lines are.
4. **The line under a removed step carries no control**, but its grams field is still present, as a removed row's has always been (the board keeps it): a removed step's restore brings the lines back with whatever was typed.
5. **The orphan flag prints on the first line still in**, now meaning: not struck by the maker's own flag and not under a removed step.
6. **Opening figures for a line that is out through its step** are read exactly as for one out by the maker (plan 01 decision 6 and plan 04 decision 3): the row's lines in when the pen opened, over the opening batch.
7. **Tests whose premise was that a removed step's lines stay in the recipe are rewritten to the new premise, keeping their intent.** Known ones: in uses.test.js, 'partitions a removed step's rows with orphanedRows' (the gums are now in a third bucket, out), 'returns the lecithin row when steps 1 and 8 are both removed' and "returns an empty coverage answer when both step 1 and step 8 are removed, and both of step 1's rows appear in orphanedRows" (the lecithin and olive oil rows have their only line in step 8, so removing step 8 takes them out, and they are neither covered nor orphaned); in Method.test.jsx, the fixture `makeRow` puts every row's line in step 1, so a case that removes step 1 and expects a coverage cue or a 'still used by' line (search 'still used by') needs its covered row's line moved to the step that covers it. Fix by moving the fixture's step, never by editing an assertion's words or Method.jsx.
8. **Measured numbers (Olive Oil v1, step n=2 'Gum slurry — the only high-heat step' removed):** total 666.0; heads 'Removed Gum slurry — the only high-heat step', 'Step 2 Build the base', 'Step 5 Allulose in, then crash-cool', 'Step 7 Emulsify the oil, cold'; Whole milk '250.4 g of 250.4 g · 37.6% in all' with share 31.3% then 37.6%; Sucrose '64 g of 64.0 g · 9.6% in all' with 8.0% then 9.6%; struck lines '120 g of 370.4 g · 46.3% in all' (share 15.0%) and '12 g of 76.0 g · 9.5% in all' (share 1.5%); the gums' shares 0.1%, 0.1% and trace, struck.
