## Findings (all in the record)

- **Engines.** WebKit and Chrome each match their own board on every compared line. They differ from each other only in the table's row metrics: the Sheet is 18.9px shorter in Chrome (2309.25 against 2328.14), on the boards as in the app.
- **1024 (no board).** In both engines the section stands 32px below the band and 32px above the Ingredients, the side column's top is level with it (diff 0) and `scrollWidth` is 1024 in a 1024 window. At 393 every case's `scrollWidth` is 393.
- **'Notes, no steps' (OPEN, constructed board panel).** The section's place and insides and the Instructions' absence match; only the table and what sits below differ, by a cause that is measured: the board's constructed panel keeps four step heads over 20 rows (the generator empties the step list in the markup only), the app draws a flat table of 16 rows for a version with no steps; the 15px at 1366 is the Watch for sentence that loses 'Step 2 targets 85 °C' with no step. Recorded as open item 1.
- **Whole route in print (found, not fixed).** On the live route Chrome splits Instructions step 1 across pages 2 and 3 although `.method-step` computes `break-inside: avoid`; with `.recipe-page` as `display: block` in print it stays whole. So plan 02's step rule holds for the Sheet as the board lays it (P1-K1) and not yet for the whole route: the page's print grid is Phase 4's print route. Row 2 for Mark says not to expect it.
