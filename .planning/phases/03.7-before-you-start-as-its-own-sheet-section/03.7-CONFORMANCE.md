## How it was measured

- The build was written on 2026-10-06 from the tree at plan 02's last commit (`f730c6a`; no `app/src` file changed in this plan) to a scratch `--outDir`, so Mark's `vite preview` on :4173, which serves `app/dist`, was never disturbed and `app/dist` was never written. The 03.5 harness served it on an ephemeral 127.0.0.1 port, with the boards on a second one; nothing contacted :4173 or the :8077 sketch server, and every request off 127.0.0.1 was aborted.
- Two engines, both coarse pointer: Playwright WebKit (the iPad's engine, the one the boards were captured in) and system Chrome. Widths 1366 and 393, plus a 1024 reading of Olive Oil v1 with no board. Every case is a fresh context on the version's own route; a pen case presses 'Next version' and waits for the pen's table. No DOM edit anywhere.
- The expectation is read live from the board's own panel, never from prose or from `bysb-measure.json`. Each board is opened coarse at its own `$preview` width through the harness's `openBoard`, once per engine, and a panel is read by its class (`.fp-<pid>-<W>`). The app and the panel are read by the same function: every position is relative to the article's own top-left (the page), and the section's insides and the pen's fields are relative to the section. Never an absolute y: the boards' page sits at 403 at 1366, 8px higher than the boards of 2026-10-04.
- Placement (decisions_recorded 2 and 4), compared on every 'top' panel: the gap from the band (`.recipe-band`) to the section (to the Ingredients where no section is drawn), the section to the Ingredients, the section's x, the Ingredients' x, the side column's top minus the first section's top (0 at 1366), and, below the Ingredients head, the Ingredients' and the table's height, the Instructions' top, the side column's top and the Sheet's height.
- Insides (decision 3): the section's width and height, its four border widths and bottom padding (all `0px`), the `h2.region-name`'s computed font-family, size, weight, line-height, text-transform, letter-spacing, margins and colour, its height, the `ul.authored__notes` offset from the section's top and its height, each `li`'s height, the inherited-note marker's text, and in the pen each textarea's and remove control's box relative to the section. The Ingredients' own heading is read the same way and also compared with the section's heading inside the app itself (decision 36's H1: the section's heading reads in the Ingredients' type).
- Instructions (on the 'ins' panels): the region's height, its heading's computed type, the count and height of the steps, the first step's offset, and that no `.method__before` block is inside. 'present' compares which of the section, the Ingredients, the Instructions and the side column each side draws.
- Numbers match within 1px; computed strings are exact. A line is MATCH, DIFF, or OPEN (a known difference whose cause is measured and written below).
- App-only checks: the DOM order band, section, Ingredients (and no section where none is drawn); at 1366 the side column's top level with the section's within 1px; in the pen, the focus order (below); every button in the section carries tabindex `0`; `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` at 393 and at 1024.
- Constructed cases (no seeded version): 'Notes, no steps' (Olive Oil v1 with its steps removed), 'Inherited-note marker' (its first note carries `inheritedFrom`, reading and pen). The probe copies the seeded Olive Oil v1 (and its batch, because the boards' panels are the built page with its As made column) into the page's own IndexedDB under a new id, then opens that route. Plan 01's jsdom file `RecipePage.beforeYouStart.test.jsx` covers the same states without a browser.
