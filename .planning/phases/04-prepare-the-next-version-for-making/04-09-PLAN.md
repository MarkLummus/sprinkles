## Phase Goal

Maker can print the new version as a bench sheet, carry it to the kitchen, and match the printed sheet back to its version — with the whole develop loop operable by keyboard and recoverable.

(ROADMAP Goal, verbatim; not in "As a / I want to / so that" form and not invented here.)

<objective>
UX1-02: entered data survives a recoverable failure. Every pen's draft is written to 04-04's drafts store while it holds ink and restored when the page opens again, with the approved notice; the leave warning retires; Home and the band name a draft that is waiting (D-14 to D-18; sketch 011 decision 19's deferred band line).

Purpose: one pen at a time means a forgotten draft keeps every other opener disabled (D-18); the maker must find it and finish or cancel it.
Output: draft.js, the persistence wiring in RecipePage, Home's draft line and lead, the band's draft line and History node, a reload probe.

Flagged assumption (spec-less edge probe, unclassified, not auto-resolved): **UX1-02** — the probe could not classify it. Assumption carried: "recoverable failure" covers a reload, a closed tab, a crash and the next day on the same device and browser profile; clearing site data or another browser is not recoverable and is out of scope. A keystroke typed within the last 250 ms before a hard kill may be lost (writes on pagehide are best effort).
</objective>
