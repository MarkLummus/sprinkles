## D-08 wiring (who owns what)

| Hook | Owns | Receives | Does not own |
|------|------|----------|--------------|
| `useDraftWriter(versionId)` | the three draft refs, `flushPendingDraft`, `discardDraft`; no effects | `versionId` (always defined, from `useParams`) | anything about pens |
| `useVersionPen({...})` | `usePenDraft()`, `versionSaveAction`, `versionSaveLockRef`, `penOpenedAtRef`, start, cancel, the eleven edits, both saves, `restorePlan` | `discardDraft`, `announce`, `onPageStatus`, `setMode`, `setVersion`, `setVersions`, `navigate`, and the records | any draft IO |
| `useKeptDraft({...})` | `draftRestoreDone`, `draftRestoreStartedRef`, `modeRef`, the restore, persist and pagehide effects | `writer`, `onRestorePlan`, `onRestoreRecord`, `onPageStatus`, `navigate`, the pen values, the records | pen state, locks |

Call order in the shell, every call above `if (loadFailed) return`: `useAnnouncer()` → `useRecipeData(versionId)` → `mode` state → `useRecordDraft()` and the batch-side state → `useDraftWriter(versionId)` → `useVersionPen({...})` → title effect → `useKeptDraft({...})` → the route-intent effects (cut 7, next plan) → Escape and Import-guard effects → width and fold hooks. The writer has no effects, so the effect order (loads, title, restore, persist, pagehide, intents, Escape, Import guard) is preserved, and on unmount persist's cleanup still runs before pagehide's flush.
