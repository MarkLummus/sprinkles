Why the payload holds steps and portions now (addresses review concern: the creation-draft contract was incomplete; reconciles criterion 2 with D-18). A payload without method and per-row portions would force Phase 11 to add keys to the create kind. So the payload carries them now, in shapes the app already stores: a step as the plan draft's method step (fitsMethodStep in draft.js), a row's portions as the version row's { step, grams } with grams as typed. Phase 11 then fills fields that exist.
