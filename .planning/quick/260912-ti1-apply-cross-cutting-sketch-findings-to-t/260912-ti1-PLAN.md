<objective>
Apply the cross-cutting type, spacing, and feedback rules from the sketch-findings-sprinkles
skill to the real app: the validated type roles mapped onto tokens.css, the 6px
caption-to-content gap everywhere a caption and its content share one label, 44px touch

DESIGN.md's own typefaces — Georgia text face and the system grotesk; only sizes, weights,
spacing, and the responsive targets transfer.

read with the same type discipline and the same touch ergonomics on a phone.
Output: role and touch tokens in tokens.css, the mapped rules in app.css including the app's
first @media block, an at-rule-aware stylesheet reader, and a third style-contract suite
asserting the whole finding as text.
