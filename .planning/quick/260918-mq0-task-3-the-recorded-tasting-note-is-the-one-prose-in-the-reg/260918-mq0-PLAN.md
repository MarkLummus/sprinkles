<objective>
The recorded tasting note is the one prose paragraph in the History register that is not set to the prose role. `DESIGN.md`/`route-recipe-version.md` "History type roles" (revised 2026-09-18) puts Why, the tasting note and Next time all in the shared serif prose role — "a nested batch ... its authored outcome remains full prose" — but only two of the three declare it. Why and Next time compute 16px/24; the tasting note computes 15px/22.5 because `.prose-text` deliberately carries no size of its own and the paragraph inherits the page's table-body default.

Purpose: one role, one size, across the three prose paragraphs of the register.
Output: one declaration in `app/src/styles/history.css`, and one style-contract test pinning all three sizes plus the small-print exclusion.
</objective>
