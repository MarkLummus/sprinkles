<objective>
Two boxes in the App context size themselves content-box while their own declarations assume border-box. The bottom tab row's tab (`.shell__tabs .shell__place`) takes `height: 100%` of a bar pinned to `--app-size-tab-h` and then adds `padding: var(--gap-xs) 0` outside that height, so each tab is taller than the bar it sits in. The home action (`.home__action`) declares `min-height: var(--touch-min)` and then adds `padding: var(--gap-s) var(--gap-m)` and a `var(--app-rule-row)` border outside it, so the rendered target is taller than the 44px minimum it names — and below 760px its `flex: 1 1 0` share is computed without that padding and border.

Purpose: make each box measure what its own declarations already claim to measure.
Output: two `box-sizing: border-box;` declarations — one per stylesheet — each pinned by an assertion added to the style-contract test that already covers its rule.
</objective>
