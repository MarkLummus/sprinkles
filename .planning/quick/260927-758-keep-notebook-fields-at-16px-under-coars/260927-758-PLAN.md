<objective>
Keep the recipe route's notebook fields at 16px or larger under a coarse pointer, so iOS Safari stops zooming the page when one of them takes focus.

**The defect.** On Mark's iPhone (the build served by `vite preview --host` on :4173), tapping Rename or Next version and then Cancel leaves the page wider than the screen, with sideways scrolling. Headless Chrome at 393x852 with a coarse pointer found no layout overflow: documentElement.scrollWidth stayed 393 throughout. What it did find is that every focused field computes under 16px, which is the threshold below which iOS Safari zooms the page on focus.

**The cause.** app.css's touch floor, `@media (pointer: coarse) { .ink-field, .prose-field { font-size: var(--sheet-type-note) } }` (app.css ~line 2392, whose comment names the iOS threshold), is a single class. notebook.css's base field rule, `.notebook-field .ink-field, .notebook-field input, .notebook-field textarea { font-size: var(--app-notebook-size-body) }` (notebook.css lines 158-170), outranks it at two classes (or a class and an element). This has been there since 715de20 (2026-09-25).

**The fix, part 1: the fields.** One `(pointer: coarse)` rule in notebook.css, among the file's last blocks. It uses the base rule's own three selectors and reads the token the touch floor already reads, so source order alone decides and the zoom threshold stays one value across the app. The fine-pointer look of Recipe name, Recipe description, Version name and Cite a batch does not change.

**The fix, part 2: the Why.** The same specificity collision keeps the Why out of the hand. `.notebook-ceremony__why` (notebook.css line 277, one class) loses font-family, font-size and color to `.notebook-field textarea` (one class plus one element). So the Why renders as grotesk at 15px in app text colour at every pointer, where 1600-pen.html draws Caveat at 22px, leading 1.25, in pen blue. Scoping the rule to `.notebook-field .notebook-ceremony__why` (two classes) outranks both the base rule and the new coarse rule. The Why then reads its own hand tokens at every pointer, and 22px also clears the 16px zoom threshold. The Why also gets the forced-colours and print fallback that every hand rule in app.css carries.

## Decisions

- **D-01** (Mark, 2026-09-27, answering the planner's question about the Why: "Fix it here"). This quick task makes the Why render in the hand at every pointer, as 1600-pen.html draws it: Caveat at --size-hand (22px), --leading-hand, sheet pen blue, tokens only. This changes the Why's fine-pointer look, which is intended.
- **D-02** (orchestrator brief, 2026-09-27). Every other field keeps its fine-pointer look (15px grotesk). The coarse floor reads an existing token. No literal values anywhere in app/src.
- **D-03** (planner's call, from DESIGN.md's Hand role: "Under forced-colors: active and in print the role falls back to the text face in italic"). notebook.css has no forced-colours or print block of its own, so it has no analogue to check. It gets one, mirroring app.css's `.app-hand` and `.sheet-hand` fallbacks exactly: the text face, italic, no colour declaration. Each block is its own top-level @media block and a named GUARD-05 step. They live in notebook.css, not app.css: app.css's print block is pinned at exactly three rules (cross-cutting.test.js line 714), and the recipe route's App rules live in notebook.css.

Purpose: the Rename and Next version forms (REC1-02, REC1-04) stay usable on a phone and on the iPad without the page jumping under the maker's thumb, and the maker's Why is written in the maker's hand.

Output: the notebook.css rules, their notebook.test.js pins, a re-runnable probe under this quick directory, and a rebuilt app/dist for Mark's iPhone UAT.
