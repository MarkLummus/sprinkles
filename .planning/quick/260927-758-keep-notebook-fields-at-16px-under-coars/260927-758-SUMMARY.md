# Quick Task 260927-758: Keep notebook fields at 16px under a coarse pointer, put the Why in the hand

**Fixed a specificity collision that kept Rename and Next version's fields at 15px under a coarse pointer (triggering iOS Safari's focus zoom), and put the Why textarea in Caveat pen-blue hand at every pointer, with forced-colours/print fallbacks.**

- **Duration:** ~15 min
- **Tasks:** 3 completed
- **Files modified:** 2 (notebook.css, notebook.test.js); 1 created (the probe)

## Accomplishments

- Added one `@media (pointer: coarse)` rule to `notebook.css` reusing the base field rule's own three selectors and app.css's `--sheet-type-note` token, so Rename's and Next version's text fields now compute 16px under a coarse pointer (was 15px) — the same specificity-collision root cause app.css's touch floor already solves for every other route, now applied here too.
- Rescoped the Why textarea's selector from the bare `.notebook-ceremony__why` to `.notebook-field .notebook-ceremony__why` (two classes), so it outranks `.notebook-field textarea` and finally reads its own five declarations: the Why now renders Caveat at 22px, leading 27.5px, in pen blue (`rgb(31, 61, 122)`) at every pointer (D-01), which also clears the 16px zoom threshold on its own.
- Gave the Why its own forced-colours and print fallbacks (text face, italic, no colour), mirroring app.css's `.app-hand`/`.sheet-hand` shape (D-03), since notebook.css had no analogue and the Why does not carry `.app-hand`.
- Built a re-runnable, real-render probe covering all three flows (Rename, Next version with one citable batch, Next version with two+ batches) across three pointer/width modes (393 coarse, 1366 coarse, 1366 fine) plus the two fallback media states — 70 checks pass against the rebuilt app.
