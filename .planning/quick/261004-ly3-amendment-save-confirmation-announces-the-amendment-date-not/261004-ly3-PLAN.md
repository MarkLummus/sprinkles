<objective>
When the maker saves a correction to a batch, the page confirmation names the date of that correction, not the date the batch was first recorded.

Purpose: `batchSavedStatus(record)` always prints `record.recordedAt`. completeRecord keeps `recordedAt` and stamps `changed: now`, so amending an older batch announces an event from weeks earlier and never confirms the amendment saved. The batch surface brief (.impeccable/surfaces/route-recipe-batch.md lines 70 and 129) and 02-CONTEXT.md line 128 say "an amendment states its date the same way". The read view already prints CHANGED as its own row. This plan makes the confirmation say "changed <date> against <version>" for an amendment and keeps "recorded …" for a first save.

Output: the failing test first, then the one-function fix in app/src/ui/RecipePage.jsx, the todo closed, and a SUMMARY.
</objective>
