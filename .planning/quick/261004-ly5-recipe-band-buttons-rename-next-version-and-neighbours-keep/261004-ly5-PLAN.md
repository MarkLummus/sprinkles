<discretion>
Planner's choice, recorded so nobody re-decides it silently.

The chosen fix keeps hover's weight on the two actions: border 2px, padding 1px less on each side. It does not flatten hover to the resting box.

Why:
- It is what Mark sees today minus the width change. The outline Cancel and Save over already thicken to 2px on hover, and the filled action's 2px border is in its own fill colour, so it stays invisible.
- It is DESIGN.md's written Hover-Is-Weight Rule.
- Flattening (restate rest padding and border-width) would remove a visible hover cue from the outline action. DESIGN.md line 268 lists App hover states as unresolved and Impeccable's to decide.

`.notebook-link` has no border to thicken. Its hover restates padding 0 and adds no new look, the way `.text-control:hover` does.

Reversibility: reversible. It is one rule. Flattening later means swapping two declarations.
