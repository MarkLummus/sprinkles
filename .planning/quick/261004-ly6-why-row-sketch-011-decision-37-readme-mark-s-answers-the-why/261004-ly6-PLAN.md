<objective>
Build sketch 011 decision 37 as Mark answered it on 2026-10-04:
- Answer 1, option B: the Why value sits on its own line, flush with its label.
- Answer 2: the saved Why shows in the hand, as decision 17 and DESIGN.md's Hand entry say. The build changes to match.

Authority: the `B` panels of `.planning/sketches/011-recipe-route-c/why-row.html`, including its CSS. The spec is the decision 37 entry in `.planning/sketches/011-recipe-route-c/README.md`: what the app draws today, the app change brief, and Mark's answers. The board's generator is `.planning/canvas-generators/why.py` (lines 23-32 hold the B and hand CSS). Read all of these; never edit them.

The cause, as measured by Sid in WebKit and Chromium:
- The Why value starts 40px right of its label. That is the browser's default start margin on a `dd`. The lineage rule resets that margin on the other values, but not on `.version-row__reason`.
- A saved Why is set in the text face (`prose-text`, Georgia 16px), not the hand.

Planner's choice: this builds the same result by a simpler route than the brief, stated here so nobody re-decides it silently.
- What the brief says: add `margin-inline-start: 0` to `.version-row__reason`, and add a second rule, `.version-row__reason.app-hand`, so the hand's size beats `.version-row__reason`'s own `font-size: var(--sheet-type-note)`.
- Why that is more than needed: once the saved dd takes `.app-hand`, that `font-size` reaches no rendered state. The saved Why would be overridden by the new rule, and the empty value is already overridden by `.version-row__reason--empty`, which comes later and ties on specificity. So it becomes dead code.
- What this plan does instead: it removes that one declaration (CLAUDE.md: remove what your change makes unused). The `.app-hand` role's own size and leading then reach the saved Why, and no override rule is needed.
- Both routes render identically. The probe compares the computed face, size, leading, colour and height against the board's B panels, which were drawn with the brief's override.
- If the orchestrator wants the brief's literal mechanism, swap in the override rule. Nothing else in this plan changes.

The same rule also keeps `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, the one thing the saved Why loses when `prose-text` leaves it. Without it, a long unbroken word (a pasted link, say) would push the 393 column sideways. The sketch findings say "393px must not overflow". This declaration draws nothing different on any board panel, and the probe's stress check proves it.

Purpose: Mark asked on 2026-09-27 to look into the Why row. It was the only row in the version details with a stray 40px indent, and the only saved maker's words in the App not written in the hand.

- one test changed and one test added, both RED first
