## Why the toggle and not a summary onClick

The toggle event is the one place `moreOpen` becomes true. A tap, and Enter or Space on the summary, all close the panel through one handler, and nothing has to guess the next state before the browser toggles. The `importErrorsShowing` guard means opening More with no panel sets no extra state (Test C).

## Focus rule

Reused from Close, not new: `closeImportErrors` moves focus only when it was inside the panel, and then to More's summary below 724. With focus elsewhere it is left alone. Tests A and B pin both halves.
