<objective>
Fix WR-01 from `.planning/phases/03.6-per-step-shared-ingredients/03.6-REVIEW.md`: `buildRowDiff` in `app/src/domain/diff.js` computes the row-level `gramsFrom`, `gramsTo`, `gramsChanged`, `shareFrom` and `shareTo` from every stored portion, but the masses those shares divide by (`activeMass`) and the per-line `lines` array already honour line removal. For a split row the two layers disagree. Taking one line out leaves `gramsChanged` false, so `stepsWithStaleAmounts` (`app/src/domain/uses.js`) never flags the steps that use the row. Editing a line that is already out sets `gramsChanged` true and raises a flag for a change with no effect on the batch.

Purpose: the stale-amount flag must say what the batch says. The row-level figures read the lines still in, on each side, through the predicate `lines` uses.

Output: one local helper and its use in `buildRowDiff` (both branches), regression tests in `diff.test.js` (the named test: a split row with one line taken out gives `gramsChanged === true`) and two consumer-level tests in `uses.test.js`. No other file changes. Out of scope: IN-01 and IN-02 of the same review, and the sibling quick items WR-02 to WR-04 (UI and lineage files).
</objective>
