<objective>
Fix the Show changes half of WR-03 from `.planning/phases/03.6-per-step-shared-ingredients/03.6-REVIEW.md`. In `renderShowChangesEntry` (`app/src/ui/IngredientTable.jsx`) a line that is out prints `formatPortionLine(portion.grams, rowGrams(openingRow), openingMass)` where `openingRow` is the parent's row of lines still in, or, when the parent's active map has no such row, the child's stored row. When the line was also out in the parent (`removedChanged` false), the parent's lines-still-in row does not contain it, so the sub-line reads the line's grams "of" a total that excludes them. When every line of the row was out in the parent, the lookup misses, the stored row (all lines) is divided by a parent mass that excludes it, and the sub-line reads 86.3% on Olive Oil's Whole milk.

State of play: the pen half of WR-03 (and WR-02's pen label) already shipped on 2026-10-05 in commits 50f91bc and f2d6b4d, with tests H1 to H6 in `IngredientTable.test.jsx`. The pen reads a line that was out at open from the parent through `rowHoldingLine` and prints no sub-line when neither version held it. This plan fixes the one place that still takes the old path. Reproduced against the current tree (throwaway probe, deleted): Olive Oil parent and child both with milk's Step 2 line out print "120 g of 250.4 g · 36.8% in all"; both milk lines out in both print "120 g of 370.4 g · 86.3% in all" and "250.4 g of 370.4 g · 86.3% in all".

Rule (the review's fix, the task text): print a portion sub-line only when the line was in at open; otherwise print the bare amount. "In at open" is answered by the opening version's own lines-still-in map at the line's stored position, through the existing `rowHoldingLine`. That is the same fact as `rowDiff.lines[i].removedChanged` being true for a line that is out now, except for a row or position the opening version does not have at all (a row new in the child), where `removedChanged` is true but the opening version never held the line. The positional check gets that case right and `removedChanged` alone does not, so it is the discriminator. `removedChanged` stays unread by the table (IN-02 is out of scope).

Purpose: a struck line in Show changes never reads a share or a total of a batch that did not contain it.
Output: a small edit in `renderShowChangesEntry`, and three regression tests that fail before it.
