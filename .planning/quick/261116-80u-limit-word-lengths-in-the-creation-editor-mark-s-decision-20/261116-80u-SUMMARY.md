## For Mark: the 768 Right in the middle shortfall (for a decide row)

With two 12-character end words, a Right in the middle scale at 768 leaves **4.28 px** between words (planning estimated 4.25), under the **6 px** of 12-UI-REVIEW finding 1. The words do not touch, and the probe holds that case to 1 px with a REPORT line saying so. Mark's 12-character end limit cannot give 6 px at a 186 px track. Choices: accept it, give a Right in the middle scale a lower end-word limit, or widen the track. Finding 1 is therefore closed at 1366 and 393 for both kinds and at 768 for Low to high, not for that one case. Finding 2 (print fit, rows, low word clear of the name) is closed on the measures above.
