# Sketch 001: The pushed tray

## Design Question
The 2026-09-09 critique and the JTBD mapping found the "imprint" band overloaded: four openers at two hierarchy levels, a version list and a batch list in different regions, ceremonies for three pens. Mark's proposal: a tray at the right edge, opened from bookcloth section tabs, that holds acts and lists and pushes the spread rather than covering it. The page keeps reading and in-place ink. The question this sketch answers is the width cost at 1440: does the table keep its unwrapped names and does the formulation note stay in view while a ceremony is open?

## How to View

Escape closes an untouched tray or pen; Save adds the child to the version list.

## Variants
- **A: Full push, 360px** — the tray is a third grid column at 360px; page padding and gutters stay at the brief's values; the 2fr/1fr spread absorbs the whole cost.
- **B: Narrow push, 300px, tight gutters** — the tray is 300px, page padding drops from 48 to 20 and the gutter from 32 to 20, column two is pinned at 340px (the rule's native 320 plus air) so column one keeps the table's width.
- **C: Tray in column two** — no third column; the tray sits at the top of column two above the formulation note, opened from a horizontal tab row. This is the 8 Sep "swap" revived as a tray; the zero-cost baseline the pushed variants must beat.

## Outcome (2026-09-09)
Mark chose **C**: no third column. A and B were rejected outright: a three-column shell does not survive a tablet as the primary device, and A shrinks the rule drawing and wraps names at 1440. Refinements requested, carried into sketch 002: the tabs and openers at the top of the page beside the headnote rather than level with Ingredients; the same push-down mechanics for column two; a look at many versions and many batches; the batch record possibly pulled out of the margin and up into the tray; the duplicated recipe name in the eyebrow removed.

## What to Look For
- In A and B with the tray open: does any ingredient name wrap? Does the rule drawing shrink below 320px? The readout says.
- In the Develop state: are the version line, reason and Save visible at the same time as the fat rule's strike (18.0% to 18.8%) and the table's struck 40?
- In C: does the formulation note push below the fold when the Develop ceremony is open? Is a tray that is not at the edge still a tray?
- Reading state: is the spread with the tab strip closed still the page the critique scored a 4?
