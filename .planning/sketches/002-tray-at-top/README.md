# Sketch 002: The tray at the top

## Design Question
Refinement of sketch 001's winner (C). The tabs and openers move to the top of the page beside the headnote; opening a panel pushes column two down and never touches column one. Three placements for the batch record are compared, the version list carries four versions and three batches so the shape of a month's work is visible, and the page stacks to one column below 1100px with the tray first, for a tablet as the primary device.

## How to View
open .planning/sketches/002-tray-at-top/index.html

Variants A, B, C in the dark bar; states Reading, Versions open, Batches open, Develop open. Viewport buttons 834 and 1024 show the tablet stack. The readout at the bottom right gives column widths and the y position of the table, the balance note, the fat rule and the batch record.

## Variants
- **A: Record stays in the margin** — the tray holds versions with their batches nested, and the openers; the margin is unchanged from today. Amend and Add tasting sit beside each batch in the tree.
- **B: Record moves up into a Batches panel** — the tray gains a Batches tab; the margin disappears; the as-made column stays in the table as in-place ink. Amend and Add tasting live in the panel.
- **C: One-line record in the margin, detail in the panel** — the margin keeps two sentences (churn and tasting) and a "whole record" control; the Batches panel holds the full record.

- **Synthesis D: the tip and its record as front matter** (added after Mark's checkpoint notes, 2026-09-09) — the page holds the latest version and its latest batch. Beside the headnote, two compact columns: the version's metadata (lineage, reason, Develop, "3 later versions") and the batch's record (come-up, draw, overrun, words, tasting marks, Record another, Amend, Add tasting, "1 later"). The block is sized to the headnote so Balance stays level with Ingredients; the earlier-version list, the earlier-batch list and the Develop ceremony open beneath it and push Balance down. No tabs, no margin record.

## Outcome (2026-09-09)
No winner. A, B and C kept a tray with tabs; Mark's notes redirected: follow the tip (latest version, latest batch), keep Balance level with Ingredients, treat the batch record as page metadata. Synthesis D tried that inside column two and was rejected as two tiny sub-columns. Sketch 003 restarts with outer rows in column one.

## What to Look For
- Reading state: with the tray closed, the balance note now sits beside the headnote rather than beside the table. Is that better or worse than today?
- Versions open: does the tree read with four versions at three depths? Where is your eye when looking for "the one I churned last"?
- Develop open: the ceremony pushes the balance note down; the readout says by how much. Is the fat rule still on the first screen?
- B: reading the table's as-made column with no record beside it. Do you miss "soft, not greasy" next to the figures?
- C: is the two-sentence summary the record, or a teaser?
- 1024 and 834: the tray first, then headnote, table, balance, record, method. Does the tab row work as a tablet's top bar?
