# Sketch 005: Folded tasting pen

## Design Question
Shaped 2026-09-10 with Mark: today's two-pen model (Record a batch, then a separate Add-a-tasting ceremony) collapses into one pen — the churn fields and the primary tasting's fields, one Save, no second ceremony. Mark's own priority for this sketch: the recording pen must not recreate the "260 controls" overload the 2026-09-08 whole-page critique found in the plan's editing pen. This sketch tests three disclosure strategies for the same field set (churn: date, come-up, draw temp, air, notes; tasting: date, temperature, 6 axis marks, melt test, words, next-time note).

## How to View

## Variants
- **A: Everything open** — the whole pen renders at once: churn fields, then a rule, then every tasting field, from the moment recording starts. The baseline to judge the other two against.
- **B: Tasting collapsed behind one toggle** — churn fields render first; "Add the tasting" is the only tasting-related thing visible until tapped, then the whole tasting section opens in place. Matches "the batch saves either way" (tasting stays optional).
- **C: Quick marks open, detail behind "More"** — the axis marks (the fastest part to fill in) show immediately after the churn fields; melt test, words, and next-time note sit behind a "More" toggle within the tasting section itself.

## What to Look For
- Which variant reads as one sitting rather than a stacked form? Does A already feel like too much before you've typed anything?
- In B, does collapsing the *entire* tasting section behind one toggle feel like it's hiding the point of the pen, or does it correctly mirror "the tasting is optional, the churn record isn't"?
- In C, is splitting the tasting itself into "quick marks" vs. "more detail" a sensible cut, or does it just relocate the bloat one level down?
- Toggle "as expected, nothing to note" — does the shortcut still read naturally sitting inline rather than in its own ceremony?
- With six axis rows always visible (four core plus two declared), does the marks section alone feel like the bulk of the bloat regardless of variant — a signal that the fix might be elsewhere (e.g., collapsing axes, not tastings)?
