Every control, every state

Three kinds of control. Joined boxes choose one from a set (a segmented control, the five-cell axis): the picked box fills pen blue. A square and a word record an independent yes or no (a checkbox, a defect, Show changes): the small square fills pen blue. A lone hairline box or an underlined word is an action; nothing fills.

A joined group is a radio: picking is final, and Clear appears in the caption line once something is picked, so an unrecorded answer is never mistaken for a middle one. Hover thickens a border to 2px in place; nothing moves. Focus is one 2px ring offset 2px around the whole control, and it means focus only. Invalid is a sentence beneath the field, never a ring. In high contrast, selection stays a fill in the system's Highlight colour. Measured fields take their width tokens, not the column.

Below 760px

Interactive targets grow to 44px: buttons, text controls, segment options, defects, the checkbox label and fields to a 44px minimum height; the axis to five joined 44×44 cells on a 216px track. Nothing else changes. At the 393 preset the whole sheet already takes these sizes; this block shows them at any width.

Settled values

Every value the sources disagreed on, decided 2026-09-14 and drawn above. The rule of thumb: small control text, comfortably sized targets, larger text for entering values and observations.

  • Pen blue for every on state. Blue fill = this choice is on; outer ring = keyboard focus; shape and grouping = how the choice behaves; label and place = what it affects.
  • 0.04em tracking on every uppercase caption, labels and region names alike; none on sentence-case text. Field captions and axis names share 0.75rem at weight 500; 600 is reserved for region names.
  • 6px beneath a region name; the larger gap above it; a helper keeps the 6px and takes its own gap before the controls.
  • Measured fields 128px (date) and 56px (numeric) through named tokens. 48px clipped "−12.5" and "120.5"; 56px holds both at 15px and 16px; "2026-08-02" fits the 128px date field in Chrome.
  • Text controls 0.8125rem (13px); segment options and defects the same. Target height and padding carry comfort, not larger type.
  • Prose field: hairline baseline, no thickening on focus; the ring is the focus signal.
  • Anchor words: text face, italic, short and consistent across axes.
  • Fields at 600px and below grow to 1rem.
  • Status line at the prose measure, 65ch.
  • Show changes 32px tall at desktop, 44px below 760px, as the checkbox and a defect.
  • Joined cells keep the 186px and 216px tracks: 38px cells at desktop, 44px below 760px.
  • Joined groups are radios: picking is final, Clear in the caption line is the way back, and the caption line reserves Clear's height so nothing moves.
