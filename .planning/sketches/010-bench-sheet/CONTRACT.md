## 1. Rules for every printed page (D7, D16, D20, D22)

- Letter portrait, 816 × 1056 px, white (`#ffffff`), ink only (`--sheet-ink`, #141414; one colour measured on the log pages and the method). Smallest type 12 px. No controls, no hover, no focus: paper. Chrome's print and WebKit's print both drop the Sheet's ground (#f7f7f4), because the app sets no `print-color-adjust`; the boards are white to match.
- Margins (D20): 0.75 in (72 px) at the left and the right, 0.5 in (48 px) above and below, on every page (Mark hole-punches the sheet; a duplex back page is punched on its right). `@page { size: letter; margin: 0.5in 0.75in }`. Content box 672 × 960 in Chrome and 630 × 900 in Safari (WebKit prints 1 CSS px as 0.8 pt). The print layout must not read the window's width: the live app's phone rules start below 724 px.
- Guard (D22): on the log pages the ink stops 2 px short of the content box's right edge (board padding `48px 74px 48px 72px`), because WebKit's print clips the date box's right border otherwise. The build keeps the 2 px.
- Foot in flow (D7): Safari cannot print a running foot or "Page n of m". Each log page carries its foot inside the page, and the Sheet carries one after its last step: real markup on the last page of the Sheet's real flow (`sheet-page-3-instructions-end.html`, section 5), in flow, not at the page's foot. No page n of m on the formula and method pages.
- Order on paper (D-07a): page 1, page 2 (one sheet, front and back), then the Sheet's pages: when the Ingredients table breaks (Olive Oil v1) the formula page, the table's tail with the Instructions beginning under it, and the rest of the Instructions (section 5, D29); when the table fits, the formula page, then the Instructions on their own pages (section 6, D-06b).
- The method treatment (D18; Mark, `decide-method-why-aside-italic`: upright) is one rule wherever the steps print: in the Sheet's Instructions (section 5) and on the method pages (section 6) every step carries the number column, the bold lead-in, the tag row (caps label, bold underlined value, no box), **Why** as the caps word "why", an em dash and the sentence (13 px, upright), and the **aside** as a callout with a 2.5 px ink rule at the left (13 px, upright). No printed step uses the build's italic Purpose and Aside, the unlabelled lines or the boxed chips. A Sheet that prints without this treatment does not conform.
- White space is part of the design: `.lf-body` ends at least 16 px above the foot rule (measured: page 1 content ends 833 px from the page top, the foot rule starts at 986; page 2 811 and 986).

## 2. Test rule: core and recipe-declared items (D25, D26)

The log pages, the pen and the reading mix items every recipe has with items only some recipes declare. A test must never fail a recipe because Olive Oil's items are missing.

1. **Core items are asserted for every recipe.**
2. **Declared slots are asserted only against the recipe's own declared list**, read from its version record, never from a board or a literal.
3. A recipe whose list is empty renders none: no declared scale, no declared problem and, in the pen, no "This recipe only" cue for that group (Mark: `decide-pen-declared-for-none-recipe`, hide both). The print has no cues (D23).
4. **Tests are parameterised by recipe.** They iterate the seed recipes (`app/src/data/seed-recipes.js`) and take the expected declared list from each version. "Body", "Oil" and "Bitter" appear as literals only in the Olive Oil case.

**Core** (every recipe, every board):
- Page 1: title Batch log, Churned ("Churn date" until 2026-10-25, `decide-p10-churn-trio`; the app's `PrintLogPages.jsx` reads the pen's caption), Out of machine, Duration ("Churn duration" until then), the At draw group (three boxes, Exit consistency, Airiness) and the Notes section with 14 lines.
- Page 2: title Tasting log, Tasted, Tempering, Temperature ("Tasting temperature" until 2026-10-25; one source, `battery.js`), Melt test, Melt style, the From freezer group (three boxes), the four core scales Hardness, Scoopability, Smoothness, Sweetness (`AXES` rows with group `core`, `app/src/domain/axes.js`, `CORE_AXIS_COUNT` 4), the four core problems Coarse, icy / Sandy, gritty / Gummy, elastic / Greasy film (`DEFECTS`, `battery.js`), and the Notes section with 5 lines. Both feet.
- The pen and the reading carry the same items, the scales and problems under the cue "Every recipe".
- The notes (At the machine, Ingredient notes, How did it turn out?, Next time) and Before you start are core: every recipe has the slot, only its words differ, so no test asserts Olive Oil's words.

**Declared** (only a recipe whose version lists it): two kinds, **0 to 2 of each** (D26, Mark: "anticipate 2 of each").
- Scales, 0 to 2: `version.declaredAxes`, names of `AXES` rows with group `declared` (Body and Oil today; Olive Oil v1 only, every other seed version has `[]`). A batch copies the list to `batch.snapshot.declaredAxes` at its first save; `axesForBatch(batch)` returns the core axes then the declared ones in that order; a new pen reads `version.declaredAxes`. Marks live in `batch.tasting.marks[axis.key]`.
- Problems, 0 to 2: today `version.declaredFlaw` (one word or null; Olive Oil v1: Bitter), copied to `batch.snapshot.declaredFlaw`, ticked as `batch.tasting.bitterDeclared`. The build row `build-declared-schema-two-each` makes it a list of up to two words.
- No third kind exists; the axes are fixed (`axes.js`: no free-form declared axis).

**Expected counts** (print page 2, per version):

The problems row holds up to six boxes on one line at Safari's 630 px (40 px short of the right edge with two declared) and at 672; seven would wrap at 630 (not a design).
