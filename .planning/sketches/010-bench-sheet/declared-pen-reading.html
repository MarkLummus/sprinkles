Notation: core and declared

Design-time marks in the gutter. They do not print and are not in the app.

core Every recipe has it. A test checks it for every recipe.

declared Only a recipe whose version lists it has it: up to two scales and up to two problems, so 0, 1 or 2 of each. A test checks it against that recipe's own list. A recipe that lists none shows none.

A declared option that sits inside a shared row (a problem).
