350 · the whole pen, both groups from head to end

Four states: opens blank; a pick in each group with a note written in each; a pick in one and a mark in the other; marks and notes in both. In the last two a field with a mark in its group stays, with both options off. The notes sit above the At draw field with one rule between them (the note's underline), and the melt fields and Next time above the From freezer field; the draw group ends at the Tasting head, the tasting group at the save buttons.
