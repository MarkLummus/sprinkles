Instructions

Before you start

  1. 1

    Lecithin into the oil. Whisk 1.2 g soy lecithin into the 40 g of Drizzle. Cover, leave at room temperature.

    temproom

    It's lipophilic — it disperses poorly if added to the water phase.

    ·

  2. 2

    Gum slurry — the only high-heat step. Toss 1.68 g of the gum blend with the sucrose. Whisk into the milk in a small saucepan. Heat, whisking constantly, then pull off.

    temp85 °Chold2 min

    At 120 g this is a thin layer in the pan. It scorches and evaporates faster than a larger volume would — keep whisking and don’t walk away.

    ·

  3. 3

    uses Whole milk, Heavy cream, Skim milk powder, Sucrose, Dextrose, Fine sea salt ··

  4. 4

    Divide. Tare, pour, check.

    per jar379 g

    Mismatched jars hit core temperature at different times and you would be timing off the wrong one.

    Two-piece lids finger-tight only — the headspace air expands and needs to vent rather than build pressure.

    ·

  5. 5

    Pasteurise in the circulator. Start the clock when the jar core reaches temperature.

    temp69 °Chold40 mincome-up10–12 min

    With 379 g in a squat wide-mouth jar the come-up is much faster than a single large vessel.

    Probe the core rather than trusting the estimate. Invert each jar once or twice partway through.

    ·

  6. 6

    Allulose in, then crash-cool. Combine both jars, stir in the 20 g allulose off the heat until dissolved, then straight into an ice bath.

    tobelow 5 °C

    Allulose skips the hold — it browns readily with milk proteins.

    ·

  7. 7

    Age. Hold covered in the fridge.

    temp4 °Ctime12–24 h

    ·

  8. 8

    Emulsify the oil, cold. Mix still at 4 °C. Pour the lecithin-oil blend in a thin stream under a running immersion blender.

    temp4 °Cblend45 s

    Never heat this oil.

    ·

  9. 9

    Churn. Freeze immediately.

    overrun25–30%

    ·

  10. 10

    Harden, and serve warm. Harden, then temper before serving.

    harden−20 °C, 4+ hserve−11 to −12 °C

    ·
