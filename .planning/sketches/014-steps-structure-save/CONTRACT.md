Read with the files themselves. Their markup and inlined CSS are the authority; this note names what they hold so a planner, a checker and UAT cite one source. Mark approved the four boards on 2026-10-25 (the look rows in `README.md`); the 2026-10-26 redraws (roadmap 24, decision 014 1) are drawn and await `look-p11-structure-r2` and `look-p11-place-row`; the third 2026-10-26 redraw (decision 014 2, the reading steps in the printed method's treatment, section 7) awaits `look-p11-screen-steps-print-treatment`; the fourth 2026-10-26 redraw (decision 014 3, each step orders its own rows, sections 2 and 3) awaits `look-p11-sketch-014-row-order-redraw`. ## 2. A step while creating (`instructions-steps.html`; MAKE-07)

- The Instructions section (`section.method-region`, aria-label Instructions) sits under the Balance; with no step it is the heading row alone and the table stays flat, Phase 10's, with each row's moves. Steps are `ol.method-steps > li.method-step.mk-step`: the number (`.method-step__n`, aria-hidden) at the left, the body (`.method-step__body`) beside it, number top and lead-in top equal (no `.method-step__open-fields` box, no first-field margin; 013 decision 32).
- The body, in order: the lead-in (`label.method-step__field > input.prose-field.prose-field--lead-in`, bold, aria-label "Step n, lead-in", 44 tall); the instruction (`textarea.prose-field`, 2 rows, "Step n, instruction"); the targets line (`p.method-step__targets`: one `.target-chip` per target holding two `input.ink-field` fields, label and value, then Add target 64 × 44 [61], then Preview step 77.5 × 44 [74.6], all `.text-control`); a purpose and an aside (`textarea.prose-field`, 2 rows) once added; the preview when open; the uses line; the Uses checklist when open; the acts row.
- Required (`span.field-requirement`) stands under an empty lead-in and an empty instruction of a just-added step. A step with Required standing refuses Save (section 4).
- The uses line (`p.method-step__uses-line`) reads "uses nothing yet" or "uses " and the row names in the step's own order (its `uses`, which is also the order of its rows in the table: decision 014 3), then "change the ingredients" (reads "done" while the checklist is open), then "· add a purpose" and "· add an aside" while the step has none. The checklist (`fieldset.method-step__uses`, legend Uses) lists every row of the table with a checkbox; ticked rows are the step's.
- Preview (013 decision 34): Preview step reads Hide preview while open; the preview (`div.mk-preview`, role group "Step n, preview") sits under the step's last content field and before the uses line: a cue "Preview" (`p.mk-preview__cue`, the checklist legend's face: `--sheet-type-label` 12 px, 500, uppercase, 0.04em) over the step as the Sheet shows it (a reading step, section 7: `.method-step__n`, `.method-step__lead` with the lead-in bold and a full stop, then the tag row, the why line and the aside callout, upright), set off by `--rule-graduation` solid `--sheet-ink` at the left, `--gap-s` above and as padding. 504 × 136.4 for step 1 with one target, a purpose and an aside (504 × 103 as the chip-and-italic form, before decision 014 2).
- The acts row (`p.method-step__acts`): "remove · Move up · Move down · Add step", each a `.text-control` 44 tall (44.7, 51.7, 69, 54.1 wide [43.1, 50.8, 68.1, 52.8]), the dots aria-hidden; aria-labels "Step n, remove / move up / move down / add a step after it". Add step adds a step right after this one; focus goes to the new step's lead-in. A move that cannot act carries `.mk-off` (`--app-text-secondary`, no underline, default cursor) and `aria-disabled="true"`.
- The table under steps: the build's step heads (`tr.ingredient-table__step-head`: "Step n" and the lead-in in `.ingredient-table__step-head-lead`) in step order, a step that uses nothing drawing no head; rows no step uses under a last head "Not in a step yet" (roadmap decision 25) as soon as a step exists. A row ticked in two steps splits into one line per step, each under its step's head with its own grams field and the build's portion line "400 g of 600 g · 58.0% in all" (roadmap decision 23); a split row with one line left reads as a one-line row with no portion line (sketch 011 decision 56). Each step's rows stand in that step's `uses` order (decision 014 3, `decide-p11-row-order-across-steps`), so a row in two steps stands in each step where that step's uses names it: on "A row in two steps" Whole milk, named last by step 2, is Step 2's last line, 200 g, "200 g of 600 g · 58.0% in all", its Move down off. Each row's name cell carries its acts on its own line (`.mk-row-acts`: remove · Move up · Move down; a row under "Not in a step yet" adds · Add to step, section 3).

## 3. Add, remove and move (`add-remove-move.html`; STRUCT-01, STRUCT-02, STRUCT-03)

- Steps: Move up and Move down reorder the list; the numbers on screen follow the order and the step's stored number never changes (STRUCT-03); the table's step groups follow the method. The first step's Move up and the last's Move down are `.mk-off`.
- Rows: Move up and Move down move a row within its step only (roadmap decision 9); the first row of a step has Move up off, the last Move down off. A move reorders that step's `uses`, so its uses line reads the new order; the other steps' rows and uses lines stay as they were (decision 014 3; "After Move down on Whole milk": "uses Skim milk powder, Whole milk, 3.5% fat, Sucrose", steps 2 and 3 untouched). Rows under "Not in a step yet" move within that group.
- remove on a step deletes it outright with no strike and no restore (roadmap decision 10): rows whose only line was in it keep their grams and go back under "Not in a step yet"; for a row split across two steps the grams fold back into its remaining line and the Balance stands (roadmap decision 24; drawn on the third panel since 2026-10-26). No removed step's key is handed out again (the roadmap's `nextStepKey`).
- remove on a row deletes it outright and prunes the uses that name it (013 decision 5, STRUCT-01); the Balance follows.
- Add to step (decision 014 1; `decide-p11-place-unplaced-row`, A): a row under "Not in a step yet" ends its acts line with a fourth word, "remove · Move up · Move down · Add to step" (`.text-control`, 69.9 × 44, aria-label "Add <row> to a step"), as soon as a step exists; a placed row has none. Pressed, the acts line becomes the chooser: Cancel first (41.6 × 44, aria-label "<row>, cancel"), then one control per step in order, "Step n, <lead-in>" ("Step n" alone while the lead-in is empty; aria-label "Add <row> to step n"), each 44 tall, the dots aria-hidden; the row stays where it is while the chooser is open. Choosing a step ticks the row in that step's Uses, moves the row under that step's head as its last row with its grams, names it on the step's uses line, and puts focus on the moved row's remove. Cancel restores the acts line with focus on Add to step. The chooser is drawn with two steps; with more it wraps within the name cell. The step's own checklist stays for a row in two steps or a move between steps.

## 6. Rules for every panel

- Every control is 44 px tall under the coarse pointer (`--touch-min`): fields, chips' fields, the add controls, the moves, Preview step, Cancel, Save, Edit. Nothing moves on hover or focus.
- No new token. The `mk-*` rules in `p11.py`'s `P11_CSS` read existing tokens only; the build keeps that.
- 1366 only, the iPad's width; no 393 panel (Mark does not create recipes on an iPhone).

## 7. A reading step on screen (decision 014 2; sketch 010 D18, its `CONTRACT.md` sections 5 and 6; `decide-method-why-aside-italic`: upright)

Wherever a board shows a step read rather than edited (`li.method-step` without `.mk-step`: the Preview step on `instructions-steps.html`, the after-Save page on `save.html`, both reading panels of `first-version.html`), the step reads as the Sheet prints it, on the screen classes at the Sheet's screen sizes:

- The number (`.method-step__n`) and the lead (`.method-step__lead`, the lead-in bold with a full stop, then the instruction) as built on screen.
- **Targets** `p.method-step__targets`: a row (flex, wrap, column gap `--gap-l`, row gap `--gap-xs`, baseline-aligned, `--gap-xs` above) of `span.target-chip` each holding `span.target-chip__label` (the caps label: `--sheet-type-label` 12 px, `--sheet-print-weight-cue` 500, uppercase, `--sheet-print-tracking-caps` 0.04em) and `span.target-chip__value` (`--sheet-type-section` 14 px, `--sheet-print-weight-title` 700, underlined `--rule-ink-field` 1 px at `--sheet-print-tag-underline-offset` 3 px), `--gap-xs` between, the chip's own border and padding gone. Measured: the row 17 tall; "temp" 35.3 wide, "85 °C" 38.8.
- **Why** `p.method-step__purpose`: `span.method-step__purpose-label` reading "why" (the same caps label face, grotesk), an em dash, the sentence; `--sheet-size-deviation-words` 13 px in the text face, upright (`font-style: normal`), leading `--sheet-leading-helper` 1.4, `--gap-xs` above, `--measure-prose`. - Order as built: lead, targets, purpose, aside; each present only when the step has it. The words are the step's own; the only word added is "why".
- The restyle is scoped to the method: the table's estimate flag (`.target-chip.ingredient-table__flag`) keeps its box, and the open step's chips of fields (section 2) are unchanged. Print (`PrintSheetPages.jsx`, `print.css` `.mp-*`) stays as built; this section is the screen's reading of the same grammar through the same tokens.
- The build's surfaces this names: the reading page's steps and the version pen's closed steps (`Method.jsx`), and the creation editor's Preview step. Show changes' struck values inside a chip are not drawn here (README, For the plan, 8).
