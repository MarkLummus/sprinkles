## Confirmed items: steps, severity, likely cause

**Item 1. Start from a narrowed New recipe entry strands a draft.**
1. Open the Notebook in a window 724 or wider, with no draft kept.
2. Press New recipe.
3. Narrow the window below 724 (iPad Split View, or drag a desktop window).
   Expect: the entry stays open; New recipe is gone.
4. Type a name.
5. Press Start.
   Expect: the page reads "New recipe / Done at the desk or on the iPad." with no control.
6. Open the Notebook at that width.
   Expect: a "draft" row with no Resume.

- Severity: minor. It needs a mid-entry narrowing and nothing is lost, but the maker is told to go elsewhere right after pressing Start.
- Likely cause, as I read it: the entry form renders on `!draft && open` with no width test (`NotebookPlace.jsx:236`), `handleStart` navigates regardless (`:144-147`), and DeskOnly switches on width alone (`DeskOnly.jsx:13-15`).

**Item 2. Cancel on that entry drops focus.**
1. Follow steps 1 to 3 of item 1.
2. Press Cancel.
   Expect: the entry closes and nothing holds focus.

- Severity: cosmetic.
- Likely cause, as I read it: the focus return targets `newRef` (`NotebookPlace.jsx:195-197`), which is not drawn below 724 (`:230`).

**Item 3. Picker Cancel goes to the heading after the opener row moved.**
1. In the creation editor at 724 or wider, have one step and an ingredient in no step.
2. Press that row's Add ingredient.
3. Press the same row's Add to step.
4. Choose the step.
5. Press the picker's Cancel.
   Expect: focus lands on the heading's Add ingredient, not the row's.

- Severity: cosmetic. Focus is not lost and lands on a like control.
- Likely cause, as I read it: Cancel builds the line from the stored anchor (`CreationPage.jsx:449-455`) and the lookup falls to the heading when that line is gone (`:334-342`). The comment at `:335-337` describes this fallback, so Mark may call it intended.

**Item 4 and item 9. Already on record.**
1. Keep a Next version draft on a recipe's latest version.
2. Open Home below 724.
3. Tap Go to draft or Record batch.
   Expect: the version page, no pen, one line under the version name saying the draft is finished at the desk.
4. For Record tasting, the batch awaiting its tasting must be on the version holding the draft.
   Expect: the batch page, no pen, the same line.

- Severity: minor, as the UI review's P1 already ranks it. Nothing is lost.
- Correction to "nothing says why": the band line is on screen at arrival (271 px down). What is missing is a notice or any word tying it to the press.

**Item 8. Already on record (IN-01).**
1. Open the creation editor at 724 or wider.
2. Open the picker, a step's Preview and an empty Purpose.
3. Narrow below 724.
4. Widen again.
   Expect: the editor returns with the draft intact, "Draft kept." showing, and the picker, preview and Purpose closed.

- Severity: minor.
- Likely cause, as I read it: `DeskOnly.jsx:13-15`.

**Item 15. Stale held line, two tabs only.**
- Severity: cosmetic. Suggest the audit note, not a row.

**Item 17. Focus lost after widening with a band-opened pen.**
1. Below 724, press the band's Record tasting or Record batch.
2. Widen to 724 or more.
3. Press Cancel.
   Expect: nothing holds focus.

- Severity: cosmetic. Pre-existing, and the same family as the open decide row.
- Likely cause, as I read it: the return targets `recordActButtonRef` (`VersionRow.jsx:142-150`), not drawn from 724 up (`:477`).

## Inventory corrections

- **A14, creation focus request:** a request written while the save lock is held is not dropped. It waits in the ref until the next render and is acted on then. In item 6 the lead-in request fired when the picker opened and took focus from the picker's Name.
- **B, "Open: is focusVersionOnMount ever cleared?":** no. After a save's landing `history.state.usr` still holds `{focusVersion: true}`; only `saved` is consumed. A reload of that entry lands focus on the version heading again (seen at 393, 723 and 1366).
- **B, the band's draft line:** add that it is the held page's only word. It is visible at arrival (271 px down at 393), with no notice and no focus move.
- **E, Picker ("once-per-entry enforced only in the picker"):** add that `handlePick` has no lock (`CreationPage.jsx:424-434`). Two activations in one JS tick add two rows; real double clicks, taps and Enter do not, because the picker unmounts between events.
- **F, Plan held:** the claim list's item 4 says the page "draws no record act, no Correct, no Record another". True, but the log's Hide fold stays; say "no opener" rather than "no control".
- **Claim list, item 26:** `CreationStructure.test.jsx:257` is the Move button test, not a keyed move.
- **Claim list, item 7 and 12-REVIEW IN-03:** both describe states that do not show (see the table).
