## Problem

Mark (2026-09-24): "I want to do a product brief specifically for phone-based jobs." The
briefs give the phone no job of its own. The recipe brief says the phone reads and
transcribes and does not formulate in milestone 1. The batch brief records batches only at
the desk. The 03.5 canvas's 393 board, checked on Mark's iPhone on 2026-09-24, raised
whether the batch and tasting log is entered on the phone at the machine. That question is
deferred out of 03.5 to this brief.

## Solution

Write the brief before any phone-specific design or build. First input: the
phone-logging todo (`2026-09-24-decide-whether-the-batch-and-tasting-log-is-entered-on-the-phone.md`)
and the two options it lays out. Open for Mark when it is picked up: which jobs the brief
covers, and where the brief lives and what writes it (a page in `product-requirements/` or
an `/impeccable shape` surface brief). Until then, 03.5 designs no phone-specific job, and
the 393 rung follows the briefs as they stand.
